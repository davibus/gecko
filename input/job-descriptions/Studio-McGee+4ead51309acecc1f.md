## Overview

The manager drives results across paid digital channels, affiliate programs, and direct mail. The role leads strategy and oversight of paid media and SEO, guides an external paid media agency, manages channel investments, and improves search performance. It owns the affiliate program other than ShopMy and LTK, maintains visibility into catalog and retargeting direct mail, and builds processes for cross-channel growth.

## Paid media strategy and performance oversight

- Lead performance marketing across search, social, display, programmatic, and other channels with measurable outcomes.
- Direct the external agency's planning, execution, and optimization for audience growth and funnel performance.
- Evaluate channel structures for clarity, scale, and performance.
- Provide creative requirements and performance insights to Brand and Creative.
- Monitor trends and competitors for growth and efficiency opportunities.
- Plan campaigns with website and lifecycle/retention teams to integrate channels and brand storytelling.

## Affiliate program management

- Own affiliate recruitment, activation, optimization, benchmarks, and partner relationships, excluding ShopMy and LTK.
- Track affiliate spend and incremental revenue within the broader paid media strategy.

## Catalog and retargeting direct mail

- Coordinate visibility and audience strategy across catalog and retargeting direct mail programs.
- Align paid digital, affiliate, and direct mail investment and targeting.

## Search strategy and SEM stewardship

- Guide keyword research and paid/organic search opportunities.
- Build SEO briefs with Brand and Creative and direct agency SEM execution.
- Review bids, ad copy, extensions, and competitors; reduce overlap between paid and organic search.

## Budgets, analytics, and testing

- Partner with finance on budgets, spend forecasts, pacing, and allocation across paid digital, affiliate, and direct mail.
- Guide agency audience, bidding, and testing decisions.
- Analyze CPA, ROAS, LTV, conversion rates, and pipeline contribution.
- Run structured multivariate tests of creative, audiences, placements, and messaging.
- Build reporting with GA4, Looker, Tableau, and attribution tools, and turn insights into investment recommendations.

## Collaboration and tools

- Work with Web Operations, Lifecycle and Retention, Marketing, Product, Social, Brand, and Creative.
- Follow data privacy and advertising rules.
- Strong command of Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Shop, and programmatic DSPs.
- Familiarity with Google Search Console, SEMRush, BrightEdge, affiliate platforms/tracking, GA4, Looker or Tableau, and campaign workflow tools.

## Qualifications and working style

- Four to six years in paid media, SEM, and/or SEO with Google Ads, paid social, and SEO optimization expertise.
- Multi-channel budget management, including affiliates, and proven ROI/conversion improvement.
- Collaborative, experimental, analytical, accountable approach that connects daily inputs to broader growth.
