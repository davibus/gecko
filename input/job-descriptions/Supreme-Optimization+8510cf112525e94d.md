Scientific Digital Marketing Strategist (Remote) Are you a PhD level scientist that wants to learn more about business and digital marketing while working remotely? This role is designed specifically for scientists who want to apply their analytical thinking, technical expertise, and intellectual curiosity to digital marketing strategy for life science companies. No prior digital marketing experience is required — we provide full training. Company Mission: Supreme Optimization’s mission is to become the #1 global provider of digital marketing services and resources for life science and pharma companies. We partner with leading biotech, pharmaceutical, diagnostics, and health-tech organizations to help them reach the right audiences through high-quality, data-driven digital marketing. We are a scientist-led team that values rigor, curiosity, collaboration, and continuous learning. Role Title: DigitalStrategist - SEO/GEO Mission of the role: To develop and lead data-driven SEO and website strategy initiatives for life science and pharma clients, improving search visibility, strengthening website performance, and driving meaningful business outcomes while serving as a trusted strategic partner. What You’ll Do Strategy and Execution Develop SEO and website strategies for life science and pharmaceutical clients across a variety of engagement types. Conduct research and analysis to identify growth opportunities aligned with business objectives. Evaluate technical website performance and provide data-driven recommendations to improve search visibility and user experience. Translate complex scientific offerings into clear digital strategies aligned with user intent. Partner closely with content, design, development, and paid media teams to ensure alignment across digital initiatives. Present insights and strategic recommendations directly to clients. Analyze performance data to measure impact and identify opportunities. Prepare data-driven reports and contribute to client-facing presentations. Collaborate with content, design, development, and paid media teams to align SEO with broader digital strategy.
