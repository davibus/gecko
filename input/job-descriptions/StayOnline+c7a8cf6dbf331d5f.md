## Job description

StayOnline is seeking a results-driven and analytical Digital Marketing Specialist to join our marketing team. This is a hands-on role ideal for someone who enjoys building campaigns, digging into data, and finding new ways to reach customers online. The Digital Marketing Specialist is responsible for managing our Google Ads account, running paid social campaigns, and improving our organic search presence. This role requires strong analytical skills, creativity, and a commitment to driving measurable results.
