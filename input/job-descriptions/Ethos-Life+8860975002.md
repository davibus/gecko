## Job description

About Ethos Ethos is a leading life insurance technology company on a mission to protect families by democratizing access to life insurance and empowering agents at scale. With its robust three-sided technology platform, Ethos is transforming the life insurance experience for consumers, agents, and carriers alike. Ethos offers instant, accessible products and a seamless online process that requires no medical exams and just a few health questions; it eliminates traditional barriers, making it easier than ever for everyone to protect their families. Ethos is redefining how life insurance is bought, sold, and underwritten. About the role Ethos is hiring a Director, Marketing Process & Technology to join our Compliance department as a senior leader. This person will own marketing compliance review at Ethos end to end: the process, the technology, and the majority of the reviews themselves. You will be both a hands-on senior reviewer and the owner of the system that performs the review, and you will be charged with a clear mandate: move marketing review toward automation through AI as far as it can defensibly go, without loosening the standard. You will serve as business owner of the AI tool Ethos uses for marketing review, and you will be measured, in part, on how much of the review volume that tool can absorb and how reliably it performs. At the same time, you will personally handle the judgment calls, escalations, and novel questions that automation should not decide. Beyond the tool, you own the marketing review process itself: intake, prioritization, review standards, approved language, recordkeeping, and the working relationship with Marketing/Brand/business teams. This is a player-coach role. You will carry a real review load from day one and will eventually hire and oversee a compliance analyst to support you, which means building the standards, playbooks, and training that make the work delegable. You will be expected to drive thought leadership on marketing compliance matters across Ethos. Importantly, the candidate must be risk-minded, be a team player, confident in determining acceptable risk, think creatively to always find solutions, and be technology forward.
