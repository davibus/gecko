## Company overview

Association Member Benefits Advisors has provided insurance and membership services for retired public servants and other association groups since 1981. The company reports serving a network of 44 million members through more than 450 associations across all 50 states.

## Role overview

The Senior Paid Search Strategist owns the strategy, performance, and fulfillment of AMBA's PPC program across Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising. The role combines hands-on campaign management, analysis, reporting, budget stewardship, creative and landing-page collaboration, cross-functional partnership, and peer support.

## Responsibilities

- Design comprehensive paid-search strategies across Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising, including account structure, keyword strategy, and bid management.
- Monitor CTR, CPC, CPA, ROAS, Quality Score, and related indicators; use the findings to improve ROI.
- Partner with creative teams, designers, external vendors, and peers on ad copy, extensions, and landing-page assets.
- Manage paid-search budgets and bid strategies for efficient allocation and cost-effective results.
- Execute keyword research, negative-keyword management, ad-copy testing, and landing-page alignment while maintaining timely and compliant delivery.
- Work with sales, product, and analytics teams to align messaging, goals, and initiatives.
- Monitor industry trends, competitors, automated bidding, and AI-driven PPC technologies for growth opportunities.
- Prepare regular reports with actionable insights and optimization recommendations.
- Mentor and collaborate with peers to strengthen team performance.

## Requirements

- Bachelor's degree in marketing, business administration, or a related field.
- Five or more years of digital marketing experience focused on paid search and PPC.
- Proficiency with Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising, Google Analytics, and Google Tag Manager.
- Strong understanding of keyword match types, bidding, Quality Score, Ad Rank, and the relationship between SEM, SEO, and other channels.
- Strong communication and project-management skills.
- Ability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
- Data-driven decision-making and the ability to translate analytics into performance improvements.
- Familiarity with A/B testing and conversion-rate optimization.
- Google Ads certification is required; Microsoft Advertising certification is preferred.
