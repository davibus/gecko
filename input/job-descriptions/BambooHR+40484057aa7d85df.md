## Job description

Marketing Manager About the Role We’re looking for a Marketing Manager to lead the strategy, execution, and optimization of marketing initiatives that drive awareness, engagement, and growth. In this role, you’ll manage multi-channel campaigns, partner closely with cross-functional teams, and ensure our brand is consistently represented across all customer touchpoints. Responsibilities Develop and execute marketing strategies that support company goals. Plan and manage digital, email, social, and content marketing campaigns. Track performance metrics and report insights to leadership. Conduct market and competitive research to inform positioning and opportunities. Support demand-generation efforts to drive qualified leads and pipeline growth. Collaborate with Product, Sales, and Customer Success teams on messaging, launches, and content needs. Ensure consistent brand voice and quality across all marketing materials. Manage marketing tools, budgets, and timelines. Requirements Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or related field. 3+ years of marketing experience (B2B SaaS experience a plus). Strong understanding of digital marketing channels and analytics tools. Excellent communication, project management, and organizational skills. Ability to use data to measure performance and guide decisions. • • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
