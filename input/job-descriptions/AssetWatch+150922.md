## Job description

AssetWatch serves global manufacturers by powering manufacturing uptime through the delivery of an unparalleled condition monitoring experience, with a passion to care about the assets our customers care for every day. We are a devoted and capable team that includes world-renowned engineers and distinguished business leaders united by a common goal – To build the future of predictive maintenance. As we enter the next phase of rapid growth, we are seeking people to help lead the journey. We're looking for a B2B Integrated Campaign Manager who wakes up thinking about pipeline. In this role, you'll own the strategy and hands-on execution of full-funnel, multi-channel campaigns that drive real revenue; building in HubSpot, pulling the levers, and iterating relentlessly until the numbers move. This is a fast-scaling SaaS environment where every campaign decision is made through one lens: what gets more qualified opportunities into the funnel, faster. This isn't your typical enterprise SaaS role. AssetWatch sells into the manufacturing plant floor - maintenance managers, reliability engineers, plant managers, and operations leaders who aren't scrolling LinkedIn between meetings. Reaching them requires a dual playbook: account-based tactics for executive buyers, and channel-agnostic creative for the plant-level practitioners who champion the technology. This role blends campaign strategy, cross-functional leadership, and technical campaign execution, and it demands someone who plans obsessively and operates with precision. You'll be managing multiple campaigns and stakeholders simultaneously, and the throughline that keeps it all moving is your ability to build the system, run the system, and hold it accountable to pipeline.
