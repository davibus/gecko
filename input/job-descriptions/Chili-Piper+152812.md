## Job description

About Chili Piper Chili Piper is a B2B SaaS company that helps GTM teams turn inbound leads into qualified meetings - instantly. Our Demand Conversion Platform makes it easier for buyers and sellers to connect fast, without the usual friction in the funnel. We’re a fully remote, global team that loves building solutions that truly help our customers, moving fast, and having fun while creating something meaningful. We’re growing quickly and aiming big! ***CURRENTLY ONLY HIRING FOR THIS ROLE IN CANADA AND THE US*** About the role At Chili Piper, community and events are at the heart of our GTM strategy. From intimate dinners to large-scale activations like our annual ChiliPalooza, we bring top GTM leaders together to connect, exchange ideas, and build lasting relationships. It's how we turn a room full of people into real pipeline, and how we've built a community that actually wants to hear from us. We're hiring an Events Manager to scale these experiences and deepen our relationships with the GTM community: executive dinners, industry conferences, sponsorships, speaking moments, webinars, and ChiliPalooza. This is the person who turns our presence in a room, physical or virtual, into brand awareness, pipeline, and lasting relationships. This role is part of our marketing team and works cross-functionally with Sales and Partnerships to run Chili Piper's global event strategy. You'll have real ownership of the calendar and where we show up, not just the execution. You'll shape the annual plan, then make it happen: logistics, promotion, on-site, and follow-up, all the way through to measurable impact. This is a hands-on role. You're the one building the landing pages, pulling the CRM lists, and running the show on-site, not handing a brief to an agency and waiting. It's a strong fit for someone who is organized and proactive, thinks creatively about how we show up, and brings original ideas and a sense of humor to every event. It's fully remote, with travel to events as needed. What you'll do Own the annual event calendar. Logistics, budgets, venues, vendors, shipments, swag, and on-site coordination, done by you and tracked by you Run event promotion from start to finish. With Marketing on branding, design, and promotion; with Sales to identify and invite the right prospects and customers; with Partnerships to coordinate sponsors and activations Ensure smooth on-site execution. Keep schedules on track and deliver a seamless experience from load-in to last handshake Build and nurture community. Deepen our relationships with GTM leaders, partners, and community members so our events compound over time Track performance. Collect attendee data, measure engagement, and report on outcomes and pipeline impact Amplify results. Work with Marketing to repurpose event content, including photos, videos, and testimonials, for social and post-event communication Manage vendors and budgets.
