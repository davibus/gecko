## Company and role overview

CHG Healthcare created the locum tenens staffing model and connects physicians and advanced-practice providers with healthcare opportunities. The Growth Marketing Manager joins the Marketing and Customer Products team and reports to the Senior Marketing Director. The role owns integrated campaigns end-to-end, finds full-funnel growth opportunities, coordinates channel teams, and connects marketing activity to measurable business results.

## Responsibilities

### Campaign development and management

- Propose, develop, and lead integrated campaigns from concept through completion, including promotions, referral programs, provider-engagement initiatives, and seasonal or market-driven opportunities.
- Translate business priorities into briefs defining audience, channel mix, messaging, KPIs, and timeline.
- Coordinate creative, paid-media, email, and web teams and keep multiple workstreams aligned and on schedule.
- Manage concurrent campaigns across specialties, programs, and audience segments.
- Evaluate post-launch performance, document learning, and apply insights to future campaigns.

### Cross-functional orchestration

- Connect paid media, lifecycle/email, web, and creative teams around integrated strategy, coordinated execution, and shared funnel outcomes.
- Partner with paid media on targeting and investment mix, email on nurturing and segmentation, web on CRO and A/B testing, and creative on briefs and messaging.

### Growth strategy and experimentation

- Develop annual plans spanning awareness, conversion, and retention.
- Maintain a prioritized backlog of tests and initiatives.
- Design structured tests across channels and funnel stages and document hypotheses, results, and learning.
- Identify conversion friction and shift the channel mix toward stronger-performing approaches.

### Performance measurement and reporting

- Own the performance narrative across CPA, conversion rate, cost per lead, ROAS, CAC, and pipeline contribution.
- Connect full-funnel KPIs to business outcomes.
- Partner with sales divisions to monitor lead quality and marketing-to-sales pull-through.

## Qualifications

- Strong data fluency and the ability to turn dashboards and analytics into strategic recommendations.
- Experience managing integrated multi-channel campaigns from brief through post-campaign analysis.
- Full-funnel knowledge of paid media, email, CRO, and SEO.
- Ability to influence and align teams without direct authority.
- Strong written and verbal communication.
- Understanding of CPA, CAC, LTV, ROAS, and conversion-rate optimization.
- Working proficiency with AI tools for daily work and decision support.
- Bachelor's degree in marketing, business, analytics, or a related field.
- Six to eight years in growth, integrated, or performance marketing.
- Cross-functional experience with paid media, web, email, creative, and analytics teams.

## Preferred qualifications

- Healthcare, SaaS, or ecommerce background.
- Adobe Analytics or Google Analytics experience.
- SQL or marketing-data-warehouse familiarity.
- Experience building or managing a structured experimentation program.
