A day in the life: The ideal candidate will have a strong understanding of modern measurement, incrementality, statistics, data analysis, and reporting, and the ability to communicate effectively with clients. In this role, you will be responsible for managing and recommending the best modern measurement strategies for clients’ marketing campaigns, including Statistical Modeling, experiment design for incremental impact, customer analysis, and attribution-based measurement. You’ll also be providing strategic guidance to clients and internal teams, managing the relationship for your book of business, and maintaining a high sentiment of Power Digital measurement clients. Responsibilities: Manage overall measurement strategy for a book of clients, including statistical modeling (MMM), incrementality experiment design (MMT), and customer analysis. Utilize large amounts of data to identify meaningful performance insights or opportunities for improvement using R, SQL, and Python. Work closely with account teams and clients to develop and maintain long-term strategic relationships, ensuring their satisfaction and success with Power Digital’s services, especially Measurement. Clearly and succinctly present and explain advanced measurement methodologies and models to internal and external teams. Ensure that insights are actionable and tied to client business goals. Provide strategic advice and "so what" insights for ongoing measurement optimization. Mentor and develop Measurement Analysts by providing ongoing performance coaching and guidance on advanced analytics, problem-solving, and insight storytelling to elevate analytical rigor, quality of deliverables, and overall client impact. Consistently drive efficiencies within the current measurement and marketing science processes Employ AI technologies to increase efficiency and support the optimization of deliverables and/or processes Utilize and leverage Power Digital's Nova ecosystem as it relates to your department Role Requirements: Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business Administration, or a related field. 6+ years of experience in Marketing Analytics and Measurement. 3+ years of agency experience. Strong business acumen, with the ability to translate quantitative data into actionable business insights and recommendations, especially related to applying outputs of statistical models and market experiments to marketing strategies. Proficiency in R, Python, and SQL. Experience with ETL tools, such as Funnel.io and Fivetran. Strong understanding of full-funnel marketing strategies. Extensive knowledge of owned, earned and paid marketing channels as well as first-party data tools.
