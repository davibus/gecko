## Job description

Digital Marketing Specialist Micron America | Part-Time | Remote Pay: hourly starting at $20 depending on experience Micron America is looking for an experienced Digital Marketing Specialist to manage our social media presence, improve online visibility, and create advertising campaigns that drive traffic, leads, and sales. We’re seeking someone who can work independently and has hands-on experience with SEO, WordPress, social media management, and e-commerce marketplace advertising. Responsibilities Create, schedule, and publish engaging social media content for Instagram, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn. Manage social media accounts, monitor comments and messages, and maintain a consistent brand voice. Set up, manage, and optimize advertising campaigns on Amazon, eBay, Walmart Marketplace, and social media platforms. Improve website and product listing visibility through keyword research, SEO, and content optimization. Update WordPress website content and create or improve landing pages to support marketing campaigns. Develop a content calendar and recommend promotions and campaign ideas. Monitor advertising budgets and campaign performance, including traffic, conversions, cost per click, and return on ad spend. Provide clear reports on results and recommend improvements. Qualifications At least 3 years of relevant digital marketing experience, with examples of measurable results. Hands-on experience managing advertising campaigns on Amazon, eBay, and Walmart Marketplace. Experience managing business accounts and creating content for Instagram, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn. Strong understanding of SEO, keyword research, and website optimization. Experience working with WordPress. Strong writing, editing, and basic graphic design skills. Ability to use analytics and advertising reports to improve campaign performance. Strong organization, communication, and time management skills. Ability to work independently in a remote, part-time role. Preferred Experience Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and advertising conversion tracking. Canva, Adobe Creative Suite, or similar content creation tools. E-commerce product listing optimization and marketing physical products. How to Apply Please submit your resume and a brief summary of your relevant experience. Include examples of social media accounts, websites, or advertising campaigns you have managed, along with measurable results where available. Please specify which advertising platforms you have worked with and your availability for part-time work.
