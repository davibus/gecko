About the role We're looking for a strategic, hands-on Growth Marketing Manager to drive core business performance and scale Thumbtack's rapidly growing Paid Social program across channels like Meta, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, and Reddit. You're a builder at heart: someone who owns their numbers, pushes ideas outside the current playbook, and has taken a channel or audience from 0 to 1 and made it stick, while collaborating with Creative, Analytics, Martech, and driving performance forward with AI and automation. If you love uncovering the next lever before anyone else sees it, this role is built for you.
