### Responsibilities

- Build customer segmentation, profiles, lifecycle insights, and Voice of the Customer capabilities from behavioral, transactional, demographic, geographic, attitudinal, and qualitative data.
- Partner with marketing teams to improve acquisition, purchase frequency, retention, cross-category shopping, lifetime value, and loyalty.
- Establish marketing measurement focused on incremental store traffic and revenue, new customers, lifetime value, retention, market share, and brand preference.
- Advance attribution and incrementality through media mix modeling, geographic testing, holdouts, store visitation, and campaign effectiveness analysis.
- Partner with Finance on marketing investment, productivity, and return.
- Own brand health and consumer insights, including awareness, preference, loyalty, and competitive benchmarks.
- Analyze store trade areas, customer density, competition, category demand, and media investment to identify local market growth opportunities.
- Build predictive customer and demand capabilities and partner with IT, eCommerce, Finance, and vendors on CRM/CDP, BI, analytics, and data quality.
- Create executive dashboards and translate complex analyses into practical recommendations.

### Requirements

- 8+ years of progressive marketing analytics, customer analytics, consumer insights, business intelligence, marketing science, or related experience; 3+ years leading analytical teams.
- Retail, consumer brand, omnichannel commerce, or other customer-driven business experience preferred.
- Customer segmentation, lifecycle analytics, lifetime value, large customer/transactional datasets, brand tracking, consumer research, and customer insights.
- Attribution, experimentation, incrementality testing, and ideally media mix modeling; understanding of store traffic, retail performance, and local markets.
- Working knowledge of CRM, CDP, BI, analytics, and marketing technology platforms; experience with analytical partners and research vendors.
- Financial and commercial judgment, executive presentations, and clear business recommendations.
