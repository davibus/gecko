About the Role Motive is hiring a Senior Customer Marketing Specialist to own and execute customer engagement and advocacy programs that drive retention, expansion, and brand credibility. In this role, you will build, scale, and nurture a thriving online customer community while managing incoming advocacy initiatives. This is a hands-on, individual contributor role for someone who enjoys building programs from the ground up and running them end-to-end. You’ll join a lean, high-impact team responsible for customer storytelling (case studies, video testimonials, speaking engagements), online reviews, community engagement, reference management and advocacy programs that amplify the voice of the customer and support business growth. Experience working in industries with physical operations—such as transportation and logistics, field service, construction, or similar environments—is a strong plus. In this role, you’ll use AI, automation, and modern customer marketing tools to make advocacy more scalable and efficient, while ensuring customers have a positive experience and programs deliver measurable business impact.
