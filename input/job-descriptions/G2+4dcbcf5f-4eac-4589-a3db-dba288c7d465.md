About The Role G2 is seeking a hands on Marketing Operations Manager to own and scale the systems behind our marketing to sales pipeline. This role owns critical revenue operations infrastructure, including lead lifecycle architecture, MQA routing and disposition, contact and account enrichment, and the scoring and prioritization frameworks that directly shape pipeline quality and sales efficiency. This role connects Marketing, Sales, and Business Systems, acting as the operational owner responsible for clean lead flow, accurate data, fast speed to lead, and a GTM stack that supports business goals. The ideal candidate pairs deep technical fluency across our MarTech stack (Salesforce, Clay, Marketo, Qualified, Ringlead) with strong analytical judgment and the ability to move fast and stay precise in a B2B SaaS environment.
