Overview NextPlay Recruitment is hiring a Digital Marketing and Social Media Specialist for our internal team. In this full-time remote position, you own how NextPlay shows up online: our social channels, our email outreach, our website content, and the campaigns that bring employers and job seekers to us. This is a role for someone who has run marketing for a business before and can set the plan as well as carry it out. You decide what we publish, measure what works, and keep improving the results. What You'll Do Build and run the social media plan across LinkedIn and other channels, from content calendar to published post Write and design posts, graphics, and short videos that promote open roles and our recruiting services Plan and send email campaigns to employers and candidates Keep website pages and content current, clear, and easy to find in search Set up and manage paid campaigns on social and job platforms Track reach, engagement, leads, and applications, and report on what the numbers show Respond to comments and messages and keep the brand voice consistent everywhere What We're Looking For Experience running digital marketing or social media for a business or brand Strong writing with a clean, professional voice Working knowledge of LinkedIn company pages, email marketing tools, and design tools such as Canva Comfort reading campaign results and acting on them The organization to manage several channels and deadlines at once A quiet home workspace and reliable high-speed internet Authorization to work in the United States What's Offered A full-time, fully remote position on the NextPlay Recruitment team Ownership of the voice and look of a growing brand Direct collaboration with company leadership NextPlay Recruitment provides reasonable accommodations for qualified applicants with disabilities throughout the application and interview process.
