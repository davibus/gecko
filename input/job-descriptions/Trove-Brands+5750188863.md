## Full job description

Trove Brands is a privately held house of brands including BlenderBottle®, Owala®, Whiskware®, and Eco-Brite®. Its products are designed to improve everyday life through innovation, quality, and style. The company describes its environment as fast-paced, entrepreneurial, collaborative, and focused on global growth.

Trove Brands is seeking a strategic, performance-driven Senior Digital Marketing Manager to lead growth of Owala's direct-to-consumer business. The role owns acquisition, retention, and website performance strategies with accountability for measurable revenue and scalable long-term growth. Its digital roadmap spans paid media, SEO/GEO, email/SMS, website performance, and conversion optimization. The manager will provide strategic leadership, performance oversight, and guidance to a growing digital marketing team.

### Key responsibilities

- Own Owala's overall DTC digital marketing strategy, with accountability for revenue growth and channel performance.
- Develop forecasts, allocate digital budgets, and set performance targets across acquisition and retention channels.
- Identify scalable growth opportunities and drive innovation across the digital ecosystem.
- Manage and guide the teams responsible for website growth and performance, paid media, conversion rate optimization, email/SMS/lifecycle marketing, and SEO/GEO/organic performance.
- Align channels across the full funnel to improve acquisition efficiency, retention, and lifetime value.
- Lead experimentation and testing to improve conversion, creative performance, and customer engagement.
- Monitor attribution models and performance dashboards to refine strategy.
- Directly manage and mentor a team of four or more digital marketing professionals.
- Own digital marketing budgets and ensure efficient allocation and measurable ROI.
- Establish KPIs, reporting cadence, and channel performance standards.
- Partner with creative, brand, retail, and ecommerce teams to align digital strategy with business objectives.
- Present insights, forecasts, and recommendations to senior leadership.
- Move digital marketing from tactical execution toward scalable long-term strategy.

### Required qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in marketing, business, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
- Six to nine years of digital marketing experience, specifically with DTC or ecommerce brands.
- Experience owning DTC digital marketing strategy with measurable revenue impact.
- Proven ability to manage website growth, paid media, CRO, email/SMS/lifecycle marketing, and SEO/GEO/organic growth.
- Experience managing and developing at least three direct reports.
- Experience owning and optimizing significant digital marketing budgets.
- Strong proficiency with analytics and reporting tools such as Google Analytics, Meta Ads Manager, and Klaviyo.
- Deep understanding of acquisition funnels, retention strategy, and performance forecasting.
- Strong analytical, strategic, leadership, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills.

The role is fully in office at Trove Brands' Lehi, Utah headquarters.
