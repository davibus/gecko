## About Vitality Medical

Vitality Medical is a well-established and growing ecommerce company that has served customers for more than 20 years. It has been recognized multiple times as an Inc. 5000 Fastest-Growing Company and offers thousands of medical equipment and supply products, from everyday essentials to life-saving equipment.

## Role Summary

Vitality Medical is seeking an experienced E-Commerce SEO Manager to own and execute the organic and AI-search strategy for VitalityMedical.com. The role develops strategy collaboratively with catalog, technical, content, merchandising, marketing, and leadership teams, then personally carries work through implementation, testing, measurement, and maintenance. It is explicitly an execution-first position requiring flexibility between long-term planning and detailed work.

Direct ecommerce SEO experience is required. Working knowledge of Magento is a strong plus.

## Responsibilities

### Own and execute SEO strategy

- Own organic-search and AI-search strategy and maintain a prioritized roadmap based on customer value, revenue potential, impact, risk, and effort.
- Take initiatives from research and diagnosis through implementation, testing, measurement, and maintenance.
- Personally execute improvements when access and skills allow.
- Establish goals, benchmarks, and KPIs; report priorities, risks, results, and next steps to leadership.
- Adapt priorities to performance, business objectives, algorithm changes, competitor activity, technical issues, and customer behavior.

### Catalog collaboration

- Improve product data, categories, manufacturer pages, product relationships, attributes, navigation, and discoverability.
- Establish SEO standards and repeatable processes for adding, updating, organizing, and discontinuing products.
- Improve product/category names, descriptions, metadata, images, attributes, and internal links.
- Determine when pages should be created, optimized, consolidated, redirected, canonicalized, or removed.
- Work directly with catalog staff, review changes for quality, and create scalable templates and documentation.

### Technical SEO

- Partner with developers to improve crawling, indexing, architecture, page speed, Core Web Vitals, canonicalization, redirects, structured data, XML sitemaps, and robots directives.
- Address filters, faceted navigation, pagination, product variants, duplicate content, and complex URLs.
- Define technical requirements, support implementation, test deployments, monitor releases, and remain accountable for SEO outcomes.

### Hands-on maintenance and ecommerce SEO

- Audit and correct broken links, 404s, redirect chains, duplicate content, canonical tags, indexing problems, metadata, headings, images, schema, and internal links.
- Find orphaned, underlinked, outdated, competing, and underperforming pages.
- Diagnose changes in traffic, rankings, indexing, conversions, and revenue.
- Conduct keyword, competitor, content-gap, search-intent, and customer-question research.
- Optimize product, category, manufacturer, brand, and informational pages.
- Scale SEO across thousands of catalog pages while preventing cannibalization and unnecessary duplication.
- Connect organic work to qualified traffic, conversions, revenue, customer value, and the full buying journey.

### Content, authority, and AI search

- Develop content supporting product research and purchase, including category pages, medical conditions, comparisons, buying guides, FAQs, and customer questions.
- Create or edit content and actionable briefs; establish accuracy, sourcing, citation, and review standards for medical information.
- Own backlink, external-linking, outreach, digital PR, and off-site authority strategy with manufacturers, suppliers, associations, publishers, and partners.
- Monitor new, lost, broken, low-quality, or harmful links; reclaim links and unlinked mentions while following search-engine guidelines.
- Improve visibility in Google AI Overviews, AI assistants, answer engines, and emerging discovery platforms through content structure, entity signals, structured data, citations, and authority.
- Use AI tools for research, content analysis, briefs, internal linking, reporting, workflows, and automation while enforcing quality and medical-content review standards.

### Mentoring, measurement, and reporting

- Mentor catalog, content, technical, merchandising, and marketing staff through hands-on work.
- Create checklists, templates, training materials, and quality-control processes.
- Track organic traffic, rankings, conversions, revenue, backlinks, AI visibility, and other KPIs.
- Build reporting that connects SEO activity to business outcomes and explain results to technical and nontechnical stakeholders.
