About The Role Solink is looking for a Field & Channel Marketing Manager to own the event calendar and field marketing strategy across a specific region or channel. Rather than running one event at a time, you’ll design the playbook for how events, dinners, and tradeshows are planned, executed, and measured, and you’ll build focused go-to-market programs around Solink’s strategic verticals and accounts, including Restaurant, Financial Institution, and Strategic segments, to grow enterprise pipeline and deepen account penetration. You’ll evaluate ROI across the full portfolio of events and reallocate budget and effort toward what performs, manage a portfolio of vendor and partner relationships (including performance discussions), and act as the escalation point when vendor, partner, or stakeholder issues can’t be resolved at the individual-contributor level. You’ll also improve the systems, tools, and templates the broader field marketing team plans against. This role suits a senior marketer who thinks in terms of portfolios and systems rather than single events, coaches others on collaborating effectively with sales and partner teams, and instills urgency and accountability across multiple concurrent events and stakeholders. What You’ll Be Doing Strategic Vertical & Account Programs Build and execute focused GTM event programs around Solink’s strategic verticals and accounts to grow enterprise pipeline and penetration. Partner with Sales and Account Executives to align the event portfolio and invite strategy to target accounts and verticals. Evaluate which event formats and investments perform best within each vertical, and reallocate budget accordingly. Event Calendar, Strategy & Portfolio ROI Own the end-to-end event calendar and strategy across a region or channel, not just individual events. Design the playbook for how events, dinners, and tradeshows are planned, executed, and measured. Ensure pre-show, on-site, and follow-up processes are consistent across multiple simultaneous events. Evaluate ROI across the portfolio of events and reallocate budget/effort toward what performs. Improve systems and tools (tech stack, automation, templates) to increase team efficiency. Vendor & Partner Portfolio Management Manage a portfolio of vendor and partner relationships across events and channels, including performance discussions. Align AEs, CAMs, and partners across multiple events on shared priorities and expectations. Build standardized stakeholder processes (partner briefing templates, AE requirement docs) to reduce friction.
