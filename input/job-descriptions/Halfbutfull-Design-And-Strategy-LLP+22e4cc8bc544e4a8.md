About Us: Halfbutfull is a dynamic design and marketing company specializing in driving performance marketing for D2C brands. We are passionate about delivering exceptional results for our clients and are looking for a Performance Marketing Executive to join our growing team. Job Description: We are seeking a talented and experienced Performance Marketing Executive with a strong background in managing ads for e-commerce brands on Meta (Facebook and Instagram). The ideal candidate will have a minimum of 1-2 years of experience and a proven track record of driving successful ad campaigns for D2C brands . Responsibilities: Develop and implement effective performance marketing strategies for our D2C clients. Manage and optimize Meta ad campaigns (Facebook and Instagram) to achieve desired ROAS. Monitor and analyze campaign performance on a daily basis, ensuring KPIs are met. Collaborate with the creative team to develop compelling ad creatives that resonate with target audiences. Provide regular performance reports and insights to clients, recommending optimizations and adjustments as needed. Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and best practices in Meta advertising and performance marketing. Troubleshoot and resolve any issues related to ad performance and delivery. Ensure all campaigns are executed within budget and meet the client’s objectives. Requirements: Minimum 1-2 years of experience in performance marketing, specifically with Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram). Proven experience working with e-commerce brands; experience with D2C brands is non-negotiable. In-depth knowledge of Meta Ads Manager and familiarity with all relevant terminologies and tools. Strong analytical skills and the ability to interpret data to make informed decisions. Excellent communication and presentation skills. Ability to strategize, execute, and monitor ad campaigns independently. Detail-oriented with strong organizational skills.
