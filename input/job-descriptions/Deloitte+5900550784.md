## Full job description

Deloitte Global develops and leads strategic programs and services across the Deloitte network.

The Alliance Marketing Manager develops and implements integrated marketing campaigns, client-facing programs, and event sponsorships for the Salesforce Alliance. The role oversees marketing strategy, content development, core messaging and positioning, creative direction, and campaign management. It creates targeted campaigns to drive pipeline, visibility, and eminence while collaborating with stakeholders to define requirements and deliver results.

Responsibilities include planning marketing programs with global counterparts to build pipeline and support go-to-market initiatives; measuring effectiveness and contributing to ROI reports; creating strategic messaging and positioning; producing thought leadership, reports, surveys, events, videos, webcasts, speaking opportunities, social media, blogs, and digital advertising; maintaining project timelines, lead tracking, and reporting; collaborating with relationship managers, alliance teams, and sales teams; using marketing automation, events, collateral, thought leadership, and digital channels; developing event strategy; managing budgets; coordinating internal service providers and external vendors; sharing best practices; and monitoring marketing effectiveness.

Required qualifications include a bachelor's degree in Marketing or a related field; 8+ years of enterprise B2B marketing experience in professional services; 3+ years of management experience leading direct reports; experience creating demand-generation programs and digital marketing strategies; strong multichannel marketing-program execution; experience with digital advertising, branding, social media, event marketing, written messaging, thought leadership, B2B collateral, and presenting concepts to colleagues, clients, and alliance partners; experience leading through influence in a matrix organization; high proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and AI tools; and willingness to travel up to 30%.

Preferred qualifications include 5+ years of technology-alliance marketing experience, an advanced degree, experience managing and developing marketing teams, and experience working virtually with remote resources. The role also calls for succinct synthesis, technology integration, written and oral communication, negotiation, project management, problem solving, adaptability, organization, and simultaneous management of time-sensitive projects.
