## Role overview

Boostability is seeking a hands-on senior individual contributor who can turn marketing strategy into measurable business growth. The role owns integrated go-to-market, account-based marketing, and demand-generation programs that create qualified demand, improve pipeline, and support revenue growth. It works closely with Sales, Business Development, Revenue Operations, and Marketing and reports to the Vice President of Marketing.

## Responsibilities

- Develop integrated go-to-market, demand-generation, and growth campaigns across email, paid media, web, content, events, and other channels.
- Execute account-based marketing programs across one-to-one, one-to-few, and one-to-many account strategies.
- Partner with Sales, Business Development, and Revenue Operations to define target accounts, personas, campaign priorities, and pipeline goals.
- Build vertical-specific programs around audiences, customer pain points, market opportunities, and competitive positioning.
- Own campaigns from strategy and audience definition through launch, optimization, measurement, and pipeline contribution.
- Write campaign briefs covering audiences, messaging, offers, channel plans, sales alignment, and success metrics.
- Improve lead volume, lead quality, conversion rates, account engagement, and pipeline contribution.
- Segment campaigns by persona, industry, account tier, buying stage, and engagement activity.
- Identify buyer and account signals and translate them into marketing and sales opportunities.
- Coordinate partner campaigns, co-branded assets, webinars, events, and lead-generation programs.
- Support prospecting, nurture, opportunity progression, and sales-enablement efforts.
- Coordinate web, creative, content, marketing operations, and paid-media deliverables across concurrent initiatives.
- Support pre-event, during-event, and post-event campaigns that improve engagement and pipeline.
- Facilitate alignment meetings across Marketing, Sales, and Business Development.
- Monitor MQL volume, qualified pipeline, conversion rates, cost per lead, customer acquisition cost, account engagement, channel performance, and ROI.
- Maintain dashboards and reporting that show campaign performance and pipeline contribution.
- Report results, risks, insights, and recommended changes.
- Test and optimize audiences, messages, channels, offers, and campaign tactics.
- Maintain consistent marketing and sales messaging and collaborate in a shared-services matrix.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in marketing, communications, business, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
- Five or more years of progressive B2B marketing experience.
- Two or more years in growth marketing, demand generation, ABM, or go-to-market campaign execution.
- Experience creating integrated multi-channel campaigns.
- Hands-on ABM experience including account targeting, audience segmentation, campaign development, sales alignment, and measurement.
- Knowledge of B2B demand generation, lead management, buyer journeys, funnel conversion, and pipeline development.
- Proficiency with Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and third-party analytics platforms.
- Experience with CRM and marketing automation platforms such as HubSpot and Salesforce.
- Experience with Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads, or similar paid platforms.
- Experience with dashboards and reporting for funnel, campaign, and pipeline performance.
- Strong project management across multiple complex initiatives.
- Strong written, verbal, analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
- Ability to communicate with senior leaders and cross-functional stakeholders.
- Ability to lead projects, influence teams, and mentor or support team members.
- Experience with collaboration tools such as Asana, Monday.com, and Slack.
