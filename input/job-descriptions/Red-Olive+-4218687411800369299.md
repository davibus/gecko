## Role overview

Red Olive is a digital agency seeking an experienced paid media manager to oversee advertising accounts across Google, Meta, and other platforms. The role works with account managers, SEO specialists, designers, and content strategists to improve client performance. Red Olive is part of the Stellar agency group, which is owned by Pixis AI and gives the team access to additional AI capabilities and cross-agency resources.

## Requirements

- 3+ years of hands-on PPC management experience.
- Expertise in Google Ads, Facebook/Meta Ads, Bing Ads, product listing ads, and related paid-media formats.
- Current Google Ads certification; Bing Ads certification is preferred.
- Strong conversion tracking, analytics, and retargeting knowledge.
- Familiarity with HTML, URL structures, and campaign transitions during website changes.
- Strong analytical ability and attention to detail.
- Google Tag Manager experience.
- Experience configuring GA4 goals and managing campaign transitions during website redesigns.
- Clear communication and the ability to present performance results to enterprise clients.

## Responsibilities

- Manage and optimize Google Ads, Facebook/Meta, Bing Ads, product listing ads, Amazon Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and remarketing programs across industries and budget levels.
- Review performance metrics daily and recommend data-driven improvements.
- Partner with account managers on enterprise reporting and present performance insights in client meetings.
- Monitor budget pacing and reallocate spend toward the strongest return.
- Advise on landing-page improvements and conversion-funnel performance.
- Combine AI-powered and manual analysis to improve quality scores, reduce click fraud, and increase conversions.

## Benefits and culture

Benefits include medical insurance, 401(k), holiday pay, vacation pay, and half-day Fridays. Red Olive describes a collaborative, low-ego environment that values autonomy, curiosity, ownership, and sustainable agency work.
