## Job description

What is Contentstack ? Contentstack is redefining how modern digital experiences are built and managed. As the pioneer of the Agentic Experience Platform (AXP), Contentstack brings together structured content and brand governance (Content Cloud), real-time customer data, omnichannel personalization (Data Cloud) and autonomous AI orchestration (Agent OS) into one unified system. While many organizations adopted headless CMS or standalone AI tools expecting transformation, they often found themselves managing disconnected systems and manual workflows. Contentstack helps enterprises move beyond that complexity by connecting content, data and AI in a way that makes digital experiences faster to launch, easier to manage and more adaptive in real time. Leading brands including Steve Madden, LG Electronics, Subaru of America, Dolce & Gabbana, ASICS, Decathlon, and Caesars Entertainment rely on Contentstack to reduce operational friction and deliver personalized, scalable digital experiences with confidence. The role has a strong commercial focus. You’ll help protect and grow our largest installed base, drive expansion from CMS into Assets and Launch, and shape how we compete in highly competitive enterprise markets. You’ll also help evolve our story as Contentstack moves toward agentic experiences, using customer insights, market intelligence, and adoption data to continuously improve our strategy. Contentstack is an AI-forward company, and we bring practical experience using AI tools and technologies to improve how we work, learn, solve problems, and drive impact. If you’re excited by the opportunity to own a significant product line, shape how we compete in the market, and make a measurable commercial impact, we’d love to hear from you!
