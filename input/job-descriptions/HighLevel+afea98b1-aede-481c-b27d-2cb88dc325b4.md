Role Overview As Senior Product Manager – Marketing OS, LeadGen, you will help shape the future of lead generation at HighLevel. You will own product strategy, roadmap, and execution across a portfolio that includes Forms, Surveys, Quizzes, QR Codes, Chat Widget, External Tracking, and adjacent lead capture and attribution capabilities. Your mandate will be to evolve these products into a cohesive LeadGen experience that enables agencies and SMBs to create high-converting acquisition journeys, collect better-quality data, understand visitor behavior, and activate every lead through HighLevel's CRM and automation ecosystem. This is a senior individual contributor role. You will work closely with Engineering, Design, and Data on your portfolio, partner with adjacent product teams on shared foundations, and help shape how HighLevel competes in lead generation, conversational engagement, behavioral tracking, and marketing attribution. Your work will directly impact product adoption, lead volume, lead quality, engagement, conversion, retention, and overall platform growth. Your Responsibilities Own the LeadGen roadmap: Define and execute the product strategy and roadmap across Forms, Surveys, Quizzes, QR Codes, Chat Widget, and External Tracking. Build cohesive experiences: Drive consistency across builders, templates, publishing, data models, integrations, analytics, and reporting. Improve lead capture: Ship capabilities that increase visitor engagement, completion rates, lead quality, qualification, routing, and follow-up. Connect the customer journey: Partner with CRM, Workflows, Conversations, Analytics, and Payments teams to connect interactions with downstream business outcomes. Drive External Tracking: Define how HighLevel captures visitor behavior, conversions, identity, attribution, and campaign activity across third-party websites. Strengthen analytics foundations: Contribute to consistent event taxonomies, instrumentation standards, and reporting across the LeadGen portfolio. Drive AI-assisted creation: Deliver AI-powered creation, copy generation, logic recommendations, qualification, insights, and conversion optimization. Improve cross-device experiences: Ensure creation and consumption experiences are intuitive, responsive, accessible, and performant across desktop, mobile, and tablet. Partner on technical decisions: Work with Engineering on builders, embeddable widgets, APIs, submission pipelines, tracking scripts, identity resolution, and event infrastructure. Drive cross-functional execution: Align Product, Engineering, Design, Data, AI, Security, Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, and Partnerships around shared outcomes. Support go-to-market: Contribute to product positioning, pricing, packaging, messaging, launches, enablement, onboarding, and customer education. Operate hands-on: Write PRDs and strategy documents, conduct discovery, analyze data, validate concepts, prioritize initiatives, and resolve ambiguity. Own measurable outcomes: Drive improvements in adoption, activation, publishing, engagement, completion, conversion, attribution coverage, and retention.
