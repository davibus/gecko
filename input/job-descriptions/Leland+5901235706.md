## Job description

Leland is building a platform for ambition in the age of AI, connecting people with experts, programs, and communities that help them land jobs, enter school, or upskill. The company also helps businesses become AI-native through training, hiring, and infrastructure. This full-time, on-site Performance Marketing Manager role is based at Leland's Lehi, Utah headquarters.

The manager will own paid acquisition across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn for Leland's consumer business while helping establish an early B2B paid pipeline for the AI Builder Program. Day-to-day work includes budgets, bidding, targeting, creative rotation, and reporting across Google Search, Performance Max, YouTube, Meta, and LinkedIn. The role manages spend across core consumer categories and develops a clear view of where incremental budget should go.

The manager will drive performance against CAC and payback targets, flag underperformance early, and help define the metrics that matter across the funnel. The role runs structured tests across audiences, offers, landing pages, and creative, documenting learnings so the marketing team improves over time. It partners with channel and business-unit owners to support the full buyer journey from first touch through purchase or closed deal.

For B2B growth, the manager will develop paid programs across Google, LinkedIn, and Meta to generate pipeline for the AI Builder Program's team and enterprise offering. This includes collaborating with sales and AI Programs marketing on ideal customer profiles, target-account lists, lead magnets, and the handoff from lead to booked meeting. The manager will also help refine paid support for Leland's university business.

Reporting must connect spend to pipeline and closed revenue rather than stopping at form fills, signups, or clicks. The role uses Claude and other AI tools to automate repetitive work such as report pulls, anomaly detection, ad-variation drafting, audience building, and campaign QA. It is expected to maximize platform-native automation while applying human judgment, and to create systems and documentation that spread channel knowledge across the organization. The manager also works closely with content and creative partners or contractors to maintain a healthy ad pipeline and provide specific performance feedback.

Qualifications include five to seven years of hands-on performance marketing experience and direct ownership of meaningful budgets across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn. Experience spanning consumer purchase acquisition and sales-led B2B pipeline is a plus. Candidates should have strong data instincts, comfort in ad platforms and GA4, an understanding of attribution and lift testing, and practical experience using AI for automations, scripts, skills, agents, or other real outputs. The role calls for a systems thinker who builds scalable processes and a direct communicator who gives an honest read on performance.

Databricks experience is a plus. Additional preferred experience includes edtech, marketplaces, AI enablement, or selling to learning-and-development or university buyers. The working toolset includes Google Search, Performance Max, YouTube, Meta, LinkedIn, GA4, Databricks, Claude, attribution analysis, and lift testing.
