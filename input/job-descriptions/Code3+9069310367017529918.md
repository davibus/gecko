The Opportunity As a Senior Strategist, Paid Search B2B, you’ll be responsible for leading paid search strategy, activation and thought leadership across a portfolio of B2B clients. You’ll bring a strong understanding of how search can drive demand throughout the B2B buyer journey, from initial consideration and lead generation through qualified opportunities, pipeline and revenue. You have a deep passion for staying on top of trends and understand how paid search fits into the broader B2B media and demand generation ecosystem. You’ll work closely with cross-disciplinary teams across both challenger and enterprise accounts, managing performance, driving growth and helping clients shift their thinking beyond basic campaign metrics to connect paid search investment with broader marketing, sales and business outcomes.
