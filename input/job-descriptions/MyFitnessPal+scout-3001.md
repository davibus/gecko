## Job description (tracker description/listing field)

<p><strong>About the role</strong></p>
<p>MyFitnessPal is investing in creators as a scalable growth channel. As Influencer Marketing Manager, you will lead the day-to-day execution of our influencer program while owning how we measure its success and demonstrate its return on investment. You will manage a team of influencer specialists and work directly with talent, agents, and internal partners.</p>
<p><strong>What you'll do</strong></p>
<p><em>Day-to-day execution</em></p>
<ul>
<li>Oversee creator communications and provide hands-on support throughout the partnership lifecycle, from outreach and onboarding to briefing, content delivery, payment and performance analysis</li>
<li>Build and maintain long-term relationships with creators, talent managers, and agencies to drive loyalty, preferred rates, and repeat partnerships</li>
<li>Evaluate incoming and proposed influencer deals, determining which are worth pursuing based on audience fit, performance potential, and cost</li>
<li>Directly manage relationships with celebrity and macro influencers, including negotiation oversight and content planning</li>
<li>Review creator content to ensure it meets brand standards, messaging requirements, and campaign objectives</li>
<li>Track influencer performance against established KPIs and report results to stakeholders on a regular cadence</li>
</ul>
<p><em>Program management</em></p>
<ul>
<li>Manage influencer budgets and allocate spend across creators, tiers, and platforms to maximize return</li>
<li>Partner with cross-functional teams (e.g., Brand, Product Marketing, Growth Marketing, Legal, Finance) to support ongoing marketing campaigns and integrate creator activity into broader plans</li>
<li>Lead, coach, and develop influencer specialists who negotiate creator contracts, setting priorities, deal guidelines, and quality standards</li>
<li>Improve existing automations and build new automated processes (e.g., outreach, tracking, reporting, payments) to increase team efficiency and scalability</li>
</ul>
<p><em>Strategy and measurement</em></p>
<ul>
<li>Develop influencer strategies and programs aligned with overall brand and business objectives</li>
<li>Research industry trends, emerging platforms, and competitor activity, and translate findings into actionable recommendations</li>
<li>Determine how we define and measure program success, including the KPIs, benchmarks, and reporting framework used across the organization</li>
<li>Own our approach to conversion measurement, including attribution methodology and tracking tools, so creator activity can be credibly tied to downstream results such as installs, trials, and paid subscriptions</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What you'll bring</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>5-7 years of experience in influencer, creator, or social media marketing, including experience managing celebrity or macro talent</li>
<li>Proven experience managing budgets and negotiating or overseeing creator contracts</li>
<li>Experience leading or mentoring team members</li>
<li>Strong analytical skills, with experience in attribution, performance reporting, and ROI analysis</li>
<li>Excellent communication, relationship-building, and negotiation skills</li>
<li>Strong understanding of TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube creator ecosystems&nbsp;</li>
<li>Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment</li>
<li>Experience in health, fitness, nutrition, or consumer subscription apps</li>
<li>Familiarity with mobile measurement partners, affiliate platforms, and creator management tools</li>
<li>Experience with AI tools and automations a plus</li>
