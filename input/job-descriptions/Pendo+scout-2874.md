## Job description (tracker description/listing field)

Headquarters: Remote / San Francisco, CA / New York, NY / Raleigh, NC The Team + The Role Pendo’s Growth Marketing team owns the engine that creates predictable, efficient demand across paid, organic, lifecycle, and product-led channels. The team connects marketing strategy, analytics, experimentation, and cross-functional execution to generate qualified pipeline and improve the buyer and user journey. As VP, Growth Marketing, you will lead Pendo’s full growth engine from top-of-funnel awareness through qualified pipeline creation. You will own demand generation, website performance, marketing analytics, experimentation, lifecycle programs, and product-led growth motions while partnering closely with Sales, Product, the CMO, the CRO, and the executive team. Remote in the United States What this looks like day-to-day Demand generation and pipeline: Own demand generation strategy and execution across paid search, paid social, SEO/GEO, content-driven acquisition, and multi-channel campaigns. Build programs that generate qualified pipeline and improve the efficiency and predictability of growth. Cross-functional partnership: Partner closely with Sales on pipeline handoff and conversion, with Product on growth features and activation, and with the CMO and CRO on GTM priorities and investment allocation. Bring a clear growth point of view to executive and operator-level decisions. Marketing website performance: Own the marketing website as a core growth asset, including performance, conversion rate optimization, experimentation, lead routing, and continuous improvement of the buyer journey and demo experience. Partner with the CMO, VP of Product Marketing, and design team on UX/UI elements and product page content. Paid creative strategy: Bring a strong point of view on paid creative and partner with the broader marketing team on evergreen paid assets and integrated campaign assets. Ensure the team tracks creative performance and understands why each asset wins or loses. Marketing analytics and attribution: Build and maintain clear attribution models, dashboards, and reporting that give leadership accurate visibility into what drives pipeline and revenue. Use measurement infrastructure to guide prioritization, investment decisions, and budget efficiency. Experimentation system: Establish a rigorous testing culture across acquisition, the website, onboarding, and lifecycle. Identify what works, scale successful tests, and create a system for continuous improvement. Team leadership: Build, lead, and develop a high-performing growth organization across demand generation, performance marketing, SEO/GEO, website, analytics, and product/lifecycle growth. Create a team culture defined by ownership, pace, measurement, and cross-functional alignment. Product-led and lifecycle growth: Drive activation, onboarding, time-to-value, retention, and expansion programs that increase product adoption and net revenue retention. Connect product-led and lifecycle motions to broader pipeline, revenue, and customer growth outcomes. Who You Are Beyond the qualifications, we hire through a specific lens.
