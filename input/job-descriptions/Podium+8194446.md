Podium is building what we believe will be the most impactful AI employee ecosystem for local business. Podium has been recognized as the Best AI Implementation by Inc. Our growth is fueled by hiring exceptional people, holding them to high standards, and creating opportunities for them to grow and make an impact. Our operating principles guide daily behavior and ensure we hire people who will thrive at Podium. If you're hungry for growth, aligned to our operating principles, and ready to get to work, you won't find a better place to learn and accelerate your career. About the Role We are transforming the way small businesses operate with an AI workforce, and we're hiring a Director who will run marketing demand generation end-to-end. Think of this as the CMO of a sub-vertical: you own the monthly revenue, pipeline, and efficiency numbers the business is counting on. You own the strategy and channel execution to get there. This is a builder and operator role: you'll roll up your sleeves, drive critical projects, lean in deeply with a team of direct and indirect reports, and IC where needed to get the job done. You're deeply performance and demand focused. The right person is highly biased towards action, hungry to drive impact, exercises extreme ownership, thrives at a startup pace, and can set a vision that inspires the team. You're a heavy AI user, passionate about building a marketing engine at the bleeding edge of what's possible. This role is required to be in office 5 days per week at our Lehi HQ. What You'll Own Set an integrated marketing strategy that delivers on demand targets and rallies the team. Set the vision, set the pace, and hold the org to it. Hold full accountability for marketing's monthly revenue, pipeline, and efficiency targets, plus the levers underneath them: channel mix, budget allocation, execution, comms calendar, and demand content. You own the number and how it gets hit. Track pacing and pipeline against targets, and proactively lead the team in building and executing on gap plans the moment funnel targets slip. Lead, coach, and raise the ceiling for a lean team of direct reports owning trade show/events, partner marketing, webinars, third-party media, lifecycle and content marketing channels. Proactively collaborate with a team of indirect reports (centralized marketing subject matter experts) across digital channels, AEO/SEO, and web, to execute on the highest impact strategies in your vertical. Rigorously measure the efficacy of tactics/channels/funnel and continuously optimize via experimentation. Align marketing portfolio bets to maximize impact. Manage the vertical budget, spending within profitability constraints, surfacing opportunities. Drive AOP planning prep and recommendations. Regularly report on marketing performance and strategy to leadership / exec team. Set the standard for AI-first marketing in tooling, planning, and agent building, deploying AI workflows that transform impact. Work closely with marketing leadership across other verticals (auto, aesthetics) and partnering functions (design, product marketing) to prep for launches, align on resourcing and positioning. Align with Sales, CS, Product, Finance, and Revenue Operations leaders on goals and priorities. Candidate Profile Must Haves Successful track record of leading an integrated, demand generation strategy/function or channel group with accountability to a revenue number, a budget, and ROI. Hands-on depth across relevant marketing channels like trade shows, field or partner marketing, lifecycle, webinars, content marketing, digital, and ABM. You've personally run a collection of channels at similar scale. Extreme ownership and bias to action. You move fast without waiting for consensus, and you're comfortable building a strategy out of ambiguity and high change. Critical thinker and analytically rigorous. You make data informed decisions using full funnel metrics and know what inputs are most important to drive to a decision. Highly performance focused and ROI minded. Understands the right balance of investment into higher funnel initiatives and the limitations of various attribution methods. Excellent communicator. Clear, decisive, and collaborative. Sets a high bar for the team and provides the coaching, vision and accountability to get there. Excellent project management skills. Can drive action and alignment. Customer first. Draws on a deep understanding of the customer, industry and Podium value. Uses AI heavily, building workflows and agents. Can show what you've built. A strong cross-functional partner. Builds trust and alignment across the org. Nice-to-Haves Experience marketing to SMBs / local businesses or marketing an AI product. Familiarity with the home services trades (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest, landscaping, etc.) and their buying behavior. Familiarity with the medspa aesthetics industry and their buying behavior. Not a Fit If You need a big team, a big agency, or a big budget to operate. You want to run strategy and hand execution to someone else. You own both.
