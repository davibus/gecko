Remote Paid Advertising Manager Position (Full-Time, US, Canada, and UK Citizens Only) Seeking an experienced Paid Advertising Manager for a contract remote role, operating from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, Monday to Friday. This role is client-facing. Responsibilities include managing client accounts on Google Ads and Bing Ads, tracking progress, client communication, and maintaining detailed records. US, Canada, and UK based applicants only. This position REQUIRES Client Facing Experience. Responsibilities: Manage PPC accounts on Google Ads, Yahoo, Bing, etc. Monitor bids, budget caps, quality scores, and other metrics. Update keyword lists and manage campaign strategies. Focus on conversion metrics to optimize client ROI. Suggest creative copy and manage display network placements. Develop and execute new campaign strategies. Produce and understand client reports, keeping up with PPC industry trends. Collaborate with the team to achieve client goals. Communicate regularly with end clients by phone, video conference calls, and in writing. Qualifications: Proven experience with Google Ads; Bing Ads and Facebook Ads a plus. Knowledge of eCommerce, Google Tag Manager, and audience building preferred. Ability to troubleshoot, think creatively, and work in a team. Proficient in MS Office and analytics tools like Google Analytics. Google Ads and Analytics certification preferred; Facebook Blueprint Certification a plus. Comfortable Talking to Clients. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience; web development skills a plus. Minimum 3 years of experience in Paid Search in an online environment. Ideal candidates are detail-oriented, multi-tasking, and have a passion for internet marketing, with excellent analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
