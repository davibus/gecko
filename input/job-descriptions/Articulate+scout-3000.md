## Job description (tracker description/listing field)

We are seeking a Senior Product Marketing Manager to join our Product Marketing team. This role owns the go-to-market (GTM) strategy for one or more products within the Articulate platform, including the GTM plan against revenue goals and the commercial model behind it.
You will shape messaging, pricing and packaging, use funnel data and AI systems to sharpen the plan, and build the launch foundations for execution. You will partner with product, marketing, sales, customer success, growth, ops, and ecosystem and reseller partners to shape the roadmap and carry recommendations to senior leaders. We are looking for someone who writes the playbook, builds the systems the team runs on, and is accountable for the plan that delivers a revenue goal.

- Revenue and GTM plan. Own the GTM plan against the revenue goal for your product(s). Work with Sales and Ops on targets, track progress against them, and decide what to scale, fix, pause or stop as results come in.

- 

Commercial model. Shape the pricing, packaging, and monetization approach for your product(s). Make recommendations and carry them through to a decision with cross-functional partners.

- 

Shared context and systems. Build and maintain the positioning, messaging, and competitive context, plus the skills and workflows, that the PMM team and its AI agents run on.

- 

Funnel data and insights. Own the funnel for your product(s), from first touch through conversion and expansion. Help shape the dashboards and metrics the team relies on, using them to turn insights into GTM strategy and execution.

- 

Field readiness. Act as the voice of product, owning field messaging, FAQs, web content, and live enablement for Sales, Customer Success, and reseller partners.

- 

Build positioning, value propositions, and differentiated messaging for the full buying committee, from practitioners and business creators to administrators and IT stakeholders, and hold a high quality bar on what the team ships

- 

Develop deep insight into customers, segments, and target accounts, and use research, usage data, and buying signals to guide GTM plans and the roadmap

- 

Build the launch and campaign foundations (plans, templates, assets, standards) that Growth, Content, and Sales execute from

- 

Work with Product and Engineering on the roadmap, and make the case for what to build next and how it should be priced and packaged

- 

Work with Sales, Customer Success, Legal, and Marketing leadership to reach decisions on commercial strategy that cross teams

- 

Adapt GTM across ecosystem, integration, and reseller partners as revenue channels, including positioning, co-marketing, enablement, and shared account plans

- 

Build with AI every day - create workflows and agents for your own work, and add to the team's shared context, skills, and agents so everyone's output improves

- 

Decide what to do yourself, what to delegate, and what to automate

- 

Create internal and field assets, track competitors and market trends, and support events

-

 About us

Articulate Global, LLC, is the leading SaaS provider of creator platforms for online workplace training. Founded by Adam Schwartz in 2002, Articulate provides creator tools and services that make it simple for enterprises and SMBs to develop, deliver, and analyze online workplace training that’s engaging and effective.  Increasingly, organizations must reskill employees for ever-changing remote and hybrid work environments, create learning cultures that attract and retain employees in a tight labor market, and use training to build more equitable, empowering, and engaging workplaces. Articulate helps organizations address these critical business needs with its creator platform for workplace training. Articulate 360—a suite of creator tools for online courses—was named the 7th most-loved product in the world by TrustRadius in 2021. And Rise—an all-in-one online training system that makes online training easy to create, enjoyable to take, and simple to manage—is the first creator platform for SMBs and departments within the enterprise. Articulate has more than 118,000 customers in 170 countries and counts all 100 of the Fortune 100 companies as customers.
