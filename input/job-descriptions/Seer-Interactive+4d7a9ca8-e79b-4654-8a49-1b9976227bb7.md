## Full job description

The Role : Seer is hiring a Demand Generation Manager to own the systems, programs, and measurement infrastructure behind our demand gen strategy. This is a hands-on, individual contributor role on a 3-person marketing team, reporting to Director of Marketing. You'll own HubSpot campaigns and programs as our MOPs owner, newsletter growth and engagement, webinar and digital event programs (including owned and third-party event registration through conversion), lifecycle nurture programs, demand gen asset coordination, automations for tracking, flagging, and repurposing content across channels, and KPI ownership and reporting on outbound MQLs, program conversion, and event engagement. This role is not a content creation role, blog and editorial live with our content team, so you'll focus on distribution, repurposing, and program performance. We're looking for someone who has actually built in a marketing automation platform: built workflows, set up campaigns and emails, run a newsletter, not just logged into one. Experience with events across different regional markets is a plus, and industry background matters less than platform depth. \n Role Highlights: Campaigns: Build and run demand campaigns end to end, sequencing what the team produces across email, social, events, comms, paid, and outbound rather than letting it ship one-off Programs: Own our newsletters end to end: strategy, curation, production, reporting. Own the Signal webinar series and our event and conference presence, from promotion through logistics through follow-up PR, comms, and 3rd-party presence : Run the programs that put us in front of new audiences (press and referral partners, and the conferences where our people speak), and convert that exposure into owned-audience growth and warm conversations handed to growth. Execution in HubSpot: Own our HubSpot instance end to end, build and launch campaigns, emails, workflows, and lifecycle steps, and maintain the architecture as our MOPs owner Distribution loop: Take what our content team writes & publishes, test formats and channels, read performance, and scale what works Measurement: Own program KPIs and marketing reporting. Keep a defensible view of what's working that informs what we build next Applied AI: use AI to accelerate program production, enablement, and case study work without flattening our voice Essential Skills You've run B2B demand generation end to end: campaigns, newsletters, webinars, events, and owned the number attached to them You've been hands-on in a marketing automation platform. You've built and launched in HubSpot or comparable, not just briefed someone else to You have strong program management instincts: you can hold a campaign map and a run-of-show for a webinar in your head at the same time You have strong writing skills (though you'll have content support) and can execute a case study or review blog posts as needed You believe in building repeatable systems over one-off execution Bonus You have hands-on channel experience in more than one discipline (e.g., SEO + Paid, or Paid + Analytics) You have a background in Client Engagement, Account Management, or a client-facing strategy role and have been responsible for organic account growth You have experience in a digital agency environment where you've pitched or contributed to pitches for new clients You have familiarity with tools like Pipedrive, HubSpot, or LinkedIn Sales Navigator 30/60/90 30 Days Learn the systems, the data, and the current program calendar Begin take over newsletter production Help Establish the performance-tracking rhythm and 3/6-month targets 60 Days Take on the webinar program end to end, including promotion and logistics Run one campaign end to end across at least three channels Take on day-to-day HubSpot campaign execution from RevOps 90 days Support building measurement system for program performance in place Put trackable reporting for campaigns, events, and enablement in place Propose and implement one net-new performance improvement \n $90,000 - $105,000 a year Your final offered compensation will be determined by your skills and experience Evaluation of comp at least once a year Benefit highlights \n Ready to apply?
