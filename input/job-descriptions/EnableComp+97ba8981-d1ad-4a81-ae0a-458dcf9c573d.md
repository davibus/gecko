POSITION SUMMARY The Manager, Growth Marketing owns EnableComp’s demand generation engine — planning and executing the multi-channel campaigns that build pipeline, and, as our first dedicated content hire, creating the blog posts, ebooks, landing pages, and nurture content those campaigns run on. This role builds on the technical infrastructure the GTM Engineer maintains and the positioning the Director, Product Marketing sets, translating both into campaigns that generate and nurture qualified leads. Reporting to the VP, Marketing, the Manager will partner closely with Product Marketing, the Commercial Enablement Director, the Manager of Event Marketing & Strategic Partnerships, and the SDR function to ensure a consistent, well-coordinated path from first touch to qualified pipeline. This is an individual contributor role with no direct reports at this time. \n The Job Responsibilities Include Plan, build, and execute multi-channel demand generation campaigns across email, paid social, SEM, retargeting, and organic channels. Own marketing-sourced pipeline targets and report on campaign performance against revenue goals. Manage the demand generation budget, tracking spend against ROI and reallocating based on channel performance. Own funnel content end-to-end: blog posts, ebooks/guides, landing pages, and nurture email copy that fuel campaigns — this is our first dedicated content hire, so the expectation is building the engine, not just staffing it. Ensure all campaign content reflects current positioning and messaging maintained by the Director, Product Marketing. Establish a scalable, repeatable content calendar and production workflow. Build and manage lead nurture email programs and campaign workflows inside HubSpot, working within the lead scoring and account routing logic the GTM Engineer designs and maintains. Partner with the GTM Engineer on campaign-specific automation needs and data requirements, without owning the underlying tech stack, integrations, or scoring/routing architecture. Monitor and troubleshoot campaign-level workflow issues, escalating systemic or infrastructure issues to the GTM Engineer. Partner with the Manager, Event Marketing & Strategic Partnerships on webinar and event promotion strategy, registration goals, and post-event lead nurture (Events owns strategy, logistics, and execution; Growth Marketing owns the pipeline goals and follow-up). Partner with the Director, Product Marketing to ensure campaigns and content reflect current narrative, positioning, and competitive messaging. Partner with the SDR function on lead handoff, MQL quality feedback, and conversion tracking. Analyze campaign and content performance data to identify what’s working, kill what isn’t, and continuously optimize for MQL volume, conversion rate, and cost per acquisition. Stay current on emerging channels and AI-powered marketing/content tools, piloting new tactics to keep programs ahead of the curve. Other duties as assigned. Requirements & Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field required; equivalent combination of education and experience will be considered. 3–5 years of experience in demand generation, growth marketing, or a related B2B marketing role. Hands-on experience with marketing automation platforms (HubSpot preferred) and CRM systems (Salesforce). Strong analytical skills, with experience pulling and interpreting campaign data to make decisions.
