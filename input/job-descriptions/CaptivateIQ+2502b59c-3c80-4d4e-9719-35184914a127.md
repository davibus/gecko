## Job description

CaptivateIQ is transforming the way companies plan, manage, and optimize sales performance. We started by revolutionizing incentive compensation management, and now we're expanding our platform to solve broader sales planning challenges. Recognized by industry analysts like Forrester and G2 and backed by top-tier investors, including Sequoia, ICONIQ, Accel, and Sapphire Ventures, we empower high-growth companies like Netflix, Figma, and Stripe with the flexibility and insights needed to drive revenue performance. Join a talented, fast-growing team committed to solving some of the most complex and impactful problems in sales performance management. About the Role We’re looking for a highly strategic Senior Content Marketing Manager to own the complete lifecycle of our content: strategy, execution, and distribution. Success in this role will be rooted in critical thinking and a structured approach to problem-solving, which will directly shape our market narrative. In this role, you will be the architect of our market point of view, developing a high-velocity distribution engine across social and owned channels, and orchestrating a high-impact editorial and social calendar that directly fuels awareness, engagement, and pipeline for the business. You’ll collaborate closely with customers, SMEs, and cross-functional partners to craft differentiated narratives for RevOps, Sales, and Finance audiences. This is a highly growth-oriented role. Your success will require not just a clear, documented content strategy and standout content but also the ability to translate content into growth through increased traffic, owned audience, and brand awareness. Establish an editorial governance model including voice and tone, messaging guardrails, briefing templates, and QA standards to ensure clarity, consistency, and brand integrity. Coordinate external partners (agencies and freelancers) with clear briefs, SLAs, and editorial standards to scale production as needed.
