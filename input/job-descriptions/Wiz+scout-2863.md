About the Job As a Business Partner, Marketing Operations , your goal is to act as a strategic advisor to regional marketing stakeholders - identifying friction trends, solving system level bottlenecks, and driving pipeline scale. Rather than focusing on manual campaign builds, you will drive proactive system-level diagnosis and solutions to ensure commercial decision making and GTM execution feel effortless and fast. Your primary daily focus is simple: unblock marketers so they can focus on building pipeline.
