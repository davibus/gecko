About AutoFi AutoFi is the leading provider of digital commerce technology that powers the sales and finance experiences for the most innovative brands and dealers in automotive. The AutoFi platform enables a more transactional buying experience with $4B in funded loans processed through AutoFi annually. AutoFi’s dynamic selling platform empowers dealers to sell vehicles more efficiently and profitably, both online and in the showroom. We are funded for years of future growth and backed by investors including Crosslink Capital, Santander Holdings USA, SVB Financial Group, Ford, BMW iVentures and JP Morgan Chase. Our team is diverse - spread out across the U.S. and Canada, we have backgrounds from finance and technology as well as deep experience in all areas of the auto space. We’re empathetic, gritty, curious, and humble owners of this business and are supported by some of the biggest names in the auto and financial industries as commercial partners. We’ve never been more excited about the opportunity in front of us to help transition the auto industry from offline to online. If changing a trillion-dollar industry sounds exciting, we’d love to hear from you. For more information, visit www.autofi.com. About the Role AutoFi is looking for a versatile Digital Marketing & Creative Manager who can bring ideas to life across digital channels. This isn't a traditional graphic design role. We're looking for a well-rounded marketer who combines strong creative instincts with hands-on digital marketing skills and is comfortable working across design, video, web, social media and campaign execution. You'll work closely with our Content Marketing Manager and Head of Marketing to develop and execute campaigns across AutoFi's website, social channels, email programs, events and sales initiatives. The right person is comfortable moving between disciplines. One day you may be creating campaign graphics or editing a customer video; the next you may be building a WordPress landing page, publishing social content or helping launch a new product campaign. What We're Looking For We're looking for someone who is creative but practical, who enjoys making things but also understands why they're being made. You should be equally comfortable starting with a blank canvas and jumping into an existing project that needs to get across the finish line. You'll have room to be creative, but you'll also be part of a small marketing team where everyone contributes beyond a single specialty.
