You will turn creators and communities into a steady flow of targeted traffic and sign-ups across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit and Quora, with clear goals, your own area of responsibility and an agreed budget to work with. Every idea you test, from a new author to a new format or distribution channel, shows up directly in user growth and cost per registration. And as the channel proves itself, you will build a team of 2–3 people to scale what works About our product: This is a web-first platform for real-time text and video communication, built around instant connections between real people.
