## Job description

Who we are Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale. Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. As part of a recently public company, you’ll have the autonomy and support to make an impact as we build for the long term. About the Role Samsara is seeking a Sr. Operations Manager - Product Marketing to build the AI-first operating strategy and system that runs Product Marketing at scale, replacing manual handoffs with automated, always-current infrastructure. PMM Operations: You'll build the AI-first operational strategy that makes a world-class PMM team hum. Work with AI marketing experts to turn ad hoc and manual processes into automated workflows that run itself. Build the context layer, the strategic inputs and templates, that feeds the AI tools the team needs to multiply its impact. Release Strategy : You will re-imagine Samsara’s release motion with an AI-first system. You'll identify the strategy and context layer that is needed to create the tools and workflows that take the release process, deadlines, and launch context and turn it into the launch calendar, tiering, and messaging automatically. You will own the system that makes this knowledge actionable with reduced effort from the cross functional team. We are looking for someone who looks at systems and ways of doing things, and helps Product Marketing operate more efficiently and effectively, predominantly by putting AI streamlining in place. This is a remote position open to candidates based in the US.
