Pinterest is looking for a Senior Manager, Ads Product Marketing - Ads Automation to lead a key area of our ads delivery and automation product marketing strategy. In this role, you will help shape how Pinterest brings automation solutions to market, enabling advertisers to better understand performance, value Pinterest media accurately, and improve business outcomes. You will work at the intersection of product, market, and customer needs, translating ads delivery and automation complexity into clear strategy, positioning, and go-to-market execution. As a senior leader on the Ads Product Marketing team, you will oversee a team responsible for advancing Pinterest’s automation capabilities across areas such as bidding, targeting, or creative. You will partner closely with Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing Science, and Business Development to influence roadmaps, guide launches, and drive adoption of solutions that help advertisers automate how to reach their performance goals on Pinterest. What you’ll do Lead product marketing strategy and execution for a core area of Pinterest’s automation portfolio Manage and develop a team of PMMs responsible for complex, cross-functional automation
