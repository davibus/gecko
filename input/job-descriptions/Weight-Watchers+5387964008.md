What You'll Do The Vice President, Marketing & Advertising Counsel will serve as the key, senior legal advisor to WW’s Marketing, Brand, and Creative organizations. You will be responsible for reviewing marketing collateral to ensure compliance with federal advertising laws and intellectual property requirements, as well as drafting, reviewing, and negotiating a broad range of agreements that support advertising, digital marketing and promotional activities. This role requires a business-minded attorney who can balance legal risk with commercial objectives while supporting innovative marketing initiatives across multiple jurisdictions on a global basis. Marketing and Advertising Compliance Serve as primary legal reviewer for marketing and go-to-market collateral across the organization, ensuring compliance with FTC, FDA and state laws, as well as providing support for our global markets. Advise on proper substantiation requirements for product and service claims and alignment with regulatory requirements for weight management, medical offerings, and food, drug and supplement categories Monitor developments in advertising law and regulatory enforcement trends and advise internal stakeholders on emerging compliance risks Build scalable programs for influencer, creator, and partnerships at volume, including playbooks, vetting standards, self-service templates and disclosure controls to empower the Marketing organization to move quickly while staying within risk tolerance guardrails Cross-Functional Partnership Act as a trusted advisor to senior leadership on marketing strategy and legal risk. Collaborate closely with sales, marketing, digital, communications, and commercial teams on product launches and campaigns. Provide practical, solution-oriented legal advice in a fast-moving business environment.
