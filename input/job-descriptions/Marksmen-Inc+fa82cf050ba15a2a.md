## Company

Marksmen is a global brand-protection firm serving major companies and law firms. Its work includes confidential trademark, domain-name, and social-media-handle investigation, research, and acquisition, plus tracking counterfeiters. The company states that more than two billion people use trademarks and domain names it has secured for clients.

## Position Summary

Marksmen seeks an entrepreneurial, hands-on Director of Growth Marketing to own marketing and growth, report directly to the CEO, and generate more qualified business opportunities, pipeline, and revenue. The role combines strategy with personal execution: creating campaigns, writing copy, developing offers, analyzing results, using marketing technology, testing ideas, and ensuring initiatives launch.

The preferred leader is positive, collaborative, innovative, commercially minded, an exceptional writer, comfortable marketing expertise and trust in a sophisticated B2B environment, able to work directly with leadership, and proactive about proposing and testing growth ideas.

## Key Responsibilities

- Develop and execute the marketing and growth strategy around qualified opportunities, pipeline, and revenue.
- Create campaigns, positioning, messaging, offers, and content for target audiences.
- Grow existing client relationships through cross-selling, reactivation, and targeted programs.
- Convert company expertise, case history, and data into thought leadership and marketing.
- Launch campaigns for new services and continually test messages, audiences, and channels.
- Manage CRM, marketing automation, outside resources, and performance metrics.
- Identify growth opportunities proactively, bring ideas to leadership, and own marketing outcomes.

## Early Success Expectations
