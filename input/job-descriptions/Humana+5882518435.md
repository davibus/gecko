## Job description

Become a part of our caring community The Program Delivery Lead serves as a key partner to the VP of Commercial & Government Specialty, AD, Strategy Advancement and senior leadership team, driving strategic alignment, executive communication, division operations, event support and business planning. This role blends high level strategic advisory with hands on execution, ensuring leadership is equipped with clear insights, compelling materials, and coordinated plans to effectively run and grow the business. This role will lead the strategic design and narrative direction of Commercial & Government Specialty ensuring all programs align with organizational priorities and business goals. This role leads the development of executive materials including board presentations, strategy memos, and business reviews, while providing actionable insights through analysis of business performance, market trends, and competitive intelligence. Additionally, the Program Delivery Lead supports divisional meetings and events, facilitates cross functional collaboration, and serves as a trusted advisor to leadership to enable informed decision making and execution of key priorities. Use your skills to make an impact Key Responsibilities Executive Communications & Leadership Support Lead the development of executive level communications and materials for the VP of Commercial & Government Specialty in coordination with the AD, Strategy Advancement and senior leaders, including Board presentations, Strategy memos, Weekly Updates, Monthly and quarterly business reviews (MBR/MOR), Executive briefings and rapid response materials Ensure all content is clear, concise, and aligned to enterprise strategy and messaging. Anticipate leadership needs and proactively prepare insights and materials for key discussions. Provide perspective on risks, opportunities, and strategic choices to inform leadership decision making. Drive organizational execution and accountability through effective business cadence management, including agenda development, follow up tracking, action planning, and facilitation of divisional, leadership, and offsite meetings to ensure alignment and decision making. Act as a central coordination point to align priorities, messaging, and deliverables. Prepare tailored materials and insights for a variety of leadership audiences and forums. Coordinate business readiness efforts, ensuring stakeholders are informed, aligned, and equipped to execute against evolving priorities Strategic Planning & Initiative Management – Government & Commercial Specialty Partner with business management and strategy teams to coordinate: Annual planning cycles Goal setting and performance alignment Strategic review processes Track and monitor progress against KPIs, key strategic initiatives, identifying risks and ensuring accountability. Deliver regular updates and report to leadership on initiative status and outcomes. Use business intelligence and reporting tools to monitor, analyze and communicate performance indicators (KPIs). Lead cross-functional business initiatives by coordinating execution across key partners (e.g., Network, Delivery COE, Underwriting, Finance, Compliance, HR, and Actuarial), ensuring deliverables align with strategic priorities, operational objectives, and timelines. Division Operations & Event Support Support planning and execution of divisional events, leadership meetings, and offsites. Stakeholder Engagement & Cross-Functional Coordination across Government & Commercial Specialty. Facilitate strong relationships between business leaders and key partners.
