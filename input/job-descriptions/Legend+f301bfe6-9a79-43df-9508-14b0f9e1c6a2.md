## Job description

About Legend At Legend, we're building next-level player experiences. With 20+ years of proven success we’re the world's leading Sports and Gaming marketing company. Our people are our edge. 600+ sharp, ambitious Legends across 22+ countries who move fast, learn quickly and win together. Here growth isn’t a perk, it’s the standard. You’ll stretch, level-up and carve out a career as bold as the products we build. We celebrate wins and create experiences that people talk about for years - for our players & for each other. If you’re ready to shape the future of player experience and enjoy the ride while you do it, this is where you’ll thrive. The Role Legend is hiring a Principal Technical SEO, reporting directly to our Chief Gaming Officer. This is a rare opportunity to take portfolio-wide responsibility for the technical SEO performance of some of the largest and most commercially important domains in iGaming. Working alongside our existing Principal Technical SEO, you will co-own technical SEO across our Gaming portfolio, while also supporting our Sports portfolio. You will identify the technical opportunities and risks that matter most, turn them into clear priorities, and work closely with Product and Engineering to deliver measurable organic and commercial impact. This is a deeply hands-on role where you will solve complex challenges across crawling, rendering, indexation, architecture, internal linking, internationalisation, migrations and performance, while setting standards that scale across our portfolio. You will also play a key role in advancing how we use AI and automation across SEO. You will build tools, monitoring, diagnostics and automated workflows yourself, using technology to improve the speed and quality of decision-making and helping our wider SEO teams adopt these capabilities. This is a senior individual contributor role with significant autonomy and visibility. You will lead through expertise rather than formal authority, working alongside senior specialists and leaders across SEO, Product, Engineering and Analytics to shape decisions across the organisation. In this role, we value diverse perspectives and encourage you to apply even if you don't meet every qualification listed. Co-own our portfolio-wide technical SEO strategy, roadmap and standards, identifying and prioritising the opportunities and risks with the greatest potential organic and commercial impact. Lead complex technical investigations across crawling, rendering, indexation, architecture, internal linking, international SEO, migrations, structured data and web performance. Partner with Product and Engineering on architecture, major releases, migrations and experimentation, translating technical SEO insights into clear priorities and scalable solutions. Build automated monitoring, diagnostics and early-warning systems that identify material technical issues before they become significant commercial problems. Develop internal tools and AI-enabled workflows that improve analysis, quality assurance, prioritisation and execution across technical SEO and the wider SEO organisation. Create reusable technical patterns, guardrails and frameworks across our portfolio, raising technical capability through coaching, documentation and shared tooling. Repeated technical SEO impact at scale - a strong track record of personally leading complex technical SEO initiatives across large websites or multi-domain portfolios, with clear evidence of measurable organic or commercial outcomes. Exceptional technical SEO depth and judgement - expert knowledge across crawling, rendering, indexation, architecture, internal linking, canonicalisation, international SEO, migrations, structured data, JavaScript SEO, log-file analysis and web performance, with the judgement to separate material opportunities from technical hygiene. Commercial and strategic thinking - able to translate complex technical findings into priorities based on expected value, risk, effort, confidence and opportunity cost, connecting technical decisions to organic and commercial outcomes. Principal-level influence - experienced in influencing Product, Engineering and senior stakeholders without direct authority, challenging constructively and communicating complex technical decisions clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences. A builder's mindset - intellectually curious, technically self-sufficient and comfortable remaining deeply hands-on while creating systems, standards and capabilities that increase the impact of teams beyond your own individual contribution.
