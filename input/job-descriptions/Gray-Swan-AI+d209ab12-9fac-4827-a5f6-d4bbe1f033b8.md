About Gray Swan Gray Swan is on a mission to empower the world to use AI safely and securely. We evaluate AI models for the leading frontier labs along with building real-time threat detection and adaptive adversarial red teaming agents for teams deploying AI. We're a team of approximately 50 people, well-funded, growing quickly. Our work directly influences how the world deploys AI agents and systems at scale.. Learn more about how we work . About Arena The Gray Swan Arena is where the world's top frontier labs send their AI models to be stress-tested by our community of over 15,000 red teamers.. AI Researchers, security specialists, prompt engineers, and AI enthusiasts participate in challenges that uncover vulnerabilities, reveal model weaknesses, and generate valuable safety insights. Participants are not only incentivized by recognition and prizes, but for the opportunity to directly influence the future of AI safety and security. We're building the premier destination for AI security talent. We're looking for someone who can help us scale it. The Role We're looking for an Arena Marketing Manager to own user acquisition, engagement, and community growth for Arena. This is not a traditional marketing role. You'll be responsible for growing a highly technical audience of AI red-teamers, security researchers, hackers, builders, and AI safety enthusiasts. You'll design experiments, launch campaigns, build partnerships, manage creators and influencers, activate communities, and develop repeatable growth engines that increase participation across Arena challenges. Success in this role means attracting more qualified participants, increasing challenge engagement, and helping Arena become the default place where top AI security talent gathers. You'll work closely with product, research, partnerships, and leadership teams while having significant autonomy to shape strategy and execution.
