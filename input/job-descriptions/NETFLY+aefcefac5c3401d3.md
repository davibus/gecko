## Company and Opportunity

NETFLY is building a technology-enabled customer-acquisition organization for the personal-injury industry. The company describes the role as an opportunity for a builder—not simply a media buyer—to develop systems, solve difficult acquisition problems, and help create a model competitors will try to replicate.

The listing cites four consecutive Inc. 5000 appearances, Deloitte Technology Fast 500 recognition, Adweek Fastest Growing Agencies recognition, Big Innovation Awards, and other national technology and growth awards. NETFLY emphasizes speed, experimentation, learning, outcome measurement, minimal bureaucracy, and aggressive investment in technology, analytics, engineering, creative, and testing.

## Mission

Own and scale paid social and video acquisition across:

- Meta, required
- YouTube, required
- TikTok, preferred
- Emerging paid-social and video platforms

The objective is to build an exceptionally effective customer-acquisition engine for personal-injury firms, not merely maintain or incrementally improve existing campaigns.
