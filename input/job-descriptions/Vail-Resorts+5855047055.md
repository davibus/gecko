Job Summary: Vail Resorts is reimagining Relationship Marketing from a channel-driven function into a connected Guest Engagement ecosystem that delivers personalized, relevant, and timely communications across every stage of the guest experience with Vail Resort brands and associated products. This role will play a critical leadership role in advancing that transformation. The Senior Manager, Relationship Marketing is responsible for developing and executing CRM strategies that deepen guest relationships, increase loyalty, drive repeat visitation, and support long-term lifetime value. This leader will oversee relationship marketing programs across Email, SMS, In-App, Push, and emerging owned channels while partnering closely with Brand, Analytics, Product, Technology, Resort Marketing, and Operations teams. Beyond campaign execution, this role will help shape the future of guest engagement at Vail Resorts by evolving from promotional messaging toward guest-signal driven journeys, automation, personalization, and real-time engagement experiences that create a seamless relationship across brands, resorts, and business lines.
