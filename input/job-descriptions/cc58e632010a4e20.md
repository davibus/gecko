## About the Role & Company
Mountains West Ranches specializes in selling affordable, owner-financed rural land in Duchesne County, Utah, to a diverse buyer base including tradespeople, truckers, retirees, homesteaders, and outdoor enthusiasts. The company is actively rebuilding its marketing strategy to scale land sales.

The primary success metric shared across the marketing team is **completed land tours per month**.

The Performance Marketing Manager will report directly to ownership and collaborate closely with the Senior Marketing Operations Manager (who handles paid social, HubSpot, and automation stack).

## Key Responsibilities
- **Google Ads / Paid Search:** Build, launch, and optimize paid search campaigns from the ground up. Focus on high-intent search queries, granular keyword match types, rigorous negative keyword discipline, bid management, and high-converting landing pages.
- **Lifecycle & Lead Nurturing:** Manage email and SMS nurture sequences using ActiveCampaign and HubSpot to convert leads into scheduled and completed land tours.
- **Lead Database & Reactivation:** Own and optimize the lead database; design campaigns to reactivate older/cold leads (6+ months) and progress warm pipeline deals.
- **Organic Social Media:** Plan, execute, and maintain a consistent content cadence across both English and Spanish social media channels.
- **Offline & Multi-Channel Marketing:** Coordinate vendor relationships and campaigns across offline channels including radio (scripts, media buying, live mentions) and direct mail (list segmentation, creative direction, response tracking).
- **Performance Analytics & Reporting:** Track and report on core KPIs:
  - Cost per completed land tour (via Google Ads and paid channels)
  - Sales conversions generated from older leads (6+ months)
  - Multi-channel response tracking (call tracking, QR codes, attribution)

## Qualifications & Requirements
- 7–10 years of hands-on marketing experience.
- Deep expertise in Google Ads / SEM (managing actual budgets, intent search, negative keywords, conversion rate optimization).
- Hands-on lifecycle marketing and CRM experience (HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, or similar CRM/automation tools).
- Background or strong understanding in real estate, land marketing, or high-consideration lead generation is strongly preferred.
- Fluency in Spanish is a significant plus (managing Spanish-language social channels and reaching bilingual buyers).
- Strong analytical and performance tracking abilities (Google Analytics, GTM, Looker Studio/dashboards).
