## Why would you love this job?

Redis is the platform developers trust for real-time data—and the platform an increasing share of AI applications are built on. Now, the audience discovering and evaluating Redis is no longer exclusively human. AI agents can identify a need, decide which solution fits, review technical documentation, and create a new account within one continuous session.

The Lead, Growth Marketing owns the Redis digital surface for both agents and humans. This includes the website, SEO, answer engine optimization, generative engine optimization, conversion-rate optimization, and the measurement system that connects discovery to pipeline, account creation, and activation.

Your central challenge is to streamline agent-led growth: making Redis easy for agents to discover, understand, recommend, evaluate, and select while ensuring the same digital experience converts human developers and buyers.

This is a hands-on strategic leadership role for a builder who wants to create a new kind of digital growth system at a high-growth, pre-IPO company. You’ll define the strategy, direct external specialists, contribute to the highest-priority work, measure the outcomes, and continuously improve the system with data.

You should be as comfortable presenting a strategy to executives as you are opening an AI tool and building a working campaign prototype from a blank page in 30 minutes.

## What you’ll do

- 

Own the Redis digital surface. Set the strategy for how agents and humans discover, understand, evaluate, and convert across Redis’s website, documentation pathways, organic presence, and AI surfaces.

- 

Lead agent-driven growth. Build a low-friction journey in which an agent can identify the right Redis solution, review the relevant documentation, and create an account in a single session.

- 

Own SEO, AEO, and GEO. Establish the strategy for search engines, AI answer engines, generative search, assistants, and coding agents so Redis is accurately represented, cited, recommended, and selected.

- 

Own website strategy. Define the platform story, information architecture, page priorities, content pathways, and contextual-conversion model. Partner with web engineering on implementation and feasibility.

- 

Lead CRO and experimentation. Maintain a rigorous testing roadmap across high-intent pages, documentation journeys, sign-up flows, and other critical conversion points.

- 

Build the measurement system. Connect human and agent discovery to meaningful outcomes, including visibility, citations, recommendations, documentation engagement, account creation, conversion, activation, and pipeline.

- 

Turn data into action. Use performance data to improve the strategy, prioritize the roadmap, reallocate resources, and decide what to scale, revise, or stop.

- 

Direct external specialists. Set priorities and scopes of work for contractors and an agency partner, review output, and redirect capacity as business needs change.

- 

Align the digital growth engine. Partner with content, developer relations, product, engineering, paid media, and executive leadership so Redis presents one connected path from discovery to adoption.

## What you’ll need to have

- 

Typically 7+ years in digital growth or GTM marketing, including meaningful experience inside high-growth B2B SaaS, infrastructure, or AI-adjacent companies.

- 

A record of building or materially improving digital growth systems where the strategy and operating model were not already fully defined.

- 

Deep experience owning website strategy, digital conversion, and organic growth—not simply supporting those functions.

- 

Experience developing integrated SEO, AEO, or GEO strategies across traditional search, generative search, AI assistants, answer engines, or coding agents.

- 

An understanding of how content, documentation, citations, authority, structured information, and technical foundations influence AI recommendations and technology selection.

Experience defining measurement for emerging digital journeys, including the ability to connect leading signals to account creation, activation, pipeline, or revenue.

- 

Experience defining email and GTM-orchestration strategy while directing specialists who handle implementation and ongoing operations.

Demonstrated use of AI tools and agents to research, plan, build, analyze, or optimize marketing programs.

Experience directing contractors and agency-staffed specialists, including scoping work, setting priorities, reviewing output, and reallocating capacity.

Strong executive communication and the willingness to move between strategy and hands-on work.
