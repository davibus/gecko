A day in the life: The Paid Advertising Specialist is responsible for supporting the execution and performance of paid advertising campaigns across their book of business, partnering closely with the lead Strategist or independently owning end-to-end strategy for a select group of core clients. The Paid Advertising Specialist is an attentive learner, eager to problem solve, and hungry to grow their technical, strategic, and analytical skills. They have great communication skills and collaborate well with their lead Strategists. They are known for their reliability and attention to detail, resulting in high-quality work delivered on time. They are actively involved in supporting reporting, optimizations, and routine campaign management. They are a self-starter when it comes to their responsibilities and manage their workflow efficiently and effectively. ***Proficiency in spoken and written English at an advanced level is required for this role. Key Responsibilities: Maintain flawless execution across all paid advertising platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, etc.), ensuring accurate setup, tracking, naming conventions, and rigorous QA with zero revenue-impacting errors. Operate with high ownership and autonomy, independently managing timelines, budgets, pacing, and day-to-day performance without the need for reminders or constant oversight. Execute and optimize paid advertising programs end-to-end, including ad builds, optimizations, reporting, pacing, and ongoing performance monitoring. Demonstrate strong strategic thinking, proactively identifying growth opportunities, forming test hypotheses, and recommending data-backed optimizations aligned to client goals. Maintain a client-first mindset, consistently incorporating client objectives, constraints, and brand nuances into executional and strategic decision-making. Show calm, solutions-oriented problem solving, especially during performance volatility, technical issues, or major campaign pivots. Ensure accurate budget pacing and adherence to planned spend, proactively flagging risks or opportunities before issues arise. Own the upkeep and execution of client testing roadmaps, including project management of next steps and prioritization of experiments. Deliver on-time launches and updates for all campaigns and key deliverables. Autonomously prepare and present performance reports and recaps, using clear data storytelling to communicate insights and next steps. Leverage Power Digital’s Nova ecosystem, including Power Circuit and nova Creative Affinity, to inform strategy, execution, and reporting. Add value beyond execution by introducing new ideas, efficiencies, frameworks, or process improvements that enhance team operations or client outcomes. Utilize AI technologies to improve efficiency, performance analysis, and operational workflows where applicable.
