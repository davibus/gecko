## Job Description

ROI CX Solutions is seeking a Head of Marketing to own strategy, execution, and results end to end. The role sets direction, generates ideas, executes directly, and is primarily measured on qualified leads that convert to revenue. The company wants a leader who balances creative and analytical thinking and anticipates changes in buyer discovery through AI assistants and AI-generated search experiences.

## Responsibilities

- Translate revenue goals into channel plans with targets, timelines, and spend allocation; own positioning and messaging and defend investment decisions with performance data.
- Own lead volume, quality, cost per lead, and conversion to revenue across paid, organic, content, email, events, and partnerships; test new sources of demand, scale what works, and stop what does not.
- Own search and AI visibility across technical SEO and high-intent keywords, AEO for snippets and voice results, GEO for presence in AI assistants, and AIO for Google AI Overviews.
- Build measurement for citation share, branded-prompt visibility, and conversions from AI referral traffic.
- Track changes in buyer discovery, including AI agents, and run small experiments on emerging platforms while advising leadership on near-term implications.
- Develop campaign concepts and move quickly from idea to launch; direct website content, landing pages, advertising creative, video, case studies, and thought leadership.
- Own attribution and tracking across analytics, advertising platforms, and CRM systems. Report weekly, monthly, and quarterly on leads, CPL, MQLs, SQLs, pipeline, marketing-sourced revenue, CAC, ROAS, and channel ROI.
- Source, negotiate with, and manage agencies, contractors, and technology vendors against clear service and performance expectations.
- Lead and develop the marketing team, hire for capability gaps, partner with sales on lead quality and handoff, and present performance and strategy to executives.

## Qualifications

- Seven or more years in marketing, including at least three years leading a function or team.
- B2B marketing experience with positive results.
- Demonstrated ownership of lead-generation or pipeline performance with specific results.
- Hands-on depth across paid media, organic search, content, email, and website conversion.
- A practical, tested perspective on SEO, AEO, GEO, and AI Overviews.
- Evidence of early adoption and rigorous evaluation of new channels and tools.
- Strong analytics skills across advertising platforms, CRM reporting, and spreadsheets.
- Proven vendor and agency management against performance standards.
- Budget ownership with ROI accountability.
- Ability to set strategy and personally execute.

## Preferred Experience

- BPO, outsourcing, or contact-center services.
- Multi-touch sales cycles.
- Schema and structured data.
- HubSpot or Salesforce.
- Building a marketing function from an unstructured starting point.

## Benefits

401(k), dental insurance, employee discount, health insurance, life insurance, paid time off, referral program, and vision insurance.
