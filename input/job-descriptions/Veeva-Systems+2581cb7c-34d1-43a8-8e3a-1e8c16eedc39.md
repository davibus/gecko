## Job description

Veeva Systems is a mission-driven organization and pioneer in industry cloud, helping life sciences companies bring therapies to patients faster. As one of the fastest-growing SaaS companies in history, we surpassed $3B in revenue in our last fiscal year with extensive growth potential ahead. At the heart of Veeva are our values: Do the Right Thing, Customer Success, Employee Success, and Speed. We're not just any public company – we made history in 2021 by becoming a public benefit corporation (PBC), legally bound to balancing the interests of customers, employees, society, and investors. As a Work Anywhere company, we support your flexibility to work from home or in the office, so you can thrive in your ideal environment. Join us in transforming the life sciences industry, committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role We are seeking a Marketing Director to lead global marketing for Veeva Safety, our industry-leading pharmacovigilance platform. Partnering closely with strategy and product management, you will develop the positioning, messaging, value propositions, and go-to-market strategy for Veeva Safety. You will translate product vision and innovation, customer needs, and market opportunities into compelling stories that drive awareness, customer adoption, and business growth. You will own the product marketing strategy for Veeva Safety, working across product, strategy, sales, and regional marketing teams to bring new capabilities and innovations to market through product launches, release marketing, sales enablement, customer advocacy, and thought leadership—strengthening Veeva’s leadership in pharmacovigilance. You will shape how the market understands the value of Veeva Safety and help accelerate adoption across the life sciences industry. Success in this role requires a strategic product marketer who combines deep customer and market understanding with exceptional storytelling. You can simplify complex technologies into clear customer value, influence product and go-to-market decisions, and partner across the organization to deliver measurable business impact. You thrive in a collaborative, fast-paced environment and are equally comfortable developing long-term strategy and driving execution.
