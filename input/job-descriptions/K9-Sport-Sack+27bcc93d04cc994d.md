## Job description

We’re a specialty pet products company selling D2C, Amazon, and through wholesale worldwide. We need an ecommerce guru with strong marketing skills to accelerate and manage growth. This is a small tight knit group, we run like a 10 year old start-up.

The Role

This is a hands-on leadership role. You’ll own large portions of digital commerce operation — Shopify D2C, domestic and international marketplaces — setting strategy and executing it. You’ll work cross-functionally with product, logistics, marketing agency and leadership.

What You’ll Own

International channel selling directly or through a partner such as Channel Engine, Rithum, Base.com, or similar — market entry strategy, localized listings, cross-border logistics, and international partner relationships. Amazon Japan and EU, Mercado Libre, Coupang, Rakuten and others.
Oversee our Amazon 1PL partner for US and Candada.
Shopify D2C: store optimization, integrations (QuickBooks, shipping, returns), CRO, landing pages, and overall web strategy.
Brand protection: unauthorized reseller enforcement and MAP policy compliance across all channels
Cross-channel product data and PDP management across all marketplaces and D2C
Product feed management: Google Shopping, Facebook Commerce, Whatnot, TikTok.
Full channel P&L ownership — margin management, promotional ROI, channel profitability and partnership with our marketing agency and product management.
Returns and reverse logistics — reducing return rates and managing policies across channels
Traffic analysis: sessions, conversions, traffic quality assessment, and bot mitigation
Analytics and reporting: performance dashboards, data-driven pricing, inventory, and promotional decisions — tools like Triple Whale, Shopify Growth, GA4

What We’re Looking For Required

3+ years of ecommerce ninja skills with proven revenue growth
Hard goods experience, physical shipped products with logistics. (not SaaS)
Marketplace experience, direct or through multi-channel tools.
Shopify proficiency — store management, plugins, integrations
Strong data analysis — you make decisions with numbers
AI tools for ecommerce — content, analytics, automation, reporting

Highly Valued

Emerging U.S. marketplace experience (TikTok Shop, Etsy, Target, Walmart Marketplace, Whatnot)
CRO, AEO, GEO, and ecommerce marketing (SEO, SEM, email, social, influencer)
Multi-channel order management systems (Rythum, Channel Engine, Base.com)
Tech stack evaluation and vendor selection — assessing platforms, negotiating contracts

Bonus Skills

Wholesale B2B — sales to distributors and wholesale partners via Shopify or similar
MAP enforcement and unauthorized reseller policing internationally

Why K9 Sport?

Own the flow and see your efforts results — no bureaucracy, direct access to leadership, bonuses on success that YOU create.
Lead international expansion from the ground up in a $155B+ pet products market
Small, relaxed group doing big things. We have 85% market share, blue sky everywhere and huge growth potential.
