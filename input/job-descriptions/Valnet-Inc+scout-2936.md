## Job description (tracker description/listing field)

Summary: Valnet is hiring a Senior Performance Marketing Manager to directly manage and scale paid acquisition for one or more assigned products within our digital portfolio. This is a hands on role with day to day ownership of campaign execution, optimization, and scaling, with direct accountability for growth, efficiency, and unit economics. The role is best suited for a performance marketer who operates close to the numbers, understands platform mechanics at a granular level, and is comfortable owning outcomes across spend, revenue, and growth. Responsibilities: - Own paid acquisition strategy and execution across paid search. - Directly manage and optimize campaigns across Google Ads and Microsoft Ads, with exposure to Meta or other platforms as needed. - Own relationships and performance management for lead generation partners and marketplaces Valnet works with. - Manage budgets, forecasts, and performance targets tied to product level unit economics. - Monitor performance at campaign and product level, identifying inefficiencies and scaling opportunities. - Design and execute structured experiments across funnels, landing pages, ad copy, and bidding strategies. - Collaborate with content and SEO teams to plan and prioritize content aligned with product strategy and performance insights. - Work closely with developers to scope and ship acquisition related features such as forms, landing pages, and tracking implementations. Requirements: - 3 to 8 years of hands-on experience in performance or growth marketing with direct ownership of paid media. - Strong experience managing paid search at scale, including Google Ads and Microsoft Ads. - Proven track record managing meaningful budgets with accountability for ROI and efficiency. - Strong analytical capability with fluency in GA4, Looker Studio or similar BI tools, and spreadsheets. - Solid understanding of conversion tracking, attribution models, and data quality tradeoffs. - Experience running CRO initiatives and interpreting landing page and funnel performance. - Comfortable working in fast moving, performance driven environments with minimal process overhead. - Working knowledge of SEO fundamentals and content strategy is an asset. - Experience in insurance, finance, or regulated lead generation verticals is a strong asset.
