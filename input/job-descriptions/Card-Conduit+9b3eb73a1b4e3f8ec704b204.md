## Job description

Headquarters: Salt Lake City, UT URL: https://cardconduit.com/ About the Position We’re looking for a fully remote Digital Marketing Manager to oversee and execute (with creative support) digital ads, email, and social media across both brands. This position will own the planning, implementation, copywriting, channel management, budget allocation, partner relationships, and performance reporting. This role will work very closely with the Marketing & Creative Director and includes direct account management and oversight of external paid media contractors/agencies. Responsibilities Paid Acquisition Plan, launch, and manage campaigns across current platforms (Google, Meta, Reddit) and onboard new platforms (TikTok, Bing) Write copy for ads, landing pages, and other materials Manage budgets across platforms and reallocate based on performance data Oversee contractors and agencies executing paid media Manage relationships with partner brands, platforms, and content creators Evaluate performance and recommend adjustments, renewals, and terminations, as well as identify new opportunities Regularly report on spend, CPA, conversions, partnerships, and trends Email Marketing Build, schedule, and optimize campaigns in ActiveCampaign Manage list segmentation, automations, and lifecycles Test subject lines, creative, timing, and implement findings Social Media Maintain content calendars for Card Conduit and Spellfinder across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and X/Twitter Write, schedule, and publish biweekly content on each channel, coordinating creative with the M&C Director Monitor and respond to community engagement Required Qualifications Ability to work independently and build the proverbial bridges as you cross them 3+ years of digital marketing experience with ownership of paid campaigns/email programs Demonstrated copywriting ability Google and Meta Ads Manager experience Proficiency in ActiveCampaign (or equivalent) Ability to interpret performance data, identify variances, and communicate findings Experience managing brand social channels, including planning and community response Experience managing agency, affiliate, influencer,
