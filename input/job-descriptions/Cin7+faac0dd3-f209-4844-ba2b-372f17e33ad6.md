## Job description

Cin7 is a fast-growing global software company that provides solutions for modern-day product sellers. We help these sellers connect and simplify their operations, keep track of their inventory at scale, and help them sell their products across multiple sales channels around the world. Cin7 is expanding very quickly with a mission to empower product sellers to thrive by making selling as easy as buying. We are committed to this mission to become the leading Inventory Management Software brand in the world. Serving over 8,000 customers globally, processing more than 125 million orders per year, and supporting over $35B of Gross Merchandise Value, Cin7 has been rated best overall in inventory management software in 2024 by Forbes Advisor. Own and scale Cin7's global field and event marketing program across North America, EMEA, and APAC, helping evolve a growing function that drives pipeline, customer engagement, and brand awareness. Lead the strategy and execution of a diverse portfolio of programs including trade shows, industry conferences, roadshows, customer events, partner activations, webinars, and Cin7-hosted experiences. Develop integrated marketing campaigns that maximize the impact of every event, partnering with Demand Generation, Product Marketing, Digital Marketing, Customer Marketing, and Creative to deliver effective pre-event, onsite, and post-event programs. Collaborate cross-functionally with Sales, Product, Customer Success, Partnerships, and senior leadership to align field marketing initiatives with company priorities and go-to-market objectives. Measure and optimize performance , using data and insights to demonstrate ROI, improve future programs, and communicate field marketing's contribution to pipeline and revenue. Manage budgets, agencies, vendors, and event operations to ensure programs are delivered efficiently and to a high standard. Explore and adopt AI-powered tools and emerging marketing technologies to streamline workflows, improve campaign effectiveness, and scale the impact of the field marketing program. 5-7 years of B2B marketing experience, with significant experience in field marketing, event marketing, or integrated campaign management. Experience building or scaling field marketing programs within a SaaS or high-growth technology company. Demonstrated success planning and executing regional events, conferences, customer events, and executive experiences that drive measurable business results. Experience partnering cross-functionally with Sales, Product, Customer Success, and Marketing teams to deliver programs aligned with business objectives. Strategic thinker who is equally comfortable developing long-term plans and rolling up their sleeves to execute. Excellent project management, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence across all levels of the organization. Data-driven mindset with experience measuring campaign performance, event ROI, and using insights to optimize future programs. Curious and adaptable, with a willingness to embrace AI and emerging marketing technologies to improve productivity, campaign performance, and customer experiences. Lead a high-impact field and event marketing function at a growing global SaaS company. Build on a strong foundation while shaping the future of field and event marketing at Cin7. Work closely with a collaborative marketing team and cross-functional partners. Opportunity to make a measurable impact on pipeline, revenue, and brand presence. Competitive benefits include medical, dental, vision, and a 401k company match program. A flexible PTO policy allowing you the time you need to recharge. A diverse team, where everyone helps each other and inclusion is a core value. Remote work flexibility allows you to maintain a work-life balance in a way that suits your needs.
