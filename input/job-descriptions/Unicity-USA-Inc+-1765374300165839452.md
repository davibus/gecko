## Full job description

## About Us

Unicity is a health and wellness company focused on reversing insulin resistance through sustainable habits and products supporting metabolic health. Its products include a yerba mate formulation that supports fasting and a fiber drink designed to slow carbohydrate absorption at mealtime. Customers arrive primarily through affiliates and partners with existing trusted relationships, making the onsite and post-purchase experience a central growth lever.

## About the Role

The Growth Marketing Manager owns day-to-day website performance from the product-page landing through checkout and the customer's first weeks. The role brings analytical rigor to funnel analysis, structured testing, and prioritization, while partnering daily with a designer responsible for visual execution and brand storytelling.

## What You'll Do

- Own the conversion funnel across landing pages, product pages, cart, checkout, and post-purchase experiences; identify where affiliate- and influencer-referred visitors leave and why.
- Build and manage an A/B and multivariate testing program across landing pages, product pages, and checkout flows, prioritizing the roadmap using funnel data and qualitative signals.
- Own the promotion and launch calendar, creating and shipping site pages and experiences for product launches and promotions in partnership with marketing and design.
- Own tracking and weekly reporting for conversion rate, average order value, and app activation, translating results into a visible hypothesis backlog.
- Use reviews, support tickets, onsite surveys, and session recordings to explain customer behavior beyond quantitative funnel data.

## What We're Looking For

- Three to five years of ecommerce, CRO, or growth-marketing experience, ideally with a DTC or subscription brand.
- Working proficiency with GA4 or a comparable web-analytics platform.
- Experience with at least one A/B testing or personalization tool such as Optimizely, VWO, Convert, or similar.
- Enough HTML/CSS knowledge to troubleshoot test implementations independently.
- Data fluency sufficient to build reporting with spreadsheets; SQL is a plus.
- Experience writing clear creative briefs and partnering closely with designers.
- Interest in metabolic health, fasting, or wellness is helpful but not required.

## Nice to Have

- Shopify or comparable ecommerce-platform experience.
- Retention and reactivation optimization experience in addition to first-purchase conversion.
- Familiarity with affiliate- or influencer-driven ecommerce models and their distinct attribution needs.
