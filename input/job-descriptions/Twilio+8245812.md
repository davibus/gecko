About the job This position is needed to scale our partner ecosystem and drive regional pipeline growth through joint co-marketing strategies and through-partner execution. In this role, you will develop data-driven co-marketing plans with strategic partners, build scalable demand-generation programs, and align internal sales teams with external partner leadership. You will serve as the strategic bridge driving co-selling motions, managing operational budgets, enforcing lead governance, and tracking partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline growth across the enterprise cloud and SaaS ecosystem. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: Develop data-driven multi-quarter co-marketing plans and joint GTM strategies with strategic partners to hit regional pipeline targets. Execute targeted account-mapping using platforms like Crossbeam to drive custom 1:1 ABM campaigns for elite partners and scalable digital toolkits for broader partner networks. Co-create compelling "better together" messaging, solution briefs, and branded assets with Product Marketing to showcase joint value propositions. Orchestrate high-velocity field campaigns, executive roundtables, workshops, and sponsorship presence at major industry flagship events to source new logos. Deliver turn-key, through-partner demand-generation programs and drive partner adoption of the Twilio Partner Portal for self-service execution. Equip partner sales and BDR teams with custom outreach playbooks, script templates, and email sequences to ensure rapid campaign follow-up. Enforce strict lead-routing governance rules and follow-up SLAs to minimize channel conflict and optimize lead-to-opportunity conversion. Manage partner marketing operational budgets, ensuring fiscal compliance and optimal ROI across co-marketing investments. Track and report on Partner-Sourced and Partner-Influenced pipeline performance within Salesforce (SFDC) for regular Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs).
