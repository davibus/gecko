About the job We’re looking for a Senior Global Marketing Programs Manager to join the Field and Partner Marketing team at Wiz. In this role, you will report to the Manager of Global Programs & ABM and serve as a strategic leader within the Integrated Marketing organization. You will collaborate directly with cross-functional partners—including Product Marketing, Campaigns, Field Marketing, CXO, Digital, and Sales/BDR teams—to translate Wiz’s product innovation and campaign themes into scalable global programs. Your mission is to bridge high-level strategy with regional execution, designing high-impact initiatives and turnkey frameworks that drive demand, accelerate pipeline attribution, and support executive-level campaign priorities (targeting CISOs and CIOs). Responsibilities Bridge Strategy & Field Execution: Serve as the connective tissue between Product Marketing (PMM), Campaigns, and regional Field Marketing, translating global messaging pillars and product launches into integrated program playbooks. Execute Multi-Channel Demand Programs: Design, launch, and manage scalable global initiatives—such as Meeting Makers, Direct Mailers, Virtual Research Summits, and persona-aligned Webinar series—to drive pipeline growth and target whitespace penetration. Build & Support Executive Campaigns: Partner closely with CXO and Campaign teams to design, build, and deploy strategic programs tailored to executive buyer narratives (CISO, CIO), driving C-suite engagement and high-value pipeline. Deploy Templatized Initiatives for Scale: Build and maintain turnkey "Event-in-a-Box" frameworks and playbooks that empower regional field teams to localize and execute programs with high consistency and minimal friction. Support Customer Expansion: Collaborate on campaigns designed to educate the install base and drive adoption/upsell for key product expansion motions. Measure, Optimize, & Enable: Standardize program reporting and post-campaign assessments. Equip regional Field Marketing and Sales/BDR teams with actionable insights, lead enablement assets, and clear SLAs to maximize pipeline conversion within the 90-day window.
