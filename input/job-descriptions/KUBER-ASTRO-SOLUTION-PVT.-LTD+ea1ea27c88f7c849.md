## Job description

We are looking for an experienced SEO & Digital Marketing Specialist who can take complete ownership of our organic growth strategy. This role requires strong hands-on experience in Technical SEO, Organic SEO, Content Strategy, Analytics, Off-Page SEO and overall Digital Marketing . The ideal candidate should not be limited to keyword rankings. We need someone who understands how SEO contributes to traffic, leads, conversions, brand visibility and business growth . Key Responsibilities Perform complete technical SEO audits and resolve website SEO issues. Manage crawling, indexing, robots.txt, XML sitemaps, canonical tags and redirects. Improve Core Web Vitals, website speed and mobile SEO. Handle structured data/schema implementation and validation. Identify and resolve Google Search Console errors. Conduct advanced keyword research and search-intent analysis. Develop keyword mapping for website pages, products, services, blogs and landing pages. Manage complete On-Page SEO including titles, meta descriptions, headings, URLs, content and internal linking. Build topical authority through content clusters and strategic content planning. Perform competitor SEO and content-gap analysis. Create SEO content briefs and coordinate with writers/designers. Optimize existing content for better rankings, CTR and conversions. Develop ethical and quality-focused Off-Page SEO and backlink strategies. Conduct backlink audits and competitor backlink research. Work on digital PR, outreach and authority-building opportunities. Understand AI Search / Answer Engine Optimization / Generative Search visibility in addition to traditional Google SEO. Monitor Google algorithm changes and continuously improve SEO strategy. Work with Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console and Google Tag Manager . Prepare SEO dashboards and meaningful performance reports. Monitor organic traffic, impressions, clicks, rankings, conversions and leads. Identify opportunities for Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO). Optimize landing pages for both SEO and lead generation. Understand digital marketing funnels and customer journeys. Coordinate SEO with social media, content marketing, email marketing and paid marketing activities. Work closely with developers for technical implementations. Independently research, plan, execute and measure SEO initiatives. Preferred Tools / Skills Google Search Console, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Ahrefs, Semrush, Screaming Frog and other professional SEO tools. Knowledge of WordPress/Laravel or similar CMS/frameworks, HTML/CSS basics, schema markup and website architecture will be an advantage. Who We Are Looking For We need a hands-on SEO professional , not someone who only prepares reports or outsources execution. The candidate should have: Proven professional experience in SEO and organic growth. Strong Technical SEO knowledge. Strong understanding of Google Search and current SEO practices. Experience growing organic traffic for real websites. Ability to independently diagnose SEO problems and recommend solutions. Understanding of content marketing and digital marketing. Analytical and data-driven thinking. Strong communication and reporting skills. Ability to work independently in a completely remote environment. Good task management and ownership. Ability to coordinate remotely with content, design, development and marketing teams. Performance will be evaluated on outcomes such as: Organic visibility, qualified organic traffic, keyword growth, indexing health, technical SEO improvements, content performance, leads, conversions and overall contribution to digital growth. Work Arrangement This is a 100% Remote / Work From Home position . The candidate should have a reliable computer, stable high-speed internet connection and the ability to communicate and collaborate effectively with the team online. Important: Please apply only if you have practical, hands-on SEO experience and can independently manage Technical SEO + Organic SEO + Content SEO + Analytics + broader Digital Marketing activities . Job Types: Full-time, Part-time, Temporary, Contract Work Location: Remote
