## Job description

About the Role We are seeking a proactive and experienced Senior Digital Marketing Specialist to take ownership of a broad range of digital marketing initiatives within the cybersecurity sector. This role is ideal for an autonomous marketer who combines strategic thinking with hands-on execution. You will identify opportunities, solve problems independently, and provide commercially focused recommendations to leadership while driving brand awareness, lead generation, and business growth. You will join a small, agile team where initiative, accountability, adaptability, and measurable outcomes are highly valued. About the Client You will be working with an established Australian cybersecurity business that helps organisations identify, implement, and manage cybersecurity solutions suited to their needs. As the organisation continues to evolve, it is strengthening its digital marketing capability to support brand growth, lead generation, and broader commercial objectives. The environment is fast-paced and collaborative, with a strong emphasis on autonomy, continuous improvement, and the effective use of AI within modern marketing workflows.
