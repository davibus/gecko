## Job description

Ready to own paid media strategy for some of the fastest-growing B2B companies? If you can run a Google Ads account (LinkedIn and Meta, too) with precision, and guide conversations with conviction, composure and confidence, we want to talk to you. We're looking for a Paid Media Strategist who can lead senior client conversations, turn campaign complexity into clear business recommendations, and confidently explain what matters, why it matters, and what should happen next. Our mission at Powered by Search We exist to make B2B companies unstoppable, doing work we love, with people we like, how we want. We are a global, fully remote team across 8+ countries. We have a talented and diverse team that live by our core values: Always be in it to win it. Give a shit. Be obsessed with serving others. Practice a growth mindset. When we fail, don’t lose the lesson. The opportunity As a trusted advisor to fast-growing B2B companies, you'll join a high performing team rated 4.8/5 on Glassdoor, where 95% of team members recommend us. This is a client-facing strategic paid media role. You won't just build campaigns or ship reports. You will lead calls, set the agenda, guide clients toward decisions, and connect every dollar of spend to pipeline and revenue. never launch without it Diagnose performance issues at the root: tracking, structure, bidding, creative, or demand Prioritize by business impact, effort, and implementation reality; QA everything before it ships Required experience 3+ years hands-on B2B paid media, with at least 1 year in B2B SaaS Prior client-facing strategy role where you owned communication, not just execution Track record hitting MQL, SQL, or pipeline targets for B2B clients Direct responsibility for at least $50k in monthly ad spend across multiple accounts Google Ads depth plus at least one of LinkedIn, Meta, or Microsoft Ads Working command of GA4, GTM, and CRM attribution (HubSpot, Salesforce) Written and spoken communication skills strong enough to lead senior calls and defend recommendations under pressure Perks & Benefits: This role is fully remote 5 weeks of paid time off We offer competitive compensation plus quarterly bonuses Flexible working hours (requires 4 overlap hours with 9am - 5pm EST) Paid training (school, events, conferences) *For full-time Canadian residents only - comprehensive health, dental, and eye care This role may not be a fit if You prefer optimizing accounts without leading the client relationship You need someone else to turn your analysis into client-ready recommendations You lose the thread when a senior stakeholder challenges your numbers on a live call You report platform metrics (CTR, CPC, impressions) as results rather than business outcomes You prefer being handed tasks rather than owning outcomes, priorities, and follow-through
