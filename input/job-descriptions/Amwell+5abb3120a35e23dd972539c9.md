Brief Overview This role produces member-facing content across Amwell's service lines and is accountable for how that content performs once it reaches members. Content is a shared capability across the Engagement pillar: the account owners brief and approve, and this role produces at the volume and quality the book requires. The role covers content strategy across service lines, the Engagement Hub content library, and the production of case studies and enablement assets that the account owners design. It also directs contractor and AI-assisted capacity, which is how volume is met with a single full-time position. Content engagement is a shared measure with the execution side, which owns timing and deliverability Core Responsibilities What the role owns: • Content strategy and production across service lines, the engagement showcase, and content libraries • Inputting email content into the email CMS (Knak) — copy and image selection • The Engagement Hub content library and its maintenance • Case-study and enablement-asset production, against designs set by the account owners • AI-assisted drafting and direction of contractor capacity for volume, within the approved content guidelines • Brand-voice consistency across member-facing content How success is measured: • Content engagement across member-facing email and hub content, as the content contribution to open and click performance (primary measurable output) • Content throughput and turnaround against briefs • Brand-voice and quality consistency across the library • Coverage of the service lines against the content plan What the role
