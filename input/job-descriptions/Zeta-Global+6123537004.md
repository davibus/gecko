WHO WE ARE Zeta Global (NYSE: ZETA) is the AI-Powered Marketing Cloud that leverages advanced artificial intelligence (AI) and trillions of consumer signals to make it easier for marketers to acquire, grow, and retain customers more efficiently. Through the Zeta Marketing Platform (ZMP), our vision is to make sophisticated marketing simple by unifying identity, intelligence, and omnichannel activation into a single platform – powered by one of the industry’s largest proprietary databases and AI. Our enterprise customers across multiple verticals are empowered to personalize experiences with consumers at an individual level across every channel, delivering better results for marketing programs. Zeta was founded in 2007 by David A. Steinberg and John Sculley and is headquartered in New York City with offices around the world. To learn more, go to www.zetaglobal.com . The Role We’re looking for a strategic, data-driven Director of Paid Search to support a Higher Education division of our search team. You’ll be responsible for shaping paid search strategy across EDU clients, ensuring best-in-class execution, and driving measurable business outcomes. This role requires a balance of strategic vision and hands-on management. You’ll be responsible for guiding client strategy, overseeing performance, and ensuring flawless execution of paid search campaigns from end to end. Client Strategy & Leadership Translate client business goals into full-funnel search strategies across Google and Microsoft Ads Lead account planning including budget allocation, forecasting, audience strategy, and testing roadmaps Identify performance opportunities and risks, proactively recommending optimizations and growth strategies Serve as a strategic lead in client conversations, QBRs, and performance reviews Performance Ownership Oversee end-to-end execution of paid search campaigns, including account setup, budget pacing, optimization, and reporting. Accountable for enrollment performance outcomes and KPIs such as CPL, CPE, CPS, and overall efficiency Establish testing frameworks and innovation pipelines to continuously improve results Oversee account structure, bidding strategies, audience layering, and automation approaches Ensure consistent use of measurement best practices, attribution models, and conversion tracking Drive adoption of new betas, AI-driven features, and automation tools Analysis & Reporting Partner with analytics and ops teams to improve reporting, automation, and scalability Monitor industry trends, platform changes, and competitive landscape Deliver clear, data-driven performance reports and insights to clients, diving into the “why” behind the data Recommend testing strategies, budget allocation shifts, and bid strategy adjustments based on performance trends Ensure accuracy, consistency, and timeliness of reporting Qualifications 8-12+ years of relevant paid search experience Hands-on experience managing monthly budgets of $500k+ Experience optimizing to full funnel outcomes for EDU clients, such as CPE and CPS Preferred but not required Skai experience Google Ads Certifications is a plus BENEFITS & PERKS Unlimited PTO Excellent medical, dental, and vision coverage Employee Equity Employee Discounts, Virtual Wellness Classes, and Pet Insurance And more!!
