About Eleanor Health Eleanor Health is rethinking addiction by treating it as a chronic condition and building an integrated care model that expands access to effective, whole-person treatment to drive sustained recovery. Our goal is to improve upon earlier generations of opioid and substance use disorder treatment to create more accessible clinic- and community-based care. Eleanor Health and its associated health care providers deliver best-in-class substance use disorder care utilizing Medication-Assisted Therapy (MAT), behavioral health therapy, care management, and social support and wrap services. As a multi-site, outpatient treatment organization, Eleanor delivers accessible, evidence-based, and excellent clinical care, supported by innovative technology. If you are passionate about growing your career in a business that makes a difference, we seek highly skilled, motivated, and compassionate individuals who take responsibility and adapt quickly to change to join our deeply committed and collaborative team. About the Role Every month, thousands of people decide they are ready to do something about their substance use. They search, they scroll, they stream, they click, they call, they fill out a form. This role exists to make sure that as many of them as possible find us in more places than a search results page, and that it costs less to reach them each quarter. This is a paid media role, and it is not a paid search role. Google Ads and Microsoft Ads are our largest channels today, and a dedicated specialist runs them day to day. This role exists to build and scale everything around that foundation: affiliate, programmatic and streaming audio, Meta, and Reddit. We are hiring for depth in those channels, along with the website experimentation that turns more traffic into more members. ## Where we are We have built a real foundation over the past two years. Paid cost per new member has been reduced by more than half. Conversion tracking is clean and includes offline conversion data, so campaigns optimize toward people who actually enter care rather than toward form fills. Campaign structure has been rebuilt, landing page experience has improved substantially, and bid strategy and targeting have moved from manual management to value-based approaches. This is a performance marketing function with good bones, clear measurement, and meaningful results already behind it. ## Where we are going Three priorities define the next phase of the work. - First — Paid channel diversification. Paid search carries too much of the load today. We need to build and prove affiliate, programmatic and streaming audio, Meta, and Reddit, and to test others worth testing, so growth does not depend on a single auction that gets more expensive as we scale. Each new channel earns its budget by proving member-level economics, not lead volume. - Second — Testing rigor. Our experimentation program is young, and there is substantial gain available from running it properly: structured A/B testing on landing pages and creative, and disciplined testing of newer bid strategies and campaign types, including AI Max. - Third — Website conversion. The website is the front door for every channel. Landing pages, forms, and the online self-scheduling experience decide how much of the traffic we buy turns into a lead, and every point of lift improves every channel at once. ## The channels The channels in scope are Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, affiliate, programmatic and streaming audio, and Meta and Reddit. A paid search specialist runs the day-to-day on Google and Microsoft. This role leads the rest, builds the strategy across all five, and decides where each dollar goes. ## How the funnel is shared Marketing does not own the entire path on its own. This role owns the path from first impression to a submitted lead or call: the channels, the landing pages, the forms, and the online self-scheduling experience. From there, our Access team is the first human point of contact for every prospective member who reaches out, by phone, by web form, or through online self-scheduling. They handle insurance verification, appointment scheduling, pre-visit support, and follow-up when someone misses an appointment. The Access team reports separately to the Chief Marketing Officer, alongside this role rather than within it. The lifecycle communications that carry a prospective member from inquiry to first visit, including confirmations, reminders, pre-visit content, and no-show recovery, are owned by the Organic & AI Acquisition team, which also owns the content behind them. This role partners with that team and with Access, because paid cost per new member depends on what happens after the lead. A standing intake funnel review brings all three together. ## What this role is accountable for The primary measure is paid cost per new member, and it is deliberately end-to-end: spend divided by members who complete a first visit, not by leads generated. It does not improve by producing more form fills. It improves when more of the people we reach actually enter care. Alongside it sit website-visitor-to-lead conversion, owned outright by this role, and the mix of paid acquisition itself: the share of members coming from channels beyond paid search, and each new channel's cost against a defined target before it earns more budget. Lead-to-member conversion is owned by Access and the lifecycle function, and blended cost per acquisition across all channels, paid and non-paid together, sits with the Chief Marketing Officer. What You'll Do Paid Media Strategy & Performance - Own paid cost per new member as a quarterly target, understanding that monthly variance in this category is largely seasonal rather than performance-driven - Own budget pacing and allocation across all five channels, shifting investment toward what is working - Lead paid channel diversification: build, test, and scale affiliate, programmatic and streaming audio, Meta, and Reddit, with a defined budget, success criteria, and scale-or-stop decision for each - Be hands-on in the newer channels: build campaigns, manage partners and platforms, and optimize against member-level outcomes rather than platform-reported leads - Manage programmatic and streaming audio partners as an informed client, including planning, creative requirements, measurement, and incrementality - Direct the paid search specialist on Google and Microsoft: set priorities, review work, and stay close enough to the accounts to audit the work independently - Design and run structured tests of newer bid strategies and campaign types, including AI Max, with clear success criteria defined before launch - Plan for seasonality: demand and cost per click vary substantially by month in addiction treatment, and the annual plan should reflect that rather than assume a flat target - Maintain offline conversion tracking and value-based bidding wherever a channel supports it, so campaigns optimize toward members rather than leads - Ensure all paid activity meets LegitScript certification and platform policy requirements for addiction treatment advertising.
