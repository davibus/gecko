## Job description

Anrok is the leading tax automation platform enabling businesses to expand globally without compliance complexity. As the digital economy has grown 6x over the last decade, software businesses have gone from not worrying about sales tax to needing to monitor exposure, calculate rates, and file returns across 50+ US jurisdictions and many countries worldwide. This creates a critical bottleneck for companies that should be able to transact with customers everywhere. Anrok eliminates this complexity by connecting with billing and payment systems to automate tax monitoring, calculations, and filing end-to-end. Our unified platform handles the ever-changing maze of tax laws at municipal, state, and federal levels—so companies can focus on growth, not compliance. Our customers include: 40% of Forbes Top 50 AI companies 20% of Forbes Top 100 Cloud companies Top companies like Notion, Anthropic, and Cursor We're making compliant digital commerce a reality for companies big and small, backed by over $100M from leading investors including Sequoia, Index, Spark Capital, and Khosla Ventures. We are looking for a Paid Marketing Manager to own paid acquisition as a core growth lever for the business. You'll run our paid search and paid social programs end to end across Google, LinkedIn, Meta, and TikTok. This is a hands-on role with key support in place. You will manage our paid agency with dedicated campaign management, analytics, and creative support. In this role, you will Own paid search end to end, including agency management, account structure, keyword strategy, bidding, ad copy, and landing page tests. Own paid social with LinkedIn as the anchor channel, building and optimizing campaigns across LinkedIn, Meta, and TikTok. Manage paid media budget and pacing across channels, making weekly allocation calls based on CAC and pipeline contribution. Run a consistent testing cadence across creative, copy, audiences, and bidding, with clear hypotheses behind each test. Partner with marketing ops, lifecycle, sales, and analytics to connect ad spend to pipeline and closed revenue. Own reporting on paid performance, giving leadership a clear read on what's working and what to do next. Own paid brand investments. This includes industry publications, podcasts, and out-of-home. Build the systems and processes that let paid marketing scale as budget grows. What excites us 4-6+ years of B2B SaaS marketing experience with a hands-on focus on paid acquisition.
