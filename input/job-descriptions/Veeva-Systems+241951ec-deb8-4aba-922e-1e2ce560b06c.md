Veeva Systems is a mission-driven organization and pioneer in industry cloud, helping life sciences companies bring therapies to patients faster. At the heart of Veeva are our values: Do the Right Thing, Customer Success, Employee Success, and Speed. Join us in transforming the life sciences industry, committed to making a positive impact on its customers, employees, and communities. The Role We are seeking a Marketing Director to lead integrated and field marketing across North America for Development Cloud, our industry-leading platform for clinical, regulatory, and safety. Partnering closely with strategy, sales, product marketing, and regional marketing teams, you will develop and execute integrated marketing strategies that build brand awareness, generate pipeline, accelerate customer engagement, and support business growth. You will own the integrated and field marketing strategy for North America, leading the planning and execution of multi-channel marketing programs across digital campaigns, account-based marketing, field events, executive programs, user conferences, webinars, customer advocacy, and sales enablement. Working across global and regional teams, you will ensure marketing programs are aligned, measurable, and optimized to deliver business impact while strengthening Veeva’s leadership in drug development and across clinical, safety, and regulatory. Success in this role requires a strategic marketer with exceptional leadership, collaboration, and execution skills. You can translate business priorities into integrated marketing programs, align cross-functional stakeholders around shared goals, and use data and customer insights to optimize performance and improve outcomes. You thrive in a fast-paced, matrixed environment and are equally comfortable setting strategy, leading cross-functional initiatives, and driving execution to achieve measurable business results. Develop and drive integrated marketing strategy for Development Cloud across North America, aligning marketing initiatives with business objectives, growth priorities, and market opportunities. Partner with strategy, sales, product marketing, and regional teams to develop integrated campaigns that drive awareness, pipeline generation, customer engagement, and adoption. Lead the planning and execution of integrated, multi-channel marketing campaigns across digital, account-based marketing, field events, executive programs, webinars, customer advocacy, and sales enablement. Develop annual integrated marketing plans in partnership with the Europe marketing team, aligning campaign strategy, sharing best practices, and enabling regional execution while supporting global / regional business priorities. Partner with sales to strengthen customer engagement through executive programs, customer events, industry conferences, account-based marketing, and customer advocacy initiatives. Collaborate across corporate marketing, product marketing, digital, communications, creative, and regional marketing teams to ensure coordinated execution of integrated campaigns and consistent customer experiences. Measure marketing performance, evaluate business impact, and continuously optimize programs to improve awareness, pipeline, customer engagement, and adoption. Lead cross-functional marketing initiatives, manage multiple priorities, and ensure programs are delivered on time and aligned with business goals. 7+ years of progressive experience in integrated marketing, field marketing, demand generation, or go-to-market roles within B2B enterprise software or cloud-based solutions, including experience leading multi-channel marketing programs across global and regional teams in fast-paced, matrixed organizations. Integrated & Field Marketing Leadership: Proven success developing and executing integrated marketing strategies that drive pipeline, customer engagement, and business impact. Experience planning, executing, and measuring end-to-end marketing programs, including demand generation, customer retention and education campaigns, field events, user conferences, industry events and trade shows, webinars, digital campaigns, and PR in partnership with corporate and regional teams. Cross-functional Partnership: Proven ability to build trusted relationships and collaborate effectively with senior leaders across product, sales, strategy, corporate marketing, and regional field teams to align priorities, influence decisions, and deliver measurable business outcomes. Global-first Mindset with Regional Execution: Experience leading global marketing initiatives and partnering with regional teams to adapt messaging, content, and programs while maintaining a consistent global strategy and driving regional business goals. Team Leadership & Player-Coach Capability: Experience leading, mentoring, or managing marketing teams while remaining hands-on.
