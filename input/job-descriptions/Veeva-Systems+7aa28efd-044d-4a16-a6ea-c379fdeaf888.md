The Role The Services Engagement Manager Lead (SEM) is responsible for setting our customers up for success and representing the entire value proposition and effort behind a Veeva eCOA Implementations. This is a critical role in ensuring successful customer engagements during the scoping and planning phase to establish the right strategy for implementation success. The SEM is an integral part of the sales cycle, acting as a solution expert working in close alignment with the Veeva Sales, Strategy, and Services Teams. Align closely with services and sales teams to provide implementation proposals and establish confidence with prospects in our ability to deliver Develop and spearhead eCOA service offerings across multiple customer types Set client and internal stakeholder expectations correctly with a focus on long-term success Develop, own, and manage partner vendor relationships for contracting and services operations Lead, grow, and scale eCOA SEM activities and operations Travel estimate: Less than 25% 5+ years experience in delivering consulting services or involvement in selling services for Clinical Data Applications, including eCOA, with a proven track record 3+ years of experience scoping/defining project plans, resource models, and negotiation of Statements of Work for software implementation projects Understanding of the data capture for the clinical trials domain, including how EDC works with IVRS, safety systems, CTMS and downstream databases, and eCOA Ability and comfort to spearhead complex services contracting independently but in close collaboration with key internal stakeholders. Familiarity in working with the Life Sciences Industry Ability to engage audiences of varying perception levels, including working with executive leadership.
