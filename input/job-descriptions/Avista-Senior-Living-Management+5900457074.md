The role leads digital strategy and lead generation across Avista communities. It owns CRM strategy in HubSpot, builds and runs targeted campaigns that drive qualified inquiries and move-ins, and partners closely with community sales teams to turn leads into residents. The ideal candidate is data-driven, hands-on, and understands how to market senior living to adult children and older adults.

Responsibilities include planning, launching, and optimizing digital lead-generation campaigns; managing and growing HubSpot CRM workflows, nurture sequences, segmentation, and reporting; overseeing SEO strategy for each community and the Avista brand; directing social-media marketing; guiding creation of marketing materials and digital assets; tracking campaign performance and lead-to-move-in conversion; reporting results to leadership; and partnering with community sales directors and operations leaders to align marketing with occupancy goals.

Qualifications include a required bachelor's degree, preferably in marketing or a related field; hands-on HubSpot CRM experience; a proven record running digital lead-generation campaigns; working knowledge of SEO management and social-media marketing; exposure to Canva, Adobe Creative Suite, or similar design tools; and strong analytical skills. Senior-living marketing experience is strongly preferred.
