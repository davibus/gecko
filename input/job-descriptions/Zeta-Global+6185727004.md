## Job description

WHO WE ARE Zeta Global (NYSE: ZETA) is the AI-Powered Marketing Cloud that leverages advanced artificial intelligence (AI) and trillions of consumer signals to make it easier for marketers to acquire, grow, and retain customers more efficiently. Through the Zeta Marketing Platform (ZMP), our vision is to make sophisticated marketing simple by unifying identity, intelligence, and omnichannel activation into a single platform – powered by one of the industry’s largest proprietary databases and AI. Our enterprise customers across multiple verticals are empowered to personalize experiences with consumers at an individual level across every channel, delivering better results for marketing programs. Zeta was founded in 2007 by David A. Steinberg and John Sculley and is headquartered in New York City with offices around the world. To learn more, go to www.zetaglobal.com . THE ROLE We are seeking a Paid Search Manager who can confidently lead the day-to-day delivery of assigned accounts, set weekly priorities, and ensure campaign performance and pacing are aligned to client KPIs. You will own the translation of client goals into paid search channel plans, including budget allocation, targeting approach, measurement planning, and testing roadmaps. The role combines hands-on platform expertise, client communication, and performance analysis. You will establish and enforce quality standards for launches, tracking validation, and reporting, and coordinate work across internal and external partners. The ideal candidate can turn complex performance data into clear business narratives and structured optimization plans, while proactively identifying opportunities to improve account performance and client outcomes. Depending on your location, this role could be hybrid (NYC, Nashville, LA, San Francisco, Miami) or remote. RESPONSIBILITIES Client Strategy Translate client goals into paid search channel plans, including budgets, targeting approach, measurement plans, and testing roadmaps. Identify broader opportunities across campaign types, automation, audience strategy, and creative or landing-page learnings, and move them into action. Campaign Management & Execution Lead the day-to-day delivery of assigned accounts by setting weekly priorities, managing pacing, and ensuring campaign delivery is aligned to KPIs. Actively manage campaigns using Google Ads, Google Ads Editor, Microsoft Ads, and SA360, while maintaining strong account structure and platform hygiene. Establish and enforce QA standards for campaign launches, tracking validation, and reporting integrity. Drive structured optimization plans, including budget shifts, bidding adjustments, testing priorities, and documented learnings. Team Collaboration Collaborate with internal teams to ensure campaign execution aligns with client objectives. Manage day to day tasks and responsibilities utilizing the team’s project management platform. Analysis & Reporting Deliver weekly performance narratives, monthly and quarterly business recaps, and clear recommendations that connect platform performance to client objectives. Translate performance data into actionable insights, articulate the drivers behind results, and proactively surface risks and opportunities. Maintain accurate, consistent, and timely reporting, with clear ownership of validation, pacing, and delivery status across assigned accounts. QUALIFICATIONS 4+ years of relevant paid search experience Hands-on experience managing large monthly campaign budgets and complex paid search account structures Demonstrated ability to lead account delivery, manage pacing, and translate performance data into client-ready insights and recommendations Experience with Google Ads, Google Ads Editor, and campaign launch, QA, and tracking-validation processes Preferred but not required: Experience with Google Shopping, Demand Gen, and Performance Max Experience with Microsoft Ads, SA360, and Google Analytics BENEFITS & PERKS Unlimited PTO Excellent medical, dental, and vision coverage Employee Equity and Stock Purchase Plan Employee Discounts, Virtual Wellness Classes, and Pet Insurance And more!! SALARY RANGE The salary range for this role is $80,000.00 - $90,000.00, depending on location and experience.
