Come join us! About the Role: We are seeking an experienced lifecycle marketer to build and scale a new function accelerating customer growth for our consumption-based offerings. This role is ideal for a leader skilled in driving usage and engagement within an API-first environment. You will define the strategy, operating model, and experimentation framework to boost feature adoption, expand consumption, and drive measurable revenue. This role focuses on leveraging product and behavioral data, customer insights, and cross-functional collaboration to guide enterprise customers through feature adoption and expansion, creating personalized, scalable approaches for each integration. You will partner with Marketing teams—including Product Marketing, Customer Marketing, Customer Education, and ABM—alongside Customer Success, Sales, Product, and Revenue Operations to establish a marketing model that accelerates time-to-value, increases consumption, and fuels long-term customer growth. This is a unique opportunity to shape how marketing contributes to the success of AlphaSense’s next-generation of products. What You’ll Do: Build a New Marketing Motion Define the vision, strategy, operating model, and roadmap for Customer Lifecycle Marketing focused on usage and consumption-based offerings Establish repeatable and scalable processes, experimentation frameworks and success metrics Build the foundation for a high performing team as the function grows Drive Product Usage and Consumption Design digital-first programs that accelerate time-to-value, encourage deeper and more frequent product utilization, and facilitate coordinated orchestration with Customer Success teams Develop and implement lifecycle strategies that increase customer usage of AlphaSense’s offerings, with a focus on our API solutions Partner with cross-functional teams to leverage product data, customer health signals and behavioral insights to build enterprise lifecycle programs that increase long-term customer value Measure Business Impact Develop executive reporting and optimization strategies focused on metrics such as: Consumption-based revenue Product utilization Time-to-value Engagement with key capabilities Continuously optimize and consistently update programs based on measurable business impact and product innovation, ensuring relevance and accuracy Foster strong working relationships and a culture of accountability and shared ownership.
