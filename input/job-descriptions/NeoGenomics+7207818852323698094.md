Position Summary : The Marketing Analytics Manager is responsible for providing Marketing with a clear, trustworthy view of performance by connecting marketing activities, spend , and sales outcomes into actionable insights. This role oversees marketing reporting, attribution, and data quality, ensuring teams have reliable information to measure effectiveness and make informed decisions. By connecting campaign, channel, and Salesforce pipeline data, the Marketing Analytics Manager helps quantify marketing’s impact, optimize investment decisions, and build the foundation for advanced marketing mix and ROI measurement. trust in the data Build and maintain dashboards connecting marketing spend through to leads, MQL and SQL status, and pipeline contribution, giving every team visibility into their own performance Translate what the data shows into concrete recommendations, where to invest, where to pull back, not just reporting numbers without a point of view Partner with the Director, Marketing Operations and
