About the job We are seeking a Global Partner Marketing Programs Manager to lead our With and Through go-to-market strategy across our global partner ecosystem (GSIs, MSPs, Channel, Tech Alliances, and CSPs). In this role, you will serve as a principal pipeline driver for global partner marketing by architecting and scaling high-impact Global Pipeline Plays, Joint Account Activation campaigns, and executive engagement platforms—including Partner Advisory Boards and C-level roundtables—across our partner ecosystem. By engineering repeatable, programmatic GTM models that regional teams can seamlessly adapt, you will directly accelerate account activation, expand global co-sell revenue, and maximize overall partner marketing ROI, moving beyond tactical event execution. Responsibilities Joint Demand Generation & Global Pipeline Plays: Own the development and rollout of global With/Through-Partner programs including GTM kits and demand-generation campaigns that partners can easily execute to drive Wiz pipeline. Cross-Functional Solution Messaging: Partner with Product Marketing, Field Global Programs, and Partner Sales to build compelling joint solution messaging, campaign/event-in-a-box kits, and customer-facing assessments/workshops that partners and partner marketers can execute. GTM Play Optimization: Continuously optimize GTM plays by tracking partner usage, partner pipeline metrics, and ROI to scale high-performing programs globally. Account Activation & ABM Co-Marketing: Lead Account Activation strategies in close alignment with Regional Partner Marketers and Partner Managers, leveraging partner relationships to unlock target accounts. Targeted Account Campaigns: Design and deploy targeted ABM programs, joint executive briefings, and account-mapped campaigns that drive and accelerate pipeline. Process Standardization: Standardize programs (kits, campaigns) to ensure a consistent, sophisticated "Wiz" brand experience. Operational Excellence: Build scalable repeatable processes to ensure a predictable demand gen engine.
