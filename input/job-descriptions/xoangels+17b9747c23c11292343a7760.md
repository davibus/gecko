## Job description

Headquarters: Schwyz, Switzerland URL: http://xo-angels.com We're XO Angels, a creator management agency. As a Growth Manager, you'll own the short-form strategy of 2-4 creators end to end: scripts, shot lists, feedback to creators and editors, and constant testing until the numbers move. Your only real goal: views that turn into real reach. What you'll actually do Analyse viral short-form content, understand why it worked, and adapt it for our creators. Write scripts and shot lists that your creators then shoot. Give them precise feedback on what worked, what didn't, and what needs to change. Direct editors and posters. You own the output, they execute it. Analyse every post: why did this hit, why did that flop, what do we double down on this week. Test bold ideas fast, kill what doesn't work, scale what does. Who we're looking for This is not a 9-to-5. When a Reel goes viral on a Sunday night, you're on it. When an account gets banned at 10pm on a weekday, you handle it. Weekends are normally off, but when there's an emergency you step in, no discussion. If that puts you off, this role isn't for you. We want: Obsession with short-form. You watch TikTok and Reels for hours voluntarily and can explain why a video works. You're not learning this, you already live it. Ownership mindset. You treat your creators' accounts like your own business. No excuses, no "the editor didn't deliver", you make it happen. Analytical eye. You can look at 20 videos and tell us what pattern connects the winners. Fluent English. Non-negotiable. Bonus, not required: experience growing IG or TikTok accounts, agency or marketing background. We care more about drive and taste than about your CV. What you get 3,000 USD base + performance bonuses. Realistic total 3,500-7,000 USD/month depending on results. 100% remote. Work from wherever. Direct impact. Small team, no bureaucracy. Your ideas ship this week, not next quarter. Clear path up. If you really push, you can become Head of Marketing within 12-18 months and lead your own team.
