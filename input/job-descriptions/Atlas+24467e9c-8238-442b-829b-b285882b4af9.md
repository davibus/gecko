## Job description

About Atlas At Atlas, we believe the financial system should work for everyone. We’re building a modern rewards credit card and banking platform designed to grow with our members - helping them build credit on autopilot with 0% APR, no late fees, and premium rewards. By replacing harsh penalties with empathy and smart technology, we are making stress-free financial progress accessible to millions. We are a product- and technology-obsessed team with roots at Facebook, Amazon, Robinhood, and Uber. If you’re ready to build a high-impact product that democratizes opportunity and accelerates financial empowerment, join us. About the Role Atlas is hiring a performance-first Growth Marketer to own paid media acquisition end-to-end. This role is for someone who lives in the data you build the media strategy, launch and manage spend across channels, and relentlessly optimize toward CAC and ROAS targets. You think in testing frameworks and scaling systems, not one-off campaigns. This is a remote-friendly role. campaigns Build a testing roadmap for audiences, placements, and campaign structures Forecast and own monthly media budgets against CAC/ROAS targets 📊 Campaign Execution Launch and manage campaigns in Meta / TikTok / Google Ads Manager Structure campaigns (CBO/ABO, bid strategies, scaling frameworks) Monitor performance daily and optimize based on CPA, CTR, CVR, CPM, ROAS Kill underperforming
