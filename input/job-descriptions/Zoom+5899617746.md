## Job description

Zoom is hiring Product-Led Growth marketing leads to own organic growth for priority AI products. This individual-contributor role sits at the intersection of product marketing, organic growth, and AI-assisted content creation. The manager will help users discover and adopt Zoom productivity products through content, SEO and AEO, social distribution, and conversion optimization. The role has no paid activation budget and reports within the CEO organization. It is open to remote candidates in the United States and hybrid candidates in San Jose or Seattle.

**Responsibilities**

- Develop a deep working knowledge of Zoom productivity products, their users, the problems they solve, competing alternatives, and meaningful differentiators.
- Translate product positioning into clear, outcome-oriented messages for product-led-growth audiences.
- Own content from concept through publication and measurement, including demos, tutorials, comparisons, social posts, email, and web copy.
- Use AI tools for research, scripting, demo creation, editing, and iteration while maintaining quality.
- Build a repeatable acquisition and distribution engine that improves organic discovery, landing-page conversion, and completed sign-ups.
- Optimize landing pages and sign-up flows and run conversion-rate experiments.
- Execute SEO and AEO content programs to increase qualified organic traffic.
- Create platform-native content for X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Reddit, TikTok, and Instagram Reels while following Zoom brand standards.
- Measure engagement, trial, subscription, and adoption signals and use the results to improve content and calls to action.
- Coordinate with Online Marketing on email and in-product activation and partner with product managers, designers, engineers, and marketing teams.

**Qualifications**

- Eight or more years of product-led growth experience in product marketing, content marketing, growth marketing, or social content for digital products such as SaaS, AI tools, productivity platforms, or creator products.
- Strong product judgment and the ability to turn user problems and genuine differentiators into simple, credible product stories.
- Practical fluency with AI tools for research, scripting, demo creation, video editing, and content iteration, demonstrated through completed work.
- A record of personally creating and publishing product videos, screen-recorded demos, and social content for digital products.
- Strong understanding of how audiences, formats, and distribution differ across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube, X, Reddit, and LinkedIn, with demonstrated depth on multiple platforms.
- Experience building a content channel, product launch, or distribution system from the ground up and advancing it through ambiguity.
- Comfort with rapid experimentation, unconventional tests, learning from unsuccessful approaches, and iterating without established playbooks.
- Experience at an AI-native, SaaS, productivity, or creator-product company is preferred but not required when the candidate's work demonstrates the needed judgment and execution.
