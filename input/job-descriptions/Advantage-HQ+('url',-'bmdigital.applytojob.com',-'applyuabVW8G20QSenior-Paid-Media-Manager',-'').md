The Role

You'll own paid media end to end for a set of DTC and e-commerce accounts: onboarding a new client, building their accounts from scratch, and running live campaigns against revenue targets. You're the person the client speaks to about their spend. You explain what's working, what isn't, and what you're doing about it.

This is a hands-on seat, not an oversight one. You run the accounts yourself.

What You Own

Onboarding a new client

- Lead the kickoff: budgets, targets (CPA/ROAS), audiences, and reporting cadence
- Audit existing ad accounts, tracking setup, and creative libraries before anything goes live
- Agree what good looks like in month one, three, and six

Setting up

- Configure ad accounts, pixels, CAPI, and conversion tracking
- Build the account structure: campaigns, ad sets, audiences, budget splits
- Brief creative and feed requirements to the studio team

Running the account

- Launch, monitor, and optimise daily against KPIs
- Manage budget pacing and move spend where it performs
- Run creative, audience, and bidding tests, then scale what works
- Own the weekly and monthly client reporting and walk clients through it
- Flag risks early. If a target is slipping, the client hears it from you first, with a plan

What You Need

- 3+ years buying paid media, hands-on, across Meta and Google (TikTok a plus)
- A track record in DTC or e-commerce where you owned ROAS or CPA and can talk through specific accounts
- Comfortable running client calls solo, including the difficult ones about performance
- Strong on tracking and attribution: GA4, pixel, CAPI, and reading the data behind a result
- Able to run several accounts at once, start to finish, and keep each one on plan

Nice to Have

- Agency background running multiple clients
- Feed management and Google Shopping experience
- A creative testing framework you've actually used
