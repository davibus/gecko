## Job description

POSITION SUMMARY We are seeking a highly technical Digital Measurement & Tagging Specialist to lead the end-to-end implementation of our tracking ecosystem. You will be responsible for ensuring that every marketing dollar is accounted for by maintaining a seamless data flow between our websites, Google Tag Manager , and our various advertising platforms including Google, Meta, and Microsoft . ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES The following duties are normal for this position. This list is not to be construed as exclusive or all inclusive. Other duties may be required and assigned. 1. Google Tag Manager & Infrastructure Architecture & Deployment: Manage the full implementation of Google Tag Manager (GTM) containers, including complex Triggers & Variables to capture granular user data. Server-Side Implementation: Lead the transition to and maintenance of Server-Side Tag Manager , improving data privacy, security, and website performance. Quality Assurance: Execute rigorous Preview & Debugging processes to ensure tags fire correctly without impacting site speed or user experience. 2. Analytics & Reporting Ecosystem GA4 Mastery: Drive Google Analytics 4 setups and migrations, ensuring data continuity and accuracy across all web properties. Search Integration: Configure Google Search Console and manage Search Console API integrations to sync organic performance data with GA4 reporting . UTM Governance: Standardize UTM Tagging protocols across all paid and organic channels to ensure clean attribution. 3. Conversion Tracking & Paid Media Support Action Optimization: Define and setup conversion actions that align with business KPIs, including deep-funnel events and lead form tracking . Platform Syncing: Manage the importing of GA4 conversion actions into Google Ads to optimize bidding strategies and ROI. Multi-Channel Tracking: Oversee measurement for Google Ads and Microsoft Ads , ensuring pixels and conversion scripts are firing accurately for both platforms. 4. Testing & Technical Management End-to-End Testing: Conduct comprehensive conversion tracking form testing to validate that leads are being captured in both the CRM and advertising platforms. API Management: Utilize Google Search Console API Management to automate data exports, custom reporting dashboards, user management and property activation. 5 . Data Visualization & API Management Looker Studio: Create comprehensive dashboards utilizing data blending and real-time data access to visualize performance. Search Console API: Manage Google Search Console API connections for advanced SEO data extraction and reporting QUALIFICATIONS To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily.
