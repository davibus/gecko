The Role: Ambience Healthcare is looking for a Senior Manager, Growth Marketing to own the execution and measurement of our account-based marketing (ABM) motion. You'll sit within Commercial Marketing, reporting to the Head of Commercial Marketing, and partner closely with Sales to turn a prioritized list of target hospital and health-system accounts into concrete, measured marketing plays. This is a fully remote role — any time zone. What You'll Do: Design and launch account-based marketing plays. Turn Sales' prioritized target-account list into specific, sequenced campaigns tailored to each account's context and blockers. Own measurement and reporting. Apply Ambience's account-based measurement framework to real data pulled from our CRM and other systems, grade what worked, and report results that shape the next round of plays. Partner directly with Sales on shared accounts. Work the same target accounts as the sales team, surfacing and resolving discrepancies between marketing's and sales' view of an account before they become blockers. Coordinate cross-functionally to activate plays. Work with Events, Customer Marketing, and Product Marketing to get the right messaging and content to the right accounts through the right channels. Build a repeatable ABM operating rhythm. Establish a durable cadence for proposing, running, measuring, and iterating on account-based plays so the function scales beyond any single campaign. Who You Are: An experienced B2B marketer. 6–8+ years of marketing experience with direct exposure to a sales-aligned or account-based motion. Healthcare-tested. You've worked directly with hospitals and health systems — payer-only or life-sciences-only backgrounds don't meet this bar. Hands-on with CRM/martech. You're comfortable working inside a CRM or marketing platform (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pardot, or similar) to pull and act on your own data. Measurement-minded. You're skeptical of activity metrics and know how to separate real signal — account or pipeline movement — from noise. Comfortable with ambiguity. You propose next steps rather than waiting for direction.
