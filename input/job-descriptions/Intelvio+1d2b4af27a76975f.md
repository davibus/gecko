## Job description

Marketing Automation / Email Specialist, HubSpot Location: Remote Employment Type: Full time Reports To: Marketing Operations Manager About the Team Intelvio's marketing team is being built into a high performing, innovative, and collaborative organization capable of driving significant growth over the next one to three years. Email and marketing automation are core engines of that growth, and we're looking for someone who can turn strategy and content into well built, well measured programs inside HubSpot. Role Summary The Marketing Automation / Email Specialist owns the day to day building, execution, and optimization of email and automation programs in HubSpot. This person turns campaign briefs and content from Product Marketing and Creative into segmented, automated, on brand programs, and continuously tests and improves performance. This is a hands on, builder focused role for someone who genuinely enjoys the craft of email and automation. Key Responsibilities Build, QA, and deploy email campaigns and automated nurture workflows in HubSpot. Design and maintain list segmentation, lifecycle stages, and lead scoring logic in partnership with the Marketing Operations Manager. Build landing pages and forms in HubSpot to support campaigns and lead capture. Run an ongoing testing program (subject lines, send times, content, calls to action) and turn results into recommendations. Monitor deliverability, list health, and sender reputation, and take corrective action when metrics slip. Partner with Product Marketing and Creative on email content, design, and calendar planning. Report on email and automation performance (opens, clicks, conversions, unsubscribes) and surface insights to the broader team. Keep email and data practices compliant with CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and other applicable regulations. Serve as the team's day to day HubSpot expert, helping other team members use the platform effectively. Qualifications 3 to 5 years of hands on experience building email and automation programs, with substantial experience in HubSpot Marketing Hub specifically. Strong understanding of email marketing best practices, deliverability fundamentals, and list hygiene. Experience building multi step automated workflows and lifecycle or lead scoring logic. Comfortable with basic HTML and CSS for email template edits. Analytical mindset with the ability to read campaign data and turn it into clear next steps. Strong attention to detail and pride in clean, well tested execution. Nice to Have HubSpot certification(s), such as Email Marketing or Marketing Automation. Experience working across multiple brands or business units within one HubSpot portal. Familiarity with CRM platforms such as Salesforce and how they connect to marketing automation.
