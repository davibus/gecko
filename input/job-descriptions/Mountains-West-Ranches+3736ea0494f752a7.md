## Job description

Who We Are Mountains West Ranches sells land in Duchesne County, Utah, to people who want to hunt, camp, build, or pass it down. The company owns its inventory, finances its buyers, and is rebuilding its marketing engine. What You'll Own - Google Ads: build and run paid search from the ground up, with tight match types, negative-keyword discipline, and conversion-focused landing pages. - Lifecycle marketing: run email and SMS nurture in ActiveCampaign and HubSpot, including cold-lead reactivation and deal retention. - Organic social: maintain a planned content cadence across English and Spanish accounts. - Radio: coordinate station buys, scripts, mentions, and tracked call performance. - Direct mail: manage targeting, creative, Postalytics production, and tracking. How You'll Be Measured - Cost per completed land tour from Google Ads, progressing to cost per funded sale. - Sales from leads six or more months old. - Tracked responses per offline campaign. - Shared team metric: completed land tours per month. Who We're Looking For - 7-10 years of marketing experience; real estate or land marketing strongly preferred. - Detailed, hands-on Google Ads results from real accounts and budgets. - Lifecycle/CRM experience with HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, or similar. - Familiarity with rural and outdoor buyers. - Spanish fluency is a plus. Compensation and Details - $75,000-$90,000 base depending on experience. - Annual performance bonus. - Full-time hybrid position in Heber City, Utah. - Paid time off. - Five years of marketing experience required.
