The Marketing Manager leads the development and execution of marketing strategies for Evergreen's utility clients. The role builds relationships with internal and external clients, provides strategic marketing guidance, develops plans, manages budgets, and reports on performance.

The company seeks a strategic, client-focused marketer who can turn program goals, audience insights, and performance data into effective multi-channel campaigns. The role calls for reliability, attention to detail, responsiveness, clear communication, strategic thinking, client service, independent priority management, creativity, sound judgment, curiosity, and a willingness to learn energy-efficiency concepts.

Responsibilities and qualifications include developing client-specific marketing strategies that support program goals; owning projects, anticipating needs, driving solutions, and following through; presenting concepts to clients; collaborating with cross-functional teams; and using Microsoft Office at an intermediate to advanced level. Familiarity with Adobe, digital media, analytics, marketing automation, CRM, and project-management tools is preferred, as is a strong background in digital marketing, audience insights, consumer analytics, and campaign-performance measurement.

Candidates should have a bachelor's degree with major coursework in marketing or a related field and seven to ten or more years of marketing and client-management experience. A graduate degree and experience in energy efficiency or another technical industry are preferred. The position is fully remote, and candidates must reside in Washington, Oregon, Wyoming, Idaho, or Utah.
