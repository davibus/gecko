## Job description

Built By Shah | Remote | Full-Time Compensation: $7,000–$10,000/month, based on experience About Built By Shah Built By Shah is a growing marketing and technology company built specifically for auto body shops. We help body shop owners generate and convert more opportunities using Google Ads, custom landing pages, call tracking and recording, lead capture, customer follow-up, photo collection, appointment scheduling, CRM tools, and automated nurture systems. We are looking for a highly resourceful Growth Marketing Manager whose primary responsibility is simple: Help us as an agency generate more qualified leads (body shops to sign up) every month. This is not a traditional management position where you delegate marketing tasks to a large team. This is a highly hands-on execution role working directly alongside the founders. You will be responsible for identifying new ways to generate interest, executing those ideas, building systems around what works, nurturing existing leads, and reporting measurable results. What You’ll Be Responsible ForLead Generation & Growth Your primary focus will be increasing the number of qualified body shop owners entering our sales pipeline. You will constantly research, test, and execute new lead-generation opportunities, including: Cold email campaigns Targeted prospect lists Facebook and LinkedIn communities Industry groups and forums Direct mail campaigns Industry conferences and trade shows Strategic partnerships Long-term lead nurture campaigns Database reactivation Other creative acquisition channels you identify You should naturally be the type of person who sees a goal like “generate 20 more qualified leads next month” and immediately begins researching different ways to make it happen. You will not simply present ideas to the founders. You will be expected to execute them. If we decide to send direct mail to 2,000 body shops, you will help determine the messaging, coordinate the creative, work with the printing vendor, prepare the mailing list, launch the campaign, and track the results. If we identify a valuable industry conference, you will research the event, contact the organizers, determine sponsorship opportunities, gather pricing and information, and bring the opportunity to the founders when a decision is required. CRM, Lead Nurture & Automation You will own much of what happens after a marketing lead enters our database. Responsibilities include: Building and managing long-term email nurture campaigns Creating CRM workflows and automations Segmenting leads based on behavior and qualification Re-engaging older opportunities Testing email subject lines, messaging, CTAs, timing, and formats Monitoring lead movement through the funnel Identifying where prospects are dropping off Creating systems that reduce repetitive manual work Direct GoHighLevel experience is a major plus. However, candidates with strong experience using platforms such as HubSpot, Salesforce, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo, Marketo, or similar CRM/marketing automation systems will also be considered. The existing role concept specifically calls for automated nurture, CRM pipeline architecture, lead tagging and systematic follow-up. Marketing Creative & Consumer Psychology You do not need to be a professional graphic designer, but you must have a strong marketing eye. We want someone who understands why people respond differently to different messages, offers, layouts, colors, headlines and calls to action. You should be able to look at two advertisements and explain why one may capture more attention or create more interest than the other. You may be responsible for: Developing concepts for Meta static advertisements Creating or coordinating Canva graphics Writing advertising headlines and hooks Developing email messaging Creating direct-mail concepts Researching successful advertising angles Coordinating designers when necessary You should understand that changing a headline, word, number, image, font size, CTA, or email format can materially change how a prospect interprets the message. Strong direct-response marketing instincts are extremely valuable for this position. Paid Advertising — Important Distinction You will not be primarily responsible for managing our Google Ads or Meta Ads accounts. The founders currently oversee paid advertising but its a plus if you have the knowledge to. Your responsibility will include closely monitoring the leads generated from those campaigns and reporting: Number of leads generated Qualified vs. unqualified leads Lead source Appointments generated Conversion rates Trends in lead quality Changes compared with previous periods Your reporting will help the founders make better decisions regarding paid advertising. Reporting & Accountability This is a highly measurable position. We do not want marketing activity simply for the sake of being busy. You should be able to clearly show what you worked on, what you launched, and what results it produced. Daily You may review and report: Total new leads Lead source Qualified leads Unqualified leads Appointments generated Lead quality issues Important changes or trends The original role also specifically calls for monitoring open rates, click-through rates, conversion rates, A/B tests and funnel drop-off points. Your Most Important KPI The ultimate measurement of this position is: Are we consistently generating more qualified sales opportunities? We want someone who can help move BBS from: 30 qualified leads → 40 → 50 → 60+ and can clearly explain where that growth came from. Activity matters, but results matter more.
