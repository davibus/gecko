<li>Actively test and analyze keywords, advertising copy, landing pages, campaign types and positioning to drive results in line with client's goals</li>
<li>Communicate performance results and strategy recommendations to clients clearly &amp; secure buy-in from decision-makers</li>
<ul>
<li>Create slides with good design &amp; strong storytelling for clients</li>
</ul>
<li>Create quarterly business reviews &amp; other client-facing presentations that outline performance, insights from testing, and key strategic opportunities</li>
<li>Delegate work to junior team members &amp; provide clear direction &amp; feedback when needed</li>
<li>Stay current on SEM industry trends, contribute to internal agency conversations around SEM (including but not limited to monthly department meetings, trainings, case studies, etc.)</li>
</ul>
