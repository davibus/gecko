What you’ll do The Brex Marketing team is looking for an experienced Senior Marketing Operations Manager to architect and optimize our B2B sales-led and channel-driven GTM engine. This role will define and maintain the systems, processes, and operational rigor that align Marketing, SDR, Sales, and Partner teams. We are looking for someone who is eager to design the future-state GTM tech stack—modernizing how leads flow, how insights are generated, and how operational workloads become more efficient through automation, AI, and agentic workflows. This person will champion operational excellence by improving lead management, automating revenue processes, increasing funnel velocity, and enabling more efficient cross-functional alignment. Where you’ll work This role will be based in our Salt Lake City office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work!
