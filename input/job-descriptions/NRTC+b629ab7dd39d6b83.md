## Company and Agency

NRTC is a member-driven, technology-focused cooperative with more than 800 employees across eight offices. It provides integrated broadband, managed services, smart-grid, mobile, and video solutions that help approximately 1,500 electric and telephone members bring evolving technology to rural America.

Pivot, an NRTC company, is a marketing and customer-experience agency serving organizations doing meaningful work in their communities. Its practice areas include broadband marketing, broadband customer experience, broadband vendors, and nonprofits.

## Job Summary

The Digital Marketing Specialist develops, executes, and optimizes campaigns across paid search, social media, and emerging digital channels. The role combines strategy, creative content development, and data analysis to grow brand awareness, strengthen engagement, deliver measurable business results, and provide an exceptional client experience.

## Responsibilities

- Research, develop, execute, optimize, and analyze paid social and search campaigns across multiple accounts and platforms, including Google Ads, Meta Ads, Nextdoor Ads, MNTN Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, and other digital advertising platforms.
- Set up, monitor, and analyze conversion tracking using Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics.
- Develop and evaluate B2B and B2C social-media and search-marketing strategies based on objectives, market characteristics, and cost factors.
- Build and maintain Pivot's and clients' organic social presence through strategy, content creation, and publication.
- Conduct social-media and search-engine-marketing audits and provide strategic recommendations.
- Maintain knowledge of emerging digital trends and their effect on social and search marketing.
- Develop expertise in generative search optimization and AI-powered marketing tools, including how platforms such as ChatGPT affect search behavior and content strategy.
- Develop educational resources for the Pivot team.
- Participate in creative brainstorming and campaign/client strategy development.
- Perform other assigned duties.

## Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities

- Strong understanding of Facebook, Twitter/X, Instagram, Pinterest, Snapchat, TikTok, Nextdoor, and YouTube, including platform strengths and audience demographics.
- Ability to navigate both the creative and analytical sides of social content development and publication.
- Foundational knowledge of SEO audits, optimization, and reporting is preferred.
- Experience serving multiple clients simultaneously and building strong client relationships.
- Strong understanding of customer and market dynamics.
- Excellent grammar, professional writing, accuracy, attention to detail, communication, and cross-department collaboration.
- Ability to communicate clearly, persuade clients to try new approaches, and sell ideas.
- Nonprofit experience is a plus.

## Minimum Education and Experience

- Bachelor's degree in marketing, advertising, communications, public relations, business, digital media, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and relevant experience.
- At least two years of professional paid-social and/or Google Ads experience.
