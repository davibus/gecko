**About Neuroscale**

Neuroscale AI is building an AI Recruiting Operating System - one unified platform for the talent organization, from first touch through evaluation and hire. Our flagship platform, Arbi, helps companies discover, engage, screen, and evaluate talent at scale using AI-native intelligence, automation, and data. We have invested deeply in the product. Now we are building the repeatable growth engine that brings it to market.

**About the Role**

We are hiring a Founding Performance Marketing Lead to own performance and growth marketing from the ground up. This is a hands-on individual contributor role for someone who wants to build the machine - not inherit one.

You will own the path from first impression to click to conversion to qualified pipeline to closed ARR. That includes paid acquisition, creative testing, landing pages, attribution, funnel instrumentation, conversion-rate optimization, audience discovery, and early product-led growth experiments.

You will work directly with Neuroscale's founders and partner closely with Product, Design, Sales, and Revenue Operations. You will have the autonomy to test quickly, scale what works, stop what does not, and turn learning into repeatable playbooks.

**What You'll Own**

1\. Build and scale the paid acquisition engine

Launch, manage, and optimize acquisition across LinkedIn, Meta, Google, retargeting, and emerging channels. Personally manage meaningful media spend and make channel decisions based on pipeline quality, acquisition economics, and revenue - not vanity metrics.

2\. Turn creative into a growth system

Build a high-velocity testing engine across short-form video, UGC, founder-led creative, static ads, customer proof, use-case creative, case studies, and landing-page concepts. Develop a repeatable process for creative briefs, production, testing, learning, and iteration.

3\. Own the conversion funnel

Build and continuously improve the journey from ad to landing page to demo or trial to activation. Create and ship landing pages without waiting on a large web team, and work with Product and Design to remove friction from high-intent conversion paths.

4\. Make measurement and attribution decision-grade

Own campaign tracking, UTMs, pixels, events, conversion APIs, CRM campaign logic, attribution models, dashboards, and funnel reporting. Create a clear view of what is producing qualified pipeline, what is wasting spend, and where conversion is breaking down.

5\. Run a disciplined experimentation program

Design and execute continuous A/B and multivariate tests across hooks, creative, copy, audiences, offers, landing pages, CTAs, forms, demo flows, and channel mix. Maintain an experiment backlog and use data to decide what to scale, iterate, or kill.

6\. Discover and codify our best audiences

Identify the ICP signals, personas, use cases, pain points, messages, offers, and targeting strategies that consistently produce high-quality opportunities. Turn those findings into reusable audience and messaging playbooks for Marketing and Sales.

7\. Help shape product-led growth

Partner with Product and Revenue on activation, self-serve conversion, trial-to-paid pathways, in-product prompts, lifecycle nudges, and other early PLG loops that can complement the sales-led motion.

8\. Automate for leverage

Use AI, no-code/low-code tools, enrichment, workflow automation, and light scripting to increase testing velocity and reduce repetitive work. Build a marketing operating system that can test 20 thoughtful ideas where a traditional team might test two.

**The Numbers You'll Care About**

1. Qualified Pipeline

2. CAC / CAC Payback

3. CPL / Cost per Qualified Lead

4. ARR Sourced / Influenced

5. Conversion Rate

6. LTV / CAC

7. Pipeline Velocity

8. Experiment Win Rate


Clicks, CTR, engagement, and reach matter only when they help explain the economics of acquiring customers and creating revenue.

**Who We're Looking For**

- 4-8+ years of hands-on performance marketing, growth marketing, or demand generation experience, ideally in B2B SaaS, AI, HR Tech, recruiting technology, or another high-growth technology category.

- A track record of personally managing and scaling approximately $50K-$250K+ per month in paid media across LinkedIn, Meta, Google, or comparable channels - and translating that spend into qualified pipeline and revenue.

- Deep hands-on experience inside ad platforms. You can build campaigns, audiences, conversion tracking, creative tests, budgets, bids, and optimization logic yourself.

- Demonstrated ability to connect channel spend to downstream funnel performance, opportunity creation, pipeline, and ARR.

- Strong command of funnel economics, including CPL, CAC, LTV, CAC payback, conversion rates, pipeline velocity, and marginal acquisition efficiency.

- Experience building and improving landing pages, forms, demo flows, or trial experiences with a strong CRO mindset.

- Strong creative instincts and copy judgment. You can diagnose why a message or creative concept is working - not merely report that CTR changed.

- Experience testing video, UGC, creator-led, founder-led, or short-form paid creative.

- Comfort with analytics, dashboards, CRM data, experimentation, attribution, and imperfect early-stage datasets.

- Ability to move quickly from hypothesis to launch to data to insight to the next test.

- Strong written communication and the ability to translate customer pain into clear, high-converting messaging.

- A builder mentality: high ownership, low ego, strong judgment, and a preference for creating the playbook rather than waiting for one.


**Bonus Points If You Have**

- Built performance marketing from near-zero at an early-stage or high-growth B2B SaaS company.

- Scaled one or more paid channels into a predictable source of qualified pipeline while maintaining disciplined acquisition economics.

- Experience in HR Tech, recruiting technology, AI, developer tools, enterprise SaaS, or another complex B2B category.

- Hands-on experience with platforms such as HubSpot or Salesforce; Webflow or Framer; GA4; Segment; Mixpanel or Amplitude; Clay; Zapier or Make; and modern attribution or ad-tracking tools.

- Built lightweight automations, scripts, dashboards, or AI-assisted workflows yourself.

- Worked with Product teams on PLG, free-trial, self-service, lifecycle, or activation experiments.

- Used AI tools systematically to accelerate research, creative production, analysis, personalization, and experimentation.


**You'll Thrive Here If**

- You want meaningful ownership and are energized by a blank sheet of paper.

- You enjoy being in the campaigns, creative, landing pages, dashboards, and funnel - not only managing vendors.

- You are comfortable being accountable for qualified pipeline and revenue outcomes.

- You prefer fast, instrumented experiments over long planning cycles.

- You can balance speed with analytical rigor and know when the data is strong enough to act.

- You are resourceful and can ship with a small team while building systems that scale.


**What Success Looks Like**

- A repeatable paid acquisition engine is producing qualified demand at increasingly predictable economics.

- Campaign-to-CRM attribution provides a credible view from spend through opportunity, pipeline, and revenue.

- Creative and landing-page experiments are shipping every week with documented learnings.

- Neuroscale has a clear picture of its highest-converting ICPs, personas, messages, offers, and channel combinations.

- Landing-page, demo/trial, and funnel conversion rates are improving through systematic experimentation.

- CAC and payback are understood by channel and audience, with clear thresholds for scaling spend.

- A measurable content and creative engine supports paid acquisition rather than operating as a separate activity stream.

- Marketing, Product, Sales, and Revenue Operations share a common funnel definition and growth scorecard.

- A documented growth playbook compounds as spend, headcount, and market coverage increase.
