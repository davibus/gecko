Sr. Manager, Marketing and Brand Strategy Job Summary The David Eccles School of Business is seeking an experienced and collaborative Senior Manager, Marketing and Brand Strategy to serve as a strategic marketing partner for assigned programs, departments, centers, institutes, and school-wide initiatives. Reporting to the Senior Director, Brand Strategy, Marketing, and Communications, this role develops and leads integrated marketing strategies that advance school priorities, strengthen the Eccles brand, and engage key audiences. The Senior Manager will build trusted relationships with internal partners, translate business and program objectives into effective marketing strategies, and lead initiatives from planning through multi-channel execution and measurement. The ideal candidate is a strategic marketer who combines strong business acumen with hands-on execution. They will bring experience developing audience-focused marketing strategies, shaping messaging and content, collaborating with creative and channel partners, and using performance insights to continually improve results. Success in this role requires strong strategic thinking, communication, writing, relationship management, and organizational skills, as well as the ability to navigate a complex organization, manage multiple priorities, influence across teams, and turn strategy into high-quality, measurable marketing outcomes.
