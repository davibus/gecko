## Full job description

Wpromote is hiring a remote U.S. Paid Search Manager to directly manage SEM campaigns across Google Ads and Microsoft Ads for a diverse portfolio of clients. The individual contributor owns account activity, develops campaign strategy, performs daily optimization, and collaborates with managers across other marketing channels.

### Responsibilities

- Plan, develop, and implement paid-search strategies focused on direct response and new-user acquisition.
- Work with client teams to understand business needs, campaign objectives, and opportunities to expand relationships and investment.
- Create and manage accounts and campaigns across Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, Criteo, and other platforms.
- Manage Search, Shopping, Display, Remarketing, App Install, and Video advertising.
- Use direct-response strategies to drive revenue or qualified leads.
- Conduct keyword research, write ad copy, and establish bidding strategies tied to client KPIs.
- Design and implement A/B experiments to identify better campaign settings.
- Use Google Analytics to develop insights and actions that improve paid-search results.
- Analyze campaign performance and adjust strategy and budgets accordingly.
- Communicate regularly with clients about goals and results.
- Develop cross-channel strategies that improve CPA, ROAS, and overall efficiency.

### Required qualifications

- Approximately 1.5–3 years managing paid-search campaigns for multiple client portfolios within an advertising agency.
- Excellent verbal, written, analytical, organizational, and expectation-management skills.
- Deep SEM and paid-search advertising knowledge.
- Experience with keyword-research and web-analytics tools, including Google Analytics.
- General knowledge of SEO, social advertising, and web usability.
- Strong Excel skills, including pivot tables, VLOOKUP, and chart building.
- Ability to balance multiple clients, timelines, fire drills, and competing priorities.
- Understanding of customer lifetime value and profit optimization based on client margins.
- Experience presenting to senior client contacts.
- Agency experience managing multiple clients with monthly budgets of $50,000–$500,000 per client preferred.
- Google Workspace experience.

### Preferred qualification

- Hands-on use of AI and automation tools inside and outside paid-search platforms to improve results.
