Job Summary: The Director of Marketing Performance Analytics is responsible for leading the enterprise analytics capabilities that drive measurable improvements in marketing performance across Vail Resorts' guest journey. This leader serves as a strategic partner to Marketing, Go-to-Market, Digital Experience, CRM, Paid Media, Organic, Ecommerce, and Executive Leadership, helping the organization make smarter, faster, and more impactful marketing decisions. The team is responsible for four core capabilities: Marketing Reporting & Measurement Marketing Insights & Performance Analytics Advanced Analytics & Measurement Science Enterprise Experimentation This role combines strategic leadership with technical credibility—building a high-performing analytics organization while partnering across the business to identify opportunities, influence investment decisions, and accelerate growth. Success in this role requires equal strength in business strategy, marketing performance, organizational leadership, and analytical thinking. Job Specifications: Starting Wage: $150,000 - $185,000 + annual bonus + equity Employment Type: Year Round Shift Type: Full Time hours available Minimum Age: At least 18 years of age Housing Availability: No Job Responsibilities: This is not a traditional reporting or business intelligence leadership role.
