This is a hands-on role at the center of how Allure turns marketing investment into pipeline. You'll own our paid programs on Google, Bing, and LinkedIn, build and run our email campaigns in HubSpot, and design the nurture programs that turn content engagement into sales conversations. The strategy, the execution, and the numbers all live in this seat. You'll report to the Head of Demand Generation and work closely with our sales and BDR teams. This is an individual contributor role with broad scope and a direct line to revenue.

### About Allure Security Talent

Account Takeover Fraud costs businesses nearly 7 Billion a year.

We address the root cause.

Our AI catches the scams that other vendors miss by visually examining millions of sites every day to detect online impersonation of your brand. Our solution finds credential theft (phishing), identity theft, payment data theft, personal information theft, and content re-publishing; and responds to mitigate the risk before your customers and your brand become victims.

Our unique deception technology takes the fight back to the scammers. We stop them in their tracks by injecting realistic decoy data right into their attack sites. That breaks the scammers’ business model and helps to expose their real identities. Allure also provides best-in-class traditional responses via automated takedowns and blocklisting and a through our seasoned takedown team.

### Description

## **What You'll Do**

## **Own paid search and paid social**

Run our Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, and LinkedIn campaigns end to end: account structure, keywords, bidding, audiences, ad copy, and conversion tracking. These channels need regular, hands-on attention, and keeping that rhythm is a core part of this job. You'll manage the budget, test relentlessly, and drive awareness and pipeline more efficiently every month.

## **Build and run email campaigns and nurture programs**

Take email programs from idea to send: new asset launches, post-event follow-ups, and topical nurture tracks triggered by what people engage with (someone downloads our retail fraud guide, a short retail-specific sequence kicks off to help them raise their hand). You'll shape the touches, sharpen the copy, and build the campaigns yourself, including segmentation, workflows, emails, forms, and landing pages. You'll help build the narratives and stories that connect our product to the people we serve, measure what converts, and bring your own ideas for what we should build next.

## **Own the goals and the reporting**

Set channel-level targets, own them publicly, and report on performance against pipeline, not just clicks. You'll build the reporting rhythm that tells us what's working, what isn't, and where the next dollar should go.
