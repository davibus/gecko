We are building that future today. *Proficiency in spoken and written English at an advanced level is required for this role A day in the life: The Paid Advertising Senior Performance Analyst is responsible for owning the execution, performance, and growth of their marketing service within their book of business. They service the most complex, enterprise-level accounts and are highly regarded for their mastery over paid advertising. In addition to their account responsibilities, they are actively involved in developing the paid advertising team through training, escalation support, auditing existing accounts, and upleveling the product. The Senior Performance Analyst supports leadership in owning and improving one core pillar of the department’s business. The Senior Performance Analyst is exceptional at working under pressure and navigating probing client questions, difficult conversations, roadblocks, and account issues. In addition to their paid advertising knowledge, the Senior Performance Analyst has an understanding of cross-channel strategy, how their service impacts the broader media mix, and general business acumen. Key Responsibilities: Lead paid advertising strategy and performance marketing for key accounts, ensuring strong execution and achievement of client goals. Demonstrate expert-level proficiency in at least two major advertising channels (Google/Bing, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube, Snapchat, etc.). Serve as the primary escalation point for key accounts, resolving roadblocks and driving performance. Apply advanced cross-channel and technical expertise (attribution, incrementality, advanced measurement) to deliver impactful client results. Oversee high-quality campaign execution, including thorough QA to prevent revenue-impacting errors. Ensure accurate budget pacing and advise on budget changes. Lead proactive strategy recommendations across assigned channels, surfacing opportunities and risks ahead of client requests. Maintain client testing roadmaps and project manage next steps, ensuring a consistent, ongoing cadence of experimentation. Escalate performance risks early, distinguishing normal fluctuation from genuine account risk requiring intervention. Maintain excellent communication with clients and internal teams, leading strategic conversations and performance discussions. Mentor and elevate team talent through coaching, training, and support; identify department knowledge gaps and partner with leadership to implement solutions. Conduct training sessions on platforms, tools, industry trends, and best practices to strengthen team capability. Collaborate on new business pitches and audits, contributing strategic insights and industry perspective. Demonstrate full knowledge of the Power Circuit, executing on all recommendations from the solutions roadmap. Leverage AI technologies to optimize workflows, insights, and performance; model best practices for team adoption.
