Role Overview: As a Technical Product Marketing Manager at Defense Unicorns, you will help drive the growth, adoption, and market understanding of UDS and related product features. You will report to the Head of Product Marketing and work closely with Product, Engineering, Growth, and other cross-functional teams to bring product capabilities to market and help customers understand how UDS solves critical software delivery and platform challenges. This is an execution-focused role with meaningful ownership across product marketing programs. You will contribute to positioning and messaging, develop technical content and demonstrations, support Growth teams, execute feature launches, and help improve how UDS is packaged, adopted, and communicated. You will build deep product expertise and translate complex technical capabilities into clear, differentiated customer value for defense and national security audiences. Responsibilities: The listed responsibilities are not exhaustive, and additional responsibilities may be assigned based on the evolving needs of the organization. We are seeking a dynamic individual who can operate effectively amid ambiguity, establish structure for a new market offering, and take on new responsibilities as the capability and organization evolve. Develop and execute end-to-end go-to-market strategies for new products and features Define target audiences, positioning, messaging, and product narratives Support product naming, launch planning, adoption, and post-launch optimization Establish the go-to-market foundation for products entering new or undefined markets Establish deep technical expertise across the Defense Unicorns product portfolio Translate complex technical concepts into clear and differentiated customer value Create solution briefs, white papers, technical blogs, and web content Develop presentations, launch materials, videos, demos, and use-case narratives Partner with Product and Engineering to develop repeatable technical demonstrations Conduct market research and competitive analysis Translate market intelligence into positioning, messaging, and go-to-market recommendations Use qualitative and quantitative insights to improve marketing execution Serve as a product evangelist for Defense Unicorns products and philosophies Communicate the value of Defense Unicorns capabilities within the defense industry Provide recommendations that improve products, services, and market adoption Operate effectively amid ambiguity and establish structure for new market offerings Travel Expectation: Approximately 35%–45% travel may be required to engage with customers, communities, partners, and industry stakeholders.
