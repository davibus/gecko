## Full job description

A digital marketing company is seeking a Senior Digital Marketing Analyst for its digital channel team. The freelance role focuses on developing and executing effective SEO strategies for a range of clients. The employer emphasizes demonstrated SEO results, dedication, creative and resourceful thinking, reliability, and awareness of changing SEO needs.

### Duties

- Develop and execute comprehensive SEO strategies.
- Create new SEO programs and monitor and refine existing programs against company objectives, strategies, and forecasts.
- Lead content marketing and project management work.
- Manage multiple clients across multiple services.
- Work with cross-functional teams including sales, content marketing, and social media.
- Create digital marketing strategy aligned with the available budget.
- Develop and execute campaign strategy and tactics to achieve sales objectives.
- Apply critical thinking and creative solutions to drive traffic and conversion.

### Experience and qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in marketing or a related field.
- At least three years of proven SEO, internet marketing, and project management experience.
- Strong analytics, ROI, and problem-solving orientation.
- Creative approach and current knowledge of SEO industry trends.
- Strong leadership and the ability to manage cross-functional priorities in visible initiatives under tight deadlines.
- Ability to multitask and work both collaboratively and autonomously in fast-paced, ambiguous situations.
- Previous digital-agency SEO experience.
- Ability to influence stakeholders and partners as a digital-marketing thought leader.
- Excellent written, verbal, interpersonal, organizational, and detail-management skills.
