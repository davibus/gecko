Position Summary : The Assistant Brand Marketing Manager is responsible for supporting the strategic planning, budgeting, monthly reporting and product management of our flag ship brand – Nature’s Bounty. This position would coordinate with cross functional teams, such as Finance, Media, Customer Strategy, Sales, Demand/Supply Planning, Medical, Legal, Regulatory and Consumer Insights to support achievement of brand objectives. Key Responsibilities : Support the annual planning process, aggregating key learnings and insights to build compelling brand strategies. Collaborate with Brand Experience to build marketing plans that align with brand strategy, and meet brand objectives. Collaborate with Sales/Customer Strategy to translate brand strategy into meaningful selling materials. Oversee brand budgets - tracking PO requests, optimizing mix as needed, submitting monthly financial deliverables. Manage coupons – submitting code requests, forecasting and redemption tracking. Lead Monthly Business Reviews – aggregating latest consumption and shipment data, new product performance, competitive entrants, tactical updates/results. Support monthly GPS phasing with consumption trends, customer insights, and program changes to inform and enable consensus. Facilitate changes to packaging, bulk and new item requests. Partner with inventory team to track any OOS/issues and reduce write-offs. Partner with medical/legal/regulatory to maintain, approve claims. Experience & Education Requirements: Undergraduate degree required. MBA preferred. 3+ years of professional experience with 2+ years in a highly similar role. 3+ months of prior Nature's Bounty brand experience preferred. Branded CPG industry experience preferable, but not required. Must be proficient in all Microsoft Office Suite applications, particularly Excel and PowerPoint. Strong analytical skills and comfort level building analytical models and working with data to create actionable insights. Must possess strong written and oral communication skills, and enthusiasm for leadership. Ability to work independently and meet deadlines. Must be able to multi-task and follow-up when responses are needed. Strong organizational and planning skills are essential. Proficiency in coordinating across cross-functional teams in a variety of corporate functions including sales, operations, finance, legal and regulatory. Motivated, energetic, and excited to learn from great leaders in the CPG industry. Passion for the health & wellness space a plus.
