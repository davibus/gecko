## Job description

Company Overview Deepgram is the leading platform underpinning the emerging trillion-dollar Voice AI economy, providing real-time APIs for speech-to-text (STT), text-to-speech (TTS), and building production-grade voice agents at scale. More than 200,000 developers and 1,300+ organizations build voice offerings that are ‘Powered by Deepgram’, including Twilio, Cloudflare, Sierra, Decagon, Vapi, Daily, Cresta, Granola, and Jack in the Box. Deepgram’s voice-native foundation models are accessed through cloud APIs or as self-hosted and on-premises software, with unmatched accuracy, low latency, and cost efficiency. Backed by a recent Series C led by leading global investors and strategic partners, Deepgram has processed over 50,000 years of audio and transcribed more than 1 trillion words. There is no organization in the world that understands voice better than Deepgram. Company Operating Rhythm At Deepgram, we expect an AI-first mindset—AI use and comfort aren’t optional, they’re core to how we operate, innovate, and measure performance. Every team member who works at Deepgram is expected to actively use and experiment with advanced AI tools, and even build your own into your everyday work. We measure how effectively AI is applied to deliver results, and consistent, creative use of the latest AI capabilities is key to success here. Candidates should be comfortable adopting new models and modes quickly, integrating AI into their workflows, and continuously pushing the boundaries of what these technologies can do. Additionally, we move at the pace of AI. Change is rapid, and you can expect your day-to-day work to evolve just as quickly. This may not be the right role if you’re not excited to experiment, adapt, think on your feet, and learn constantly, or if you’re seeking something highly prescriptive with a traditional 9-to-5. Team Overview EMEA is a growing market for Deepgram, and we're moving from opportunistic sponsorships to a planned events and field program tied to pipeline. You'll be our first field and events marketer in the region, based in London, working with EMEA sales and partner leads and as part of the global Marketing team, reporting to the Senior Director, Events & Field Marketing. You'll build a London technical community and partner motions across the region, and connect every event to sales pipeline. The playbook you create will shape how Europe's builders and buyers experience Deepgram for years. Key Goals Publish a committed 6-9 month EMEA events plan and tiering model, agreed with sales and partner leads within your first 30 days Launch a recurring London technical community program with steady attendance from developers, builders and customers Stand up repeatable joint programs with cloud, technology or channel partners, with shared goals and pipeline tracked from day one Build an event-to-sales system (target account lists, pre-event meeting booking, fast follow-up) that meets pre-set pipeline objectives Ship an event-in-a-box kit (signage, swag, talk tracks, lead capture) so local sales and partner teams can run key events without hands-on marketing support Replace ad hoc requests with a simple intake-and-approval process, with a portion of budget and capacity planning reserved for late-breaking partner opportunities Minimum Skills, Knowledge & Capabilities Builds communities technical audiences show up for: hands-on content, credible speakers, real builders in the room, no sales pitches Designs partner marketing motions with cloud, technology or channel partners, with shared goals and measurable pipeline Connects events to sales: aligns on target accounts, books pre-event meetings, and drives follow-up that turns attendance into pipeline Operates AI-native: designs Human+AI workflows for event planning, follow-up and reporting Builds systems that eliminate recurring work, such as event kits and intake processes, instead of re-solving each event Ranks opportunities against clear criteria and aligns sales, partner and marketing stakeholders around one plan Executes events with sound logistics and vendor management, and handles lead capture in line with GDPR Works self-directed as the first in region, across time zones with a US-based team;
