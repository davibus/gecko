## Job description

About the Role The Marketing & Demand Generation Specialist will take ownership of building and executing a structured B2B marketing program for an established technology services business. This is a hands-on role for an experienced marketer who can combine strategic thinking with practical execution across content, campaigns, demand generation, marketing automation and sales enablement. Working closely with leadership, sales, business development and technical subject matter experts, you will strengthen brand visibility, communicate complex technology solutions clearly and create coordinated marketing activity that generates and nurtures qualified opportunities. You will have the autonomy to assess existing marketing activities, recommend priorities and establish scalable processes while personally delivering the campaigns, content and systems required to support business growth. About the Client You will be working with an established, New Zealand-owned technology services provider supporting businesses with integrated IT, communications and digital solutions. The organisation combines technical expertise with a strong understanding of commercial needs, helping customers improve productivity, security, resilience and long-term business performance. Its capabilities span managed IT services, cybersecurity, cloud solutions, AI readiness and governance, Microsoft productivity solutions, business intelligence, connectivity, and voice and communications services. With a partnership-led approach, the business works closely with customers to simplify their technology environments and deliver measurable value through practical, reliable solutions.
