## Job description

Mira Home, founded in 2024, is a home services brand operating in 14 U.S. markets with nationwide expansion planned for 2027. This hands-on individual contributor role reports to the Director of Marketing and owns Google's advertising channel end to end. Success is measured in customers, customer acquisition cost (CAC), and revenue.

Responsibilities: Own Google Search and Local Services Ads across every market; expand into Performance Max, Microsoft Ads, YouTube, and Demand Gen where justified. Build a multi-location account structure supporting growth from 14 to 20+ markets. Personally manage keyword strategy, search term mining, match types, negatives, geographic targeting, ad copy, bidding, budgets, and scheduling. Manage Local Services Ads verification, service categories, budgets, and daily lead disputes. Separate high-value recurring protection plan demand from low-value demand and protect brand terms. Own GTM, GA4, conversion actions, enhanced conversions, and call tracking; collaborate with development and data teams to send sold customers and their value back to Google. Forecast spend, leads, customers, and CAC by market and allocate budgets. Build a new-market launch playbook, test landing page improvements, maintain a documented testing roadmap and change log, and report weekly.

Required qualifications: 5+ years of hands-on Google Ads experience with direct ownership of strategy, budgets, and results. Experience in pest control, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping, other home services, or multi-location lead generation. Personally managed at least $100,000 per month in Google Ads and increased spend without losing efficiency. Hands-on Local Services Ads experience. Ability to handle GTM, GA4, enhanced conversions, offline conversion imports, and call tracking using CallTrackingMetrics, CallRail, or similar tools. Clear writing, plain explanations for leadership, and strong Excel or Google Sheets skills.

Preferred qualifications: Pest control experience; six-figure monthly Google Ads budgets; building or rebuilding a Google channel from a low base or launching new local markets; value-based bidding using CRM data; experience with FieldRoutes, ServiceTitan, PestPac, Salesforce, or HubSpot; scaled call-driven lead generation; incrementality testing; Google Ads certification.

The role requires commercial judgment, diagnostic skills across advertising and lead handling, and ownership of account changes and results.

Benefits: Performance-based bonus; health, dental, vision, life, and disability insurance; 401(k) matching; paid time off. Final compensation depends on experience, qualifications, and position scope.

Application requirements stated in the posting: Resume plus one or two short write-ups of personally managed Google Ads accounts covering monthly spend, number of markets, industry, campaign types, challenge, personal contributions, and measurable results over a stated period. Distinguish personal work from team or agency work. Applications without write-ups will not be reviewed.
