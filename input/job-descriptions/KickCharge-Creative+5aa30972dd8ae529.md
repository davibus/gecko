## Job description

At KickCharge, we don’t just help businesses get found online — we help them stand out. We’re a fast-growing agency passionate about helping businesses build powerful brands, generate leads, and drive real results. As we continue to grow, we’re seeking an experienced SEO Lead who’s ready to make an impact, lead with strategy, and help shape the future of our SEO department. We need a top-notch SEO professional who isn’t afraid to roll up their sleeves and get involved. The ideal candidate lives and breathes SEO, understands all aspects of search marketing, and can translate technical SEO concepts into clear, actionable insights for both clients and internal teams. This role is perfect for someone who is highly organized, proactive, and thrives in a fast-paced agency environment. You’ll lead SEO strategy and execution across multiple accounts, mentor and support team members, and help drive consistent performance and growth across all campaigns. If you’re ready for an opportunity to directly impact clients, help shape processes, and grow alongside a collaborative and passionate team, we’d love to hear from you. Responsibilities Lead the SEO team, ensuring consistent performance, quality, and execution across all accounts. Develop, refine, and standardize SEO strategies, processes, and best practices. Oversee account performance and ensure KPIs are consistently met or exceeded. Provide strategic direction for high-priority and complex SEO initiatives. Mentor, train, and support SEO team members across all experience levels. Conduct regular audits and reviews to maintain quality and performance standards. Collaborate with Paid Ads, Web, and Operations teams to align strategies and execution. Identify opportunities for process improvement, scalability, and efficiency. Stay current on algorithm updates, industry trends, and changes in search behavior. Skills & Qualifications Deep expertise in technical SEO, on-page SEO, and local SEO/GBP optimization. Strong leadership and team management skills. Strategic thinker with the ability to build scalable SEO frameworks. Advanced analytical and problem-solving abilities. Excellent communication and collaboration skills. Ability to mentor and develop team members. High attention to detail and commitment to quality. Strong organizational, planning, and prioritization skills. Preferred Experience Experience managing multiple SEO accounts or campaigns simultaneously. Proficiency with SEO tools such as SEMrush, Ahrefs, Google Analytics, and Google Search Console. Agency or fast-paced digital marketing experience preferred. Understanding of website UX, conversion optimization, and web performance best practices.
