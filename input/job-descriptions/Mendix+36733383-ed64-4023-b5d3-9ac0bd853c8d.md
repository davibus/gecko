## Job description

Intelligence Center X is Siemens Digital Industries Software's agentic enterprise system, empowering companies to embed agents alongside people with the context, intelligence, orchestration and governance they need to deliver AI-driven business impact. Siemens Digital Industries Software helps organizations of all sizes digitally transform using software, hardware and services from the Siemens Xcelerator business platform. Siemens' software and the comprehensive digital twin enable companies to optimize their design, engineering and manufacturing processes to turn today's ideas into the sustainable products of the future. From chips to entire systems, from product to process, across all industries. Senior Content Marketing Manager – Industrial B2B Siemens Digital Industries Software is looking for a Senior Content Marketing Manager who specializes in marketing to complex, engineering-driven industrial sectors – and lives at the intersection of technical depth and compelling storytelling. We’re looking for someone who has built content programs that speak to these industries’ buyers. You will lead and manage the production of content that earns the trust of an executive level audience, that translates complex concepts into narratives that drive awareness, pipeline, and product adoption. You'll be building category-defining content: SME-driven insight pieces, research-backed reports, practitioner guides, webinars, and executive thought leadership. To be successful in this role, you should be curious, resourceful, process and outcome oriented, and eager to stay ahead of the market to make sure what we publish resonates and leads. prune, update, and consolidate underperforming assets to protect domain authority A/B test headlines, CTAs, and formats; use data to iterate quickly and scale what works Cross-Functional Collaboration Serve as the content partner for product launches: owning the blog post, the launch email, the release notes narrative, and the sales one-pager Enable the sales team with content that maps to objection-handling moments, competitive comparisons, and proof points Coordinate with developer relations and community teams to amplify practitioner-generated content and surface customer stories What We're Looking For Required 7+ years of B2B content marketing experience, with at least 2 years Exceptional writing and editing skills.
