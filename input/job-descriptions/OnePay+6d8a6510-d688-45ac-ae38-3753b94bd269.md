About OnePay OnePay is the consumer fintech trusted by millions of Americans to make money better. Our financial system is broken. High fees, low rates, and too few ways to actually grow your money. We’re fixing it. And we’re moving fast. We’re an all-in-one financial services platform that brings together banking, high-yield savings, credit cards, point-of-sale lending, investing, and crypto in one place. We also partner with employers, HCM providers, gig platforms, and others to deliver embedded financial services to millions of employees and frontline workers. We’re backed by Walmart, the world’s largest retailer, and Ribbit Capital, one of fintech’s most respected investors, giving us rare scale, distribution, and the opportunity to build something truly category-defining. But what really sets OnePay apart is how we move. Our customers don’t have time to wait… and neither do we. This place moves fast, and we’re looking for people who are: Ready to run Hungry and driven by urgency Exceptional at what they do, with low ego Comfortable operating in motion The Role As a Senior Program Manager, Marketing Operations, you will own the operational engine behind our marketing ecosystem. This role will work cross functionally across Marketing, Creative, Design, Product and key stakeholders. You'll own the operational framework that ensures marketing initiatives move from roadmap to execution with clear priorities, defined timelines, actionable briefs, and seamless collaboration. You will: Own end-to-end program management of marketing initiatives, translating strategy into executable workstreams with clear owners, timelines, and milestones Build scalable processes — intake, briefing frameworks, production timelines, and operating rhythms — that reduce ambiguity and drive consistency Serve as the primary operational liaison with stakeholders, proactively maintaining visibility into priorities, calendars, and roadmaps Partner with creative and studio teams to ensure briefs are complete and blockers are removed Coordinate across agencies, print vendors, and production partners to keep schedules on track You Bring 8 years of experience in Program Management, Marketing Operations, Creative Operations, or a related field.
