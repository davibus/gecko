## Job description

Company Overview Deepgram is the leading platform underpinning the emerging trillion-dollar Voice AI economy, providing real-time APIs for speech-to-text (STT), text-to-speech (TTS), and building production-grade voice agents at scale. More than 200,000 developers and 1,300+ organizations build voice offerings that are ‘Powered by Deepgram’, including Twilio, Cloudflare, Sierra, Decagon, Vapi, Daily, Cresta, Granola, and Jack in the Box. Deepgram’s voice-native foundation models are accessed through cloud APIs or as self-hosted and on-premises software, with unmatched accuracy, low latency, and cost efficiency. Backed by a recent Series C led by leading global investors and strategic partners, Deepgram has processed over 50,000 years of audio and transcribed more than 1 trillion words. There is no organization in the world that understands voice better than Deepgram. Company Operating Rhythm At Deepgram, we expect an AI-first mindset—AI use and comfort aren’t optional, they’re core to how we operate, innovate, and measure performance. Every team member who works at Deepgram is expected to actively use and experiment with advanced AI tools, and even build your own into your everyday work. We measure how effectively AI is applied to deliver results, and consistent, creative use of the latest AI capabilities is key to success here. Candidates should be comfortable adopting new models and modes quickly, integrating AI into their workflows, and continuously pushing the boundaries of what these technologies can do. Additionally, we move at the pace of AI. Change is rapid, and you can expect your day-to-day work to evolve just as quickly. This may not be the right role if you’re not excited to experiment, adapt, think on your feet, and learn constantly, or if you’re seeking something highly prescriptive with a traditional 9-to-5. As a Senior Product Marketing Manager, Solutions Marketing at Deepgram, you will be our first dedicated solutions marketer. Enterprises don't buy speech models, they buy outcomes: patient intake that never misses a medication name, drive-thrus that get the order right, federal deployments that run air-gapped. You'll translate our platform into the solution narratives, first-call decks, and proof that make those outcomes obvious to buyers, and you'll build the systems that produce them repeatably, not one asset at a time. This role sits within Product Marketing, reports to the Senior Manager of Partner, Customer, and Solutions Marketing, and works most closely with marketing and sales. You'll also partner with Applied Engineering and DevRel on demos, Demand Gen on vertical campaigns, and Partner and Customer Marketing on proof and distribution. What You'll Do Own the solutions bill of materials end-to-end: for each priority use case and vertical, build and maintain the narrative, first-call deck, solution brief, hero demo script, battlecard, proof points, and web copy, refreshed quarterly. If sellers are not using it, it does not count. Build use-case-first messaging with vertical multipliers: anchor on what buyers deploy (voice agents for support, clinical documentation, ordering, intake) and layer verticals on top (healthcare and pharma, federal and public sector, financial services, CX and restaurants) Lead the solutions layer of every major launch: own the "what this unlocks for your industry" story, including named accuracy metrics that set the category's measuring stick Be the voice of the market: mine sales calls, win-loss, and usage patterns for messaging evidence; sit in prospect calls; turn deployments into segment proof points with Customer Marketing Feed demand gen and the field: hand campaign-ready narratives and landing-page messaging to Demand Gen, package enablement with GTM Enablement, and route solutions content through co-marketing with Partner Marketing.
