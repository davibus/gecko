We are seeking a hard-working, innovative, detail-oriented, and creative team player to join our Aptive team! This is a full-time Sr Manager, Marketing Analytics position located in Provo, Utah. The individual we are looking for is someone who enjoys working in a team environment and excels working at an individual level.

What we offer:

- Annual Merit bonuses

- Group Health, Dental, and Vision plans

- Pet insurance, Life insurance, and EAP benefits

- 401K with employer match up to 4%

- Paid holidays and paid time off

- Opportunity for advancement

- Upbeat and exciting company culture and much more!

Responsibilities include:

- Lead day-to-day execution of marketing performance measurement across paid media, owned channels, CRM, web, and offline marketing

- Ensure accurate, consistent reporting of marketing KPIs across channels, campaigns, and customer segments

- Support the development and maintenance of standardized dashboards and performance reports used by marketing leadership

- Produce clear, actionable reporting that helps teams identify optimization opportunities across channels, creative, audiences, and timing

- Translate data into insights that inform campaign optimizations and near-term strategy adjustments

- Partner with channel owners to interpret performance results and recommend improvements

- Support attribution and incrementality measurement efforts in partnership with analytics and data teams

- Analyze test results from A/B tests and experiments to quantify impact and inform next steps

- Conduct deep-dive analyses to answer specific business questions and uncover performance drivers

- Support marketing forecasting efforts by providing historical performance analysis, trend insights, and scenario inputs

- Assist in re-forecasting and planning exercises in partnership with Marketing and Finance

- Monitor performance vs. forecast and highlight risks and opportunities

- Work closely with Paid Media, CRM, SEO, and Creative teams to ensure analytics insights are applied to marketing decisions.

- Partner with Finance to support ROI analysis, budgeting discussions, and performance reviews

- Present insights and recommendations to marketing leadership in a clear, concise manner

- Identify opportunities to improve reporting consistency, efficiency, and usability

- Document metric definitions and reporting standards to promote alignment across the team

- Continuously refine reporting and analysis based on business needs and feedback

Requirements:

- 8-10 years of experience in marketing analytics, business intelligence, or related roles

- Experience supporting multi-channel or full-funnel marketing teams preferred

- Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Analytics, or related field

- Experience supporting multi-channel or full-funnel marketing teams preferred

- Google Analytics (GA4) Certification, preferred. Adobe Analytics or Adobe Experience Platform certifications, preferred. Marketing analytics or experimentation certifications (e.g., CRO, A/B testing, optimization programs), preferred

- Strong experience in marketing analytics, performance measurement, or business intelligence

- Hands-on expertise analyzing paid media, CRM, and digital marketing data

- Advanced analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to translate data into insights

- Experience with analytics and visualization tools

- Strong communication skills with the ability to present insights to non-technical stakeholders

- Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
