## Job description

Fullpath by Cox Automotive is a fast growing tech company in the automotive space with hubs across the US and Israel. We are disrupting the multi-trillion dollar transportation industry with our advanced Customer Data Platform (CDP) . Our work happens in the fast lane as we bring AI-powered, data-driven solutions to a quickly evolving industry.Our team at Fullpath is made up of curious and creative individuals who are always looking to achieve the impossible. We are bold, collaborative, and goal driven, and, at our core, we believe every voice has value and can impact our bottom line.If you are a creative, solutions oriented individual who is ready to put your career in drive, Fullpath is the place for you!We are seeking a highly skilled Digital Advertising Analyst to join our dynamic team, combining expertise in Google Ads, Facebook Manager, and our proprietary optimization platform. As a key member of our team, you will be responsible for supporting Customer Success Managers (CSMs) with executing campaign strategies that leverage our proprietary ad tech. This role will be consultative and in support of CSMs and will not hold a specific book of business. Our ideal candidate will have knowledge in GA4, Google Adwords, Facebook and will be comfortable using our technology and off-platform analytics to tell a story and make clear recommendations. You will be assigned to work with mid-market customers and will be responsible for executing custom campaigns, ad copy, and budget updates as well as make proactive suggestions/monitor budget pacing in support of Customer Success Managers.This is a remote role reporting to the Director of Advertising Operations.
