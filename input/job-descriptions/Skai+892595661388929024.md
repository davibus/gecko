## Full job description

Skai is hiring a full-time, intermediate-level Paid Search Manager in New York City. Internally, the role functions as an Expert Services Manager who provides strategic consulting and hands-on search advertising support to enterprise clients. The position is hybrid, with three office days each week, and offers a salary range of $85,000–$95,000 plus bonus eligibility.

The manager owns day-to-day client enablement, campaign creation and optimization, client-call support, performance reporting, program results, and client satisfaction on the Skai Search platform. Primary channels include Google Ads, Google Performance Max, Microsoft Search, and Microsoft Smart Shopping/Performance Max. The role partners with an Expert Services Director and serves internal stakeholders as a regional search subject-matter expert.

### Responsibilities

- Help clients become successful and self-sufficient in search marketing and campaign management.
- Execute and optimize advertising campaigns to exceed client goals.
- Advise clients on media planning, campaign strategy, and paid-search best practices.
- Communicate account performance and results to internal and external stakeholders.
- Provide paid-media solutions and platform support that help clients use Skai effectively.
- Support client-services teams and existing clients through QBRs and strategic consultation.
- Develop solutions beyond established tools and templates when client needs require them.
- Partner with product development, technical account management, growth, and client-success teams to improve the platform and capture market opportunities.

### Requirements

- Undergraduate degree.
- Three to four years of hands-on paid-search campaign implementation, optimization, and management.
- At least three years of client-facing or customer-service experience.
- Data-analysis, Microsoft Excel, Google Workspace, and AI-agentic-solution skills.
- Experience collaborating with global teams both locally and virtually.
- Strong problem-solving orientation and commitment to client success.
- Experience with Skai or another search-marketing platform is preferred.
- Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising certifications are highly desirable.
- Ability to travel occasionally.
