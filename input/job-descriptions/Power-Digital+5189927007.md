A day in the life: The Senior Integrated Content Strategist, B2B owns the integrated content strategy for a portfolio of complex B2B accounts and sets the strategic direction and execution of deliverables. This role operates across the full integrated content ecosystem: thought leadership, sales enablement, lead magnets, whitepapers, research reports, one-sheeters, brochures, infographics, brand strategy, verbal identity, and messaging playbooks — all engineered to move a considered, multi-stakeholder buyer through a long B2B sales cycle. Senior Strategists build and defend content roadmaps directly with C-level stakeholders, translating pipeline and revenue goals into a clear content thesis that internal teams and client executives can rally behind. This is a strategist who thinks in systems: mapping content audits to the buyer journey, running audience research and stakeholder interviews to surface what actually moves a B2B deal forward, and adapting the roadmap as account performance and market signals shift. They bring product-level thinking to the content offering itself; proposing new formats and approaches before clients ask. They will also mentor and uplevel the strategists and writers who support their accounts. They are equally fluent identifying the single biggest content opportunity in a client's funnel and articulating why it matters in a room full of VPs and CMOs.
