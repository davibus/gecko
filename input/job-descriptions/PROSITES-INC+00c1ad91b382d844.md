## Job description

As Growth Marketing Manager, you will own the demand engine end-to-end--strategy, execution, and outcomes across pipeline creation, lead quality, and conversion. You bring deep digital acquisition expertise, high analytical and technical skills, and the operational discipline to build efficient, ever-improving funnels and loops. This role reports to the Head of Growth Marketing and partners tightly with Sales, Product, and Operations to hit aggressive pipeline and revenue goals.

### Key Responsibilities

- Act as a player/coach for customer acquisition across multiple verticals, advising and being hands on keys across multiple digital marketing strategies.
- Build and own customer acquisition plans for the ideal customer profile across multiple verticals: targeting, channel mix, budgets, measurement, and quarterly targets including MQL, SQL/PQL, pipeline, and CAC/LTV.
- Translate strategy into a live roadmap, calendar, and briefs spanning campaigns, launches, and always-on programs to enable channel marketing managers, designers, writers, and the web team to execute.
- Set hypothesis-driven goals and prioritize by expected impact, cost, and confidence.
- Build compounding loops across content, SEO, retargeting, nurture, referrals, advocacy, and partner co-marketing that improve efficiency over time.
- Run structured A/B and multivariate experiments across ads, offers, landing pages, and nurture flows, and systematize learnings.
- Collaborate with Product Marketing on messaging, offers, competitive positioning, and product launches for the vertical.
- Work with Sales and SDR leadership to align targets, handoffs, and feedback loops, and enable teams with pitch materials and objection handling.
- Partner with channel managers and Customer Success to implement lifecycle triggers, scoring, and routing that increase speed-to-lead and meeting-set rates.

### Measurement and Analytics

- Define objectives and key results to achieve company goals and drive alignment across functional teams within the vertical.
- Build or maintain basic reporting to monitor the health of the vertical, strategy, and execution, and work with specialists on more complex needs.
- Attribute impact by channel and campaign; model CAC, payback, and LTV by segment.
- Maintain data quality and enforce tracking standards across campaigns and web properties.

### Operations and Process

- Establish an operating cadence including weekly planning, standups, and post-mortems, and maintain decision logs, playbooks, and SLAs with Sales and SDR.
- Partner with Operations on lead management including scoring, routing, enrichment, and forecast accuracy.
- Ensure compliance with privacy, consent, and brand guidelines.

### Qualifications

- 5-7+ years in digital or growth marketing with ownership of demand, leads, and conversion in B2B SaaS.
- Proven track record scaling multi-channel acquisition with efficient CAC and measurable pipeline impact, with a strong focus on funnels and loops.
- High analytical and technical aptitude and comfort translating data into decisive action.
- High proficiency in Google Analytics 4 and Salesforce.
- Exceptional campaign and project management, organization, follow-through, and documentation.
- Strong collaboration with Sales, RevOps, and Product teams, with crisp written and verbal communication.
- AI-first mentality and proficiency with AI to increase speed and quality.
- DTC experience is a strong plus.

### Nice to Have

- Power BI and SQL for deeper analysis and self-serve insights.
- Operational internet and web proficiency including HTTP, tags and cookies, CDNs, DNS, performance, accessibility, and SEO.
- Experience in the relevant industry vertical and with partner or affiliate ecosystems.
- Familiarity with marketing automation and enrichment tools such as HubSpot, Marketo, Clearbit, and ZoomInfo, plus experimentation platforms.
