## Job description

About Hatch Hatch is an AI agent lead management platform that helps services businesses turn every customer interaction into revenue growth. Our AI agents handle conversations over SMS, email, and voice—24/7—enabling businesses to scale 1:1 communication without scaling headcount. Recently acquired by Yelp, Hatch operates as a standalone product and is expanding our reach to help thousands more businesses unlock the revenue sitting in their lead pipeline. We've driven $5.1B in revenue for our customers—and we're just getting started. The role We're hiring a Senior Partner Marketing Manager to run our partner program — one of Hatch's highest-leverage growth channels. Rather than a channel support role, this is a co-marketing, co-sell, and relationship-driven one — and you'll own it end to end. You'll be the main point of contact and connective tissue between Hatch and our partners the person they come to for events, campaigns, and co-created content — and you'll pull in the right people across marketing (product marketing, enablement, content, field marketing) to make it happen. This is high-impact work. Strong partnerships drive pipeline, expand our reach, and build Hatch's credibility across the industry — and you'll be in charge of scaling that program. What you'll do: Help land new partners : partner with product marketing and enablement to produce the collateral BD uses to pitch and land new partners. New strategic partner and integration launches : Onboard and announce new partners and product integrations across email, blog, website, and social — working with product marketing on the integration's value prop. Co-marketing campaigns : Plan and execute co-branded campaigns — launches, webinars, ebooks, seasonal offers — tapping partners as a distribution channel to get Hatch's content in front of their audiences. You'll own the partner relationship and the plan, and partner with content and product marketing on assets, talk tracks, and speakers. Partner program management : For partners with year-round programs (newsletter placements, blog posts, committed content), own the fulfillment — producing some content yourself and pulling in content, PMM, and enablement for the rest. You'll also help keep Hatch's listings and profiles on partner sites accurate and up to date. Partner enablement : Scope the collateral partners need to speak fluently about Hatch to their audiences, and partner with product marketing and enablement to produce it. As Hatch evolves, keep partners in the loop and their materials current — so their knowledge of Hatch stays fresh. Partner events : Work with field marketing on partner events and VIP experiences, contributing partner context, messaging, and coordination. Repeatable playbooks & scaling : Turn one-off partner activations into repeatable playbooks, so the program scales efficiently as we add partners and move into new verticals. Partner relationships : Maintain ongoing relationships with partner contacts — regular syncs, staying current on their updates, and keeping communication flowing — and proactively bring partners ideas for campaigns and content. Tracking & optimization : Measure and optimize partner ROI, track referral handoffs, and improve lead quality from partner activity. What it takes to succeed: Relevant background: 4–7 years in partner marketing, co-marketing, or related B2B SaaS marketing (product or content) Natural relationship-builder: comfortable being the face of Hatch to partners, building trust, and managing external stakeholders Strong project management: you can juggle multiple partners and orchestrate deliverables across internal teams you don't manage — knowing when to set direction and when to step back — and keep a dozen moving parts on track and shipping on time Solid content and campaign instincts: you can develop narratives, campaign angles, and webinar topics, and produce or shape content across formats Multi-partner-type experience: you've worked across associations, tech platforms, and agencies — not just one vertical Fluent in pipeline language: SQOs, referral attribution, and co-marketing ROI Thrives in a build-stage environment: comfortable with ambiguity and a fast pace, and energized by shaping a program rather than inheriting a finished one What you'll get: Effective your first day: Full medical, vision, and dental 15 days PTO (accrual begins on date of hire and increases with 2+ years of tenure thereafter), 12 paid holidays, plus one floating holiday Up to 14 weeks of parental leave Monthly wellness subsidy Work from home reimbursement Flexible spending account 401(k) retirement savings plan Employee stock purchase plan Compensation range for this position is $106,000 - $198,000 annually. You may also be offered a bonus and benefits.
