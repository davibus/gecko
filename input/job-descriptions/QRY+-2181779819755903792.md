## Full job description

QRY is a media-buying agency focused on scaling retail brands through connected brand-awareness and performance media, sophisticated analytics, and full-funnel strategy. It is hiring a remote U.S. Paid Search Strategist to own search performance across a portfolio of clients.

The strategist plans, launches, and optimizes search and Shopping programs across Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon, and other relevant retail channels. The role keeps budgets and pacing controlled, converts performance data into clear decisions, and communicates complex findings simply to clients and teammates.

### Responsibilities

- Plan, book, and optimize paid-search programs for approximately five to eight clients using appropriate planning tools.
- Design and execute A/B experiments involving keywords, bidding strategies, ad copy, landing pages, and offers.
- Advise clients on search strategy, rationale, and plans tied to business goals.
- Stay current on search, social, mobile, tracking, tagging, behavioral marketing, retargeting, and addressability.
- Own monthly channel reporting, strategy sessions, and communication with clients, with support from associate directors.
- Scope and manage channel initiatives, identify risks and growth opportunities, and recommend strategic pivots.
- Present performance reports that connect channel results to the broader funnel and business outcomes.
- Produce monthly channel forecasts based on budget and revenue goals.
- Collaborate with media and creative teammates, including cross-channel planning and issue resolution.
- Deliver against KPIs such as MER, ROAS, CAC, LTV, revenue, and efficiency while maintaining strong QA and documentation.

### Qualifications

- Two to five years of successful hands-on paid-search or SEM experience, ideally in an agency environment.
- Bachelor’s degree in marketing, science, or a related discipline preferred.
- Strong analytical ability and experience turning complex data into clear reports and actionable plans.
- Excellent communication with senior stakeholders.
- Strong time management and ability to manage competing priorities.
- Genuine interest in ecommerce, advertising, and agency work.
- Creative problem solving, continuous learning, and comfort working remotely with professional video-conferencing practices.

### Compensation and benefits

- Salary range of $70,000–$80,000 based on experience.
- Quarterly bonuses tied to company revenue.
- U.S. remote flexibility, unlimited PTO, fully covered employee health/dental/vision insurance, a monthly wellbeing allowance, 401(k) match, professional development support, company-provided technology, and team retreats.
