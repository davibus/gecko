## Job description

About Us We're a Boca Raton-based digital marketing agency with more than a decade in the industry, focused on behavioral health, mental health, addiction treatment and senior living. We help treatment providers compete in a market dominated by large operators with big budgets, and we do it with data-driven decisions, not overblown promises. Paid search is at the core of what we deliver, supported by our SEO, web development, and call tracking and dashboarding teams. About the Role We're hiring a Director of Paid Search with deep, hands-on Google Ads expertise to lead PPC performance across our client accounts. You'll own search end to end: structure, bidding, tracking, testing, budget and reporting. Success is measured by qualified calls, admissions and cost per acquisition, not clicks. This is a search role, not a social media role. Responsibilities Own Google Ads strategy and performance across multiple client accounts, with Microsoft Advertising as a secondary channel. Build scalable account structures by service line, level of care, location and payer focus. Get the most out of every client budget so clients can compete against larger operators. Manage Search and Performance Max campaigns, and keep PMax from cannibalizing branded search. Lead keyword research, search term mining and negative keyword management in high-cost, highly competitive healthcare verticals. Write and test ad copy that complies with Google's healthcare and addiction-services policies and client LegitScript requirements. Manage Smart Bidding strategies optimized toward qualified calls and admissions, not raw leads. Own conversion tracking across GA4, Google Tag Manager and call conversions. Integrate call tracking data from CallTrackingMetrics and CallRail into campaign optimization and client dashboards. Partner with our SEO and web development teams on landing page performance and conversion rate optimization. Run A/B tests on ads and landing pages. Manage client ad budgets: pace spend, forecast CPA and explain variances. Give clients honest, data-backed reporting and set realistic expectations. Support and develop paid search team members. Requirements 4+ years of hands-on Google Ads experience. Has managed Google Ads accounts independently, from build through optimization. Solid understanding of Smart Bidding, Performance Max and keyword strategy. Working knowledge of GA4 and Google Tag Manager. Experience setting up and auditing conversion and call tracking. Track record of improving CPA or campaign performance. Strong Excel/Google Sheets skills. Comfortable presenting results directly to clients. Preferred Behavioral health, addiction treatment, mental health or senior living advertising experience. Familiarity with Google's addiction-services ad policy and LegitScript certification. Experience with CallTrackingMetrics or CallRail. Experience leading or mentoring a team. Google Ads certification. Google Ads scripts, Search Ads 360 or Looker Studio.
