## Job description

Butler/Till is a results-driven marketing agency offering deeply collaborative client experiences, proprietary technology, and world-class partnerships. At Butler/Till, we take immense pride in our independent, women-owned and led status, our unwavering commitment to a purpose-driven approach, and our unique structure as a 100% employee-owned company (ESOP). SUMMARY The Paid Search Strategist at Butler/Till supports small business clients by combining hands-on paid search execution with consultative client support. This role balances campaign management, reporting, and optimization with educating clients on how paid search drives their business goals. As a subject matter expert, the Strategist analyzes performance, ensures quality, supports strategy and planning, and optimizes KPIs while collaborating closely with internal teams and staying current on industry trends.
