About Outreach Outreach, founded in 2014, is the only complete agentic AI platform for revenue teams. Outreach infuses agentic AI, conversation intelligence, and assistive AI to power hundreds of use cases across revenue motions. From new logo prospecting to expansions, deal acceleration, driving retention, and forecasting, Outreach AI automates workflows and frees sellers to focus on more strategic conversations and actions. Revenue leaders benefit from connected account visibility, performance insights, and higher forecasting accuracy across every GTM team. World leading enterprise organizations use Outreach to power their revenue teams, including Databricks, SAP, Siemens, and Verizon to name a few. About the Role At Outreach, what we build changes how thousands of sales teams sell every day. We're looking for a Principal Product Marketing Manager, Competitive to shape how we understand, position, and compete in the rapidly evolving revenue technology market. In this role, you'll own competitive intelligence across our AI and revenue platform portfolio, turning customer, competitor, and market insights into differentiated narratives, strategic recommendations, and competitive programs that help our Sales, Marketing, Partner, and Customer teams win. As a senior individual contributor, you'll influence go-to-market decisions, anticipate market shifts, and serve as a trusted advisor to leaders across the organization. If you're passionate about turning market intelligence into competitive advantage, you'll thrive in this role.
