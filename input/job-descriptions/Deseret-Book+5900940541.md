## Job description

Deseret Book is a mission-driven retailer and publisher of faith-centered books, media, and products, operating retail stores, DeseretBook.com, and the Bookshelf+ digital subscription service. The company is seeking a strategic, execution-minded Influencer & Affiliate Marketing Manager to build, launch, and scale both programs. The role reports to the Director of Marketing and follows a hybrid schedule in Salt Lake City.

The manager will own the full partner lifecycle: discovery, recruitment, onboarding, contract negotiation, content execution, performance tracking, and optimization. The affiliate program includes Deseret Book creators, third-party creators, and external influencers managed through Tapfiliate. Responsibilities include serving as the primary partner contact, developing training and enablement resources, supplying briefs and creative assets, maintaining promotional calendars, and ensuring brand-consistent content.

The role will optimize conversion paths across landing pages, links, offers, and attribution to improve ROI. It will track sales, conversion rate, CAC, AOV, and revenue per affiliate; report insights to stakeholders; enforce brand guidelines and FTC disclosure requirements; and monitor fraud and policy compliance.

For influencer marketing, the manager will design and launch a structured program focused on audience and Bookshelf+ subscriber growth. The work includes discovering and vetting influencers based on engagement, audience demographics, content quality, and brand alignment; negotiating rates, exclusivity, deliverables, and usage rights; reviewing content for quality and compliance; and optimizing the creator mix and platform allocation based on performance.

The manager owns the influencer and affiliate budget, reconciles spend monthly, and coordinates with Ecommerce, Content, Retail, Bookshelf+, and Digital teams to integrate partnerships with company-wide campaigns. Regular reporting will cover growth, revenue attribution, and partner health.

Qualifications include four to six years in affiliate, influencer, or creator partnership marketing, preferably in retail, e-commerce, or media; experience building or scaling an affiliate program; hands-on familiarity with Tapfiliate or platforms such as Impact, CJ, or Rakuten; experience vetting, negotiating with, and managing influencer partners; proficiency with Google Analytics, performance reporting, and marketing budget management; knowledge of FTC and affiliate compliance; strong communication and relationship-management skills; familiarity with major social platforms and current content trends; and a bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field.

Preferred qualifications include experience with faith-based, publishing, or family-oriented consumer brands; familiarity with subscription business models and subscriber acquisition; experience creating training and enablement content for external partners; and knowledge of click-fraud detection and affiliate compliance monitoring.
