**Travel to Office expectations**
For Remote Roles: If this role is remote, there will be in-office events that will require travel to and from the Mississauga and/or Salt Lake City office. These will include, but not limited to, onboarding, team events, semi-annual and annual team meetings.
 
For Hybrid Roles: If this role is Hybrid, there will be an expectation to reside within commutable distance to the office/location specified in the job listing. This will include, but not limited to, weekly/bi-weekly/monthly events in the office with your specific team. This is a requirement for this role.

- Data Pipelines & Integrations: Design, build, and maintain data pipelines connecting Salesforce, Marketo, and the data warehouse (e.g., BigQuery) to support marketing performance reporting.

- Reporting Automation & Analytics Enablement: Automate recurring campaign reporting workflows and write/optimize SQL to build reusable datasets, views, and queries that power dashboards and leadership reporting.

- Campaign Attribution & Data Quality: Strengthen campaign attribution logic (campaign codes, UTM/campaign member data, lead-to-opportunity linkage) and monitor, troubleshoot, and improve data quality across Marketo, Salesforce, and downstream reporting tables.

- AI-Enabled Analytics Workflows: Apply AI tools such as Claude to accelerate data exploration, documentation, and query development, while maintaining rigor and accuracy in production reporting.

- Systems Support & Documentation: Support backend administration and configuration in Salesforce and Marketo (fields, objects, sync rules, campaign structures), and document data models, definitions, and processes so metrics stay consistent and self-serve for marketing stakeholders.

- Bachelor's degree (completed or in final year) in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related technical field. New graduates are encouraged to apply.

- 0 – 2 years of applicable experience.

- Strong SQL skills: comfortable writing joins, aggregations, and window functions against large relational datasets.

- Working knowledge of at least one programming language used in data work (e.g., Python) for scripting, automation, or ETL.

- Understanding of relational data models and how CRM/marketing-automation data is structured (leads, contacts, accounts, campaigns, opportunities).

- Highly analytical and detail-oriented, with a strong bias toward automating repetitive work.

- Intellectually curious and open-minded, with a genuine thirst to learn new tools, systems, and business context.

- Hands-on exposure to Salesforce (SOQL, reports, campaign objects, admin concepts) or Marketo (programs, smart campaigns, field sync).

- Experience with a cloud data warehouse (BigQuery, Snowflake, Redshift) and BI tools (Power BI, Looker, Tableau).

- Familiarity with AI-assisted development or analytics tools, such as Claude or similar large language model platforms.

- Exposure to ETL/ELT tooling, APIs, or workflow orchestration (e.g., dbt, Airflow, Fivetran, or equivalent scripting).

- Familiarity with marketing funnel metrics (MQLs, opportunities, pipeline) or B2B demand generation.

- Co-op, internship, or project experience in a data engineering, analytics engineering, or revenue operations context.
