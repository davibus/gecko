Vice President, Customer Marketing Role Overview Siemens Digital Industries Software is looking for a Vice President of Customer Marketing to join a specialized growth team to lead the strategy, team and operating model for how Intelligence Center X, Siemens agentic enterprise system, engages, grows and learns from its customers across the full lifecycle. This leader will oversee customer advocacy and storytelling, digital and in-person community programs, lifecycle marketing and customer insight measurements to strength retention, adoption, expansion and market credibility. This role sits at the center of the customer experience, partnering closely with Product, Product Marketing, Solutions Marketing, Brand, Field and Sales. This leader will turn customer voice and customer proof into strategic assets for the business, while building the programs and measurement systems that deepen engagement and inform go-to-market priorities. including positioning, launches, releases, competitive positioning. This role will work cross-functionally to support analyst engagements and sales enablement. This role creates the strategic product marketing foundation that the rest of the Growth org, regional teams, and customer-facing functions build on to drive consistent, differentiated go-to-market execution.
