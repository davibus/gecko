## Full job description

At Compass, our mission is to help everyone find their place in the world. Founded in 2012, we are revolutionizing the real estate industry with an end-to-end platform that empowers residential real estate agents to deliver exceptional service to seller and buyer clients.

As a Strategic Growth Manager at Compass International Holdings, you will be at the center of the growth strategy, directly responsible for driving net-new revenue across company-owned brokerages, which may include Coldwell Banker, Sotheby's International Realty, Corcoran, and Compass. You will partner with top-producing real estate agents—CEOs of their own businesses—and influence them to grow their business with Compass.

## What You'll Do

- Drive strategic growth by identifying, engaging, and structuring deals with top-performing real estate agents and teams, expanding Compass's presence within the market.
- Conduct consultative business discovery and deliver enterprise-style demonstrations of the technology platform, tailoring presentations to the needs of agents, teams, and brokerage leaders.
- Position the value proposition across technology, marketing, culture, and growth.
- Build and manage a strong pipeline through a consultative, relationship-driven sales process.
- Develop deep market expertise and maintain a strong local presence.
- Collaborate with leadership, agents, and cross-functional teams.

## What This Role Offers

- Work directly with decision-makers for faster, high-impact conversations.
- Engage in a highly consultative, human sales process requiring high IQ and EQ.
- Build trusted relationships with high-performing professionals.
- Operate with high autonomy and ownership over success.

## How You'll Be Measured

This is a quota-carrying role measured by revenue attainment. Deal sizes range from $75,000 to more than $5 million with an average 30-day sales cycle. Revenue is measured by agent, team, and brokerage production volume.

## Location

This is an onsite, market-facing role based in the Salt Lake City office, engaging clients in the field while collaborating in person with local teams and agents.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree with 5+ years of relevant experience.
- Proven success in a quota-carrying role.
- Strong interpersonal, influence, and communication skills.
- Self-starter with strong judgment.
- Highly organized with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
- Track record of high performance.
- Demonstrated ability to deliver technology-focused presentations.

## Compensation

Base pay is $100,000-$110,000 annually, with a target bonus of $30,000-$60,000 and uncapped bonus potential. Compensation may also include equity and benefits.
