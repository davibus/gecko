## Job description

The Opportunity: Data privacy has become one of the most strategic challenges facing modern enterprises — sitting at the intersection of AI governance, data operations, and customer trust. As companies adopt AI at scale, they need new ways to automate privacy and manage risk without slowing innovation. DataGrail has launched the first agentic privacy platform, and we're entering a pivotal phase of growth as we scale toward the next stage of the business. DataGrail is shipping innovative products faster than ever before to meet our customers' needs and outpace the competition. As a Senior Product Marketing Manager, you’ll build the launch and enablement engine that turns that product velocity into customer adoption and revenue. You’ll ensure every release reaches the right audience, equip our GTM teams to confidently communicate the value of new capabilities, and help customers understand how the DataGrail platform solves their most important privacy challenges. This is a foundational, high-impact role. The systems, enablement programs, and AI-powered workflows you build during your first six months will shape how DataGrail brings new capabilities to market, helping accelerate adoption, support revenue growth and set the business up for long-term success.
