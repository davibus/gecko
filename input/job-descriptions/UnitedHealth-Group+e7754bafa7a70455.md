As the Director of Business Lending Acquisition Marketing within Optum Bank Small Business Lending, you will be responsible for driving profitable customer growth across the bank's business lending portfolio, including small-dollar and large-dollar lending solutions. Serving as the primary bridge between Lending Product, Marketing, Analytics, Digital, and Risk teams, you will own the end-to-end acquisition strategy and marketing roadmap designed to increase awareness, generate demand, improve conversion, and accelerate portfolio growth. This role operates at the intersection of product strategy, customer acquisition, digital marketing, analytics, and customer experience. You will act as the central orchestrator for lending initiatives, ensuring cross-functional alignment and execution of integrated, data-driven marketing programs that deliver measurable business results. The successful candidate will combine deep acquisition marketing expertise, lending industry knowledge, and strong leadership skills to optimize borrower acquisition and enhance the end-to-end customer journey. In this role, you will oversee multi-million-dollar acquisition budgets across paid search, paid social, affiliates, SEO, and emerging digital channels to drive qualified traffic, improve conversion performance, and maximize return on investment.
