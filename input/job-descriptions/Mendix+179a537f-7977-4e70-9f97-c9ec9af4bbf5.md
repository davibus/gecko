Head of Marketing Operations Role Overview Siemens Digital Industries Software is looking for a Head of Marketing Operations to lead the systems, data, reporting and operational infrastructure that enables a high-performing global marketing organization for its Intelligence Center X portfolio. This leader will oversee marketing technology, reporting, analytics, tooling, procurement, system migrations and program management, ensuring the marketing organization can operate effectively in a fast-paced, data and AI-driven, and highly matrixed enterprise environment. This role is both strategic and operational: helping to define how marketing runs, how performance is measured, and how systems and processes scale across teams, regions and product units. This leader will serve as the operational backbone of the marketing organization, partnering across Marketing, Revenue Operations, Product, Finance, Procurement and IT to drive efficiency, visibility and execution rigor.
