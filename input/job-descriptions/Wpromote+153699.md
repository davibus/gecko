The Role We're looking for an Associate Director, Influencer & Creator Marketing to provide hands-on strategic and operational leadership across a portfolio of client accounts. This role combines deep influencer and creator expertise with team leadership, quality control, and senior client counsel - ensuring programs are strategically sharp, operationally sound, and built to deliver measurable business impact. You'll be serving as the go-to resource for complex strategy, campaign planning, executional challenges, measurement, and executive-ready storytelling. As part of Wpromote 's Creative organization, you'll help advance an integrated approach to influencer and creator marketing - connecting organic influence, creator content, paid amplification, affiliate and commerce opportunities, experiential moments, analytics, and broader media strategy to drive full-funnel outcomes.
