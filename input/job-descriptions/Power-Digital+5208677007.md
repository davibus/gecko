A day in the life: As the Senior Marketing Operations Strategist, you provide comprehensive marketing operations support for our larger and more complex B2B and B2C Lead-Generation clients. You are responsible for managing the tools and processes that enable lead capture, nurture, and flow through the revenue funnel. This includes building the foundation for marketing operations success as well as optimizing and maintaining marketing operations excellence on an ongoing basis. You will also perform high-level sales operations tasks, including but not necessarily limited to building intermediate automations and dashboards for revenue teams, and building and advising on standard CRM-use processes for the sales team. You fully understand all standard system processes needed to produce factual and reliable reports. You will also provide senior support to other Marketing Operations Strategists on accounts, and be someone that team members can turn to with ad hoc strategy or troubleshooting assistance. You know how to act fast to solve problems and think of long-term processes and solutions. You know how to capitalize on our standard deliverables, or on the flip side, know how to pivot if a specific solution doesn't make sense for a client at that point in time. You have mastered presenting creative reporting to clients and team members through storytelling, and are constantly implementing learnings to enhance future processes and strategies.
