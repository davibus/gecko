We are seeking experienced Paid Search operators who can step into accounts immediately and drive measurable business impact. As a Paid Search Associate, you bring strong hands-on experience managing paid search campaigns focused on ecommerce and direct response performance. You are comfortable operating directly in platform, analyzing data, and implementing optimizations that improve revenue, efficiency, and scale. You take ownership of campaign performance and thrive in fast-paced environments where speed, accuracy, and analytical thinking are critical. You will analyze performance data, identify opportunities for optimization and expansion, and proactively implement changes to ensure campaigns consistently meet or exceed client KPIs. This role requires someone who is comfortable operating independently in platforms such as Google Ads and Microsoft Ads and has direct experience managing ecommerce campaigns with measurable revenue impact.
