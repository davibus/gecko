We are building that future today. **We support 100% remote, in-office, or hybrid work styles for anyone legally eligible to work in the U.S. A day in the life: You'll own a portfolio of 4–6 fashion and apparel accounts. These clients move fast — seasonal drops, trend-driven creative, influencer campaigns that need to be in market in days, not weeks. The growth constraints in fashion are usually one of three things: the brand is winning on product but losing on customer acquisition efficiency; they're acquiring customers but not retaining them; or their creative is outpacing their targeting strategy (or vice versa). Your job is to find which one it is, build the roadmap, and hold the team to it.
