## Job description

About Crimson Brand Partners Crimson Brand Partners is a newly formed partnership with the University of Utah designed to modernize how the University continues to build its brand. Crimson Brand Partners will manage commercial operations across Utah Athletics and the broader university – including events at stadiums and arenas, branding, licensing, sponsorships, ticketing, and digital media. Job Summary We is seeking a creative, high-energy Social Coordinator to help with day-to-day social media strategy and content posting across University of Utah Athletics platforms. This role’s primary responsibility is elevating the Utes brand through compelling storytelling, real-time engagement, and platform-optimized content that connects fans to programs, student-athletes, coaches, and the department as a whole. You will contribute to content ideation, production, and execution across all major social channels while collaborating closely with sport programs, athletics communications, digital media, marketing, and commercial teams. This is a hands-on position in a fast-paced, high-visibility college athletics environment that demands creativity, strong organizational skills, and the ability to thrive on game days and across a multi-sport calendar. The Social Coordinator will support assigned sports and department-wide initiatives, with flexibility to shift focus based on season, priority moments, and departmental needs. Key Responsibilities: Help develop and execute a comprehensive social media content strategy and calendar for assigned Utah Athletics accounts and department-wide channels across Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, YouTube, and emerging platforms. Create, edit, and publish high-quality content including short- and long-form video, photography, graphics, stories, and live content that highlights student-athletes, coaches, game action, culture, and behind-the-scenes moments. Design and produce engaging graphics that support social media, digital campaigns, and department-wide content needs. Manage pre-event, in-event, and post-event social coverage, ensuring timely, on-brand posts that maximize fan energy and program visibility. Drive real-time fan engagement, community management, and rapid response across platforms. Collaborate with coaching staff, athletics communications, digital production, marketing, ticketing, and commercial partners to align content with program goals and broader athletics initiatives. Support multiple Utah Athletics teams and department-wide digital efforts as priorities shift throughout the year. Track performance using analytics tools, report key metrics (engagement, reach, growth, conversions), and continuously optimize content and strategy. Stay ahead of social media trends, platform algorithm changes, emerging formats, and best practices in college and professional sports content. Maintain strict adherence to NCAA, Big 12, University of Utah, and brand guidelines, including NIL and copyright standards. Work flexible hours including nights, weekends, holidays, and occasional travel for games and events. Qualifications Required: 2+ years of professional experience in social media management, content creation, or digital marketing (college athletics, professional sports, or entertainment preferred). Demonstrated ability to create engaging social content and manage brand accounts with measurable growth in audience and engagement. Strong written communication skills with the ability to write in an authentic, platform-native voice. Proficiency in video editing, photo editing, and graphic design tools (Adobe Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Lightroom, CapCut, or similar). Deep understanding of major social platforms, their audiences, and content best practices. Excellent organizational skills, attention to detail, and ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, multi-sport environment. Ability and willingness to work non-traditional hours, including evenings, weekends, holidays, and travel as required by the athletics calendar. Passion for college athletics and the University of Utah / Utah Utes brand. Preferred: Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Journalism, Digital Media, Sports Management, or a related field. Direct experience managing social media for a college athletics program or professional sports team. Hands-on experience producing live event content and rapid-turnaround social assets. Knowledge of current trends in sports content, short-form video, and fan engagement strategies. Experience collaborating with coaches, sport administrators, and athletic department staff. Benefits and Perks Crimson Brand Partners offers a comprehensive benefits package that includes excellent medical, dental, and vision coverage; a 401(k) retirement plan with a generous 4% employer match; standard PTO (10 days of paid vacation annually); five paid sick days; 11 paid holidays; voluntary life, AD&D, and short- and long-term disability coverage; an Employee Assistance Program (EAP); and a variety of voluntary supplemental benefits, including accident, critical illness, hospital indemnity, and legal coverage.
