Actively identify, test, and scale innovative approaches, automation opportunities, and emerging technologies (including AI applications) within paid search across your accounts to maintain a competitive edge Be a collaborative partner between your peers in client partnership, creative, growth and activation teams to ensure proper execution of strategy and client stewardship Be a storyteller: turning complex search strategies into compelling campaign recommendations Maintain best practices on your accounts&nbsp;
