## Full job description

This is a hybrid role requiring three days per week at the Cottonwood Heights office in Salt Lake City.

Extra Space Storage is seeking a Digital Advertising Analyst to execute paid-search tactics. The analyst will work in a fast-paced environment supporting paid-search execution for more than 4,000 facilities across the country, completing independent work and collaborating with the broader team.

## Duties and Responsibilities

- Build campaigns, ad groups, and keyword structures based on senior analyst and manager direction.
- Add paid-search campaigns for new stores each week.
- Execute ad-copy and bidding tests designed by senior analysts and report results.
- Monitor paid-search account performance for health and efficiency.
- Manage creative uploads for paid search and paid media, including Google Display Network, Demand Gen, and connected TV campaigns.
- Track daily and weekly campaign-spend pacing and flag anomalies.
- Support lead-generation partners through data pulls and performance tracking.
- Process and reconcile vendor invoices for accuracy and timely submission.
- Analyze keywords and search queries to improve targeting and performance.
- Report regularly on KPIs, competitor metrics, and campaign performance.

## Qualifications

- One to three years of paid-search advertising management experience.
- Bachelor's degree preferred but not required.

## Competencies

- Knowledge of paid-search platforms, KPIs, industry trends, PPC account structure, and optimization strategies.
- Comfort using Google Ads and Excel to analyze large datasets for insights and opportunities.
- Ability to collaborate cross-functionally and communicate findings clearly.
- Expertise with Google Ads and Bing Ads web interfaces and desktop editors.
- Advanced Excel skills, including VLOOKUP, SUMIFS, pivot tables, and data analysis.
- Attention to detail, communication, and critical-thinking ability.
- Initiative and ability to work independently.
- Process-improvement orientation.
- Ability to contribute to vendor partnerships and project success.
- Adaptability in a fast-paced digital-advertising environment.
