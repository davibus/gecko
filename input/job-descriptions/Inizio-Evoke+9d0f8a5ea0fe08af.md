## Job description

We are looking for an experienced and dynamic Paid Search Supervisor to lead our search engine marketing (SEM) programs for the pharmaceutical sector. You will be an expert on the evolving search landscape, with a focus on the impact of AI and generative search. You will establish best practices for SEM tailored to compliance within the pharmaceutical industry. It will also involve authoring points of view and white papers that position Inizio Evoke as an voice on paid search. Additionally, you will use new technologies to optimize our search campaigns. The Paid Search Supervisor will collaborate with a talented team of media subject matter experts. This team includes specialists in programmatic advertising, ad operations, social media, and SEO. You Will: Best Practices Development Establish and implement SEM best practices across the organization Review and refine SEM strategies to align with our goals, maximizing ROI on search campaigns Create QA process to ensure all campaigns meet organizational standards and align with new best practices Author points of view, white papers, and internal briefings that establish the agency's perspective on the future of paid search and how AI is reshaping search behavior in pharmaceutical marketing Trend Analysis and Adaptation Monitor industry trends, new technologies, and competitive landscape related to SEM I dentify opportunities for the use of tools and techniques that enhance search performance Lead the agency's perspective on how AI, generative search engines, and AI-driven answer experiences (e.g., Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT) are reshaping paid search strategy in a regulated industry Evaluate emerging AI-powered bidding, creative, and audience tools and recommend where to pilot, scale, or hold based on performance and compliance fit Campaign Management Support the planning, execution, and optimization of paid search campaigns across multiple platforms (e.g., Google Ads, Bing Ads) to achieve targeted KPIs Conduct regular campaign analysis and use data-driven insights to inform decisions regarding bidding strategies, keyword selection (including relevant therapeutic areas), ad copy, and audience targeting Troubleshoot live campaign performance issues and investigate reporting discrepancies across platforms, ad servers, and analytics tools, identifying root causes and implementing fixes to keep campaigns delivering accurately Team Collaboration Collaborate with the Center of Excellence team to implement integrated marketing strategies that drive traffic, engagement, and conversions Provide guidance internally to brand team members, assisting in their daily campaign management. Externally, act as a technical voice to clients for SEM, providing recommendations and campaign updates.
