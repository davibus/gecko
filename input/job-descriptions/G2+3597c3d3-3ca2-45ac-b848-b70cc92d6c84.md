About the Role We’re looking for a Senior Specialist of Marketing Analytics & Planning to support the strategic and day-to-day oversight and analysis of our marketing investments through budget planning, monthly forecasting, and performance insights across channels for our buyer marketing team. This role connects marketing & financial data and uses its analytical capabilities to help teams understand what’s driving results, spot risks and opportunities, set expectations, and propose actions that can directly impact business performance. You’ll be closely assisting across the full marketing funnel from spend through gross profit across paid search, paid social, paid display, and email. You’ll manage monthly, annual, and intra-month projections, helping the team understand expected revenue and performance and how results may change as conditions shift. The role works across Performance Marketing, Email Marketing, Data Science, Analytics, Product, and Finance. You’ll partner closely with the buyer marketing team on sizing scenarios, estimates, financial models, and Monthly Business Reviews (MBRs), as well as other leadership reporting. In This Role, You Will Lead budget planning and forecasting across marketing channels, from spend through gross profit. Build and maintain monthly, annual, and intra-month projections, including expected revenue and other key business outcomes. Lead weekly performance meetings, presenting business and channel results and highlighting the most important risks, opportunities, and recommended actions. Support day-to-day channel performance management. Analyze results, identify performance drivers, and surface risks, opportunities, and changes in expectations. Build actionable analyses of performance trends, including channel, category, and country opportunities and sources of growth or decline. Partner with the VP of Buyer Growth on sizing scenarios, estimates, financial models, MBRs, and leadership reporting. Partner with Analytics, Data Science and Finance to build accurate forecasting & bidding models that enable scenario planning and accurate tracking into the marketing P&L. Use AI tools to build and improve reporting, tracking, dashboards, and models - for example, dashboards or forecasting models in Claude or Google Sheets - that help teams understand performance and take action. Improve marketing reporting models and work with Analytics to define requirements for dashboards and other reporting tools. Support testing and experimentation by helping teams plan learning agendas, evaluate results, and incorporate findings into forecasts and performance tracking. Develop clear performance read-outs and presentations that help leaders make informed decisions about marketing investment and business goals.
