## Job description

Istari Digital is a high-growth technology company enabling the next generation of AI-native engineering across aerospace, defense, and industrial enterprises. Our infrastructure platform orchestrates data across tools, organizations, and classification levels, removing barriers that limit digital engineering, and empowers customers to modernize mission-critical workflows and unlock meaningful ROI from digital transformation at scale. As demand accelerates across commercial aerospace & defense, we are expanding our marketing organization to support execution of integrated, multi-channel demand generation programs, including the content that fuels them. Position Overview The Digital Marketing & Content Specialist leads content creation and digital campaign execution across Istari's marketing channels. This role partners closely with the Marketing Lead to translate messaging and campaign strategy into the content and campaigns that drive demand: emails, landing pages, digital ads, webinars, social media video clips. You will write and produce campaign content, execute email, paid, social, and web campaigns end to end, and report on performance to inform what gets built next. You'll also turn raw material like event footage, employee and customer interviews, or podcast recordings into publish-ready content. Your work should ultimately be measurable against top-of-funnel pipeline impact. The Marketing Lead owns marketing strategy, budget, and messaging across the business. The Field Marketing Manager owns field and in-person execution. The Digital Marketing & Content Specialist owns content creation and digital campaign execution, ensuring every email, ad, and landing page reflects Istari's brand and drives measurable results. Together, these roles translate company strategy into consistent, coordinated go-to-market execution across every channel prospects encounter.
