About the job Twilio.org is seeking a Sr. Marketing Strategy and Analytics Manager to serve as the dedicated operational business partner to our Social Impact marketing leadership. Sitting at the intersection of Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy, marketing operations, and advanced data modeling, you won't just report on what happened, you will design the automation, attribution, and account prioritization engines that drive what happens next. This is an ideal opportunity for: The Analytics Strategist ready to drive decisions: You have deep technical data chops (SQL, Python, modeling) but want to move beyond reporting to directly author investment proposals, influence leadership, and see your analyses translate into decisions being made. The Specialized Marketer ready for full-lifecycle ownership: You have deep subject-matter expertise in one domain (such as demand generation, marketing ops, or lifecycle marketing) and are ready to expand your runway to own the entire marketing engine, from top-of-funnel triggers and budget allocation to field event ROI and executive scorecards. Responsibilities In this role, you’ll: Act as Strategic GTM Business Partner: Drive regular operating rhythms, quarterly pipeline generation (PG) OKR targets, QBR data packs, and marketing budget allocation frameworks alongside marketing leadership. Lead Investment Proposals & Impact Modeling: Architect rigorous business cases, scenario models, and ROI forecasts to secure investment for new marketing programs; evaluate capital trade-offs across channels and stress-test the expected pipeline return and payback periods before launch. Lead Pipeline Automation & Marketing Triggers: Design, script, and operationalize automated marketing triggers in Python/SQL notebooks to power high-converting marketing plays and feed targeted accounts directly into sales workflows. Architect Attribution & Single Source of Truth: Own end-to-end pipeline attribution models across sales, marketing, and partner channels; maintain rep-level marketing metrics integrated directly into Frontline Manager (FLM) data packs and leadership scorecards. Drive Account Tiering & Routing: Operationalize advanced account tiering and segmentation logic across Salesforce (SFDC) and 6sense/Segment to optimize lead routing, demand generation campaigns, and Account-Based Marketing (ABM) outreach. Advanced Commercial Modeling: Partner with broader central data science teams on company wide marketing frameworks (like Marketing Mix Modeling), while independently owning custom scenario analysis (e.g., ASP impact of bundling, Customer Lifetime Value/LFTV cohorting, and field event ROI modeling). Maintain & Modernize GTM Data Infrastructure: Curate and optimize our Looker, Tableau, and SFDC reporting assets, managing SQL logic via GitHub version control, dashboard glossaries, and automated data QA processes.
