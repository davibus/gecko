## About the Company & Role
Omni Advertising is a premier full-service automotive advertising agency representing top-tier automotive dealership groups across the country. The SEM Specialist I will support paid search marketing initiatives across a large portfolio of automotive dealership clients, optimizing search visibility, local lead generation, and multi-tier vehicle sales campaigns.

## Key Responsibilities
- **Campaign Execution & Management:** Assist in building, managing, and optimizing Google Ads campaigns (Search, Display, Video, and Vehicle Listing Ads / VLAs) across multiple dealership accounts.
- **Conversion & Lead Tracking:** Monitor campaign performance, tracking conversions including digital form fills, Click-to-Call/phone inquiries, chats, and dealership appointment requests.
- **Keyword & Market Research:** Conduct granular keyword research, competitor analysis, negative keyword pruning, and search term analysis focused on vehicle models, lease specials, financing offers, and automotive service specials.
- **Promotions & Ad Copy Optimization:** Support monthly OEM and dealer specials with timely ad copy refreshes, extensions, and campaign pacing adjustments.
- **Landing Page Alignment:** Collaborate with dealer marketing teams to align messaging, ad extensions, and dedicated landing pages with real-time local vehicle inventory and promotional offers.
- **Reporting & Performance Insights:** Generate monthly/weekly performance reports and actionable insights using Google Ads, Google Analytics (GA4), Google Tag Manager (GTM), CRM feeds, and Looker Studio/spreadsheets.
- **Account Audits & Budget Governance:** Conduct regular account audits, budget tracking, quality score improvements, and proactive bid/pacing optimization reviews.

## Qualifications & Requirements
- 2–3+ years of hands-on digital marketing and SEM experience (automotive advertising or multi-location agency experience strongly valued).
- Demonstrated track record of building, restructuring, and optimizing Google Ads accounts.
- Deep expertise with Google Ads, Google Analytics (GA4), Google Tag Manager (GTM), and spreadsheet data analysis (Excel / Google Sheets).
- Strong understanding of local lead generation, conversion tracking (calls, forms, chats), and conversion rate optimization (CRO).
- Excellent attention to detail, communication skills, and analytical problem-solving mindset.
