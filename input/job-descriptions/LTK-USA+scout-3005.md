<p><strong>About the role</strong></p>
<p>The Paid Media Specialist will be responsible for assisting in the&nbsp; execution of boosting Creator posts on Instagram and TikTok, from start to finish, including but not limited to campaign set-up, targeting parameters, implementation of UTM tracking, reporting on campaign performance, etc, for brands in verticals such as Fashion, Beauty, Big-Box retailers, retail media networks, etc.&nbsp;</p>
<p>The ideal candidate will have 3+ years of digital media experience, specifically paid social, including but not limited to META, and TIKTOK. This candidate will manage and coordinate budgets, planning calendars,&nbsp; assets, materials and approvals.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Role requires marketing knowledge with creativity and passion to deliver bar-raising, best-in-class digital marketing.</p>
<p>The ideal candidate will have experience implementing and managing paid social campaigns, working closely with creator content, and influencer marketing campaigns, collaboration with sales departments, and&nbsp; analyzing results and performance.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>How you will make an impact</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Develop, strategize, and execute creator boosting campaigns against targeted audiences from ideation to post campaign analysis</li>
<li>Facilitate teams campaign operations and workflow deadlines, deliverables and processes to ensure successful and on-time campaign execution</li>
<li>Support the Marketing team by monitoring KPIs (such as website traffic, top-of-funnel conversions, ads performance, and revenue) and informing on findings</li>
<li>Perform analysis of key business metrics to evaluate success and growth opportunities</li>
<li>Coordinate boosting efforts with Campaign Operations team</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Key Measures:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Effectively manage Media Boosting on META and TIKTOK driving significant incremental traffic through boosted posts that beat industry standard media buy click through rates.</li>
<li>Drive incremental revenue by supporting sales in growing existing client relationships, revenue streams and total revenue.</li>
<li>Achievement of marketing implementation and success metrics</li>
<li>Disciplined campaign development and execution</li>
<li>Contributing to a robust and scalable funnel that grows relationships and revenue streams and overall revenue from existing clients</li>
<li>Able to report on performance, with understanding of multi-touch attribution modeling and experience sharing insights across teams.</li>
</ul>
