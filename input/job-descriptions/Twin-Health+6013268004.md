This role sits at the intersection of marketing strategy, data, and technical execution. You will turn raw campaign and behavioral data into clear analysis that drives decisions at every level, from channel owners to the executive team. Right now, the first priority is to understand performance before we optimize it. In your first year you will establish trusted, consistent performance baselines across channels, find where the data does not add up, and tell us what is working and what is not. As that foundation matures, the role grows into attribution, marketing mix, and scoring models that guide budget allocation and targeting. Our data foundation is built by our data engineering team. You will be its most demanding user. You will query across sources to answer marketing questions, find the gaps and inconsistencies others miss, and tell the data team what needs to be tracked, modeled, or fixed so the next question is easier to answer. This role is for someone whose edge is judgment. You know what to ask before you write the query, you notice when an answer does not add up, and you give the marketing team a recommendation they can act on. It is a hands-on, high-impact individual contributor role for someone who works independently in a fast-moving environment and is equally comfortable digging through a warehouse and presenting to senior leaders.
