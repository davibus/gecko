About the Role Rula is fundamentally transforming mental healthcare access by seamlessly bridging the gap between quality care and insurance coverage. As Director of Organic Growth, you will steward the primary gateway to our ecosystem: our organic channels. Reporting to the Head of Brand & Integrated Marketing and serving as a key member of the Brand Leadership Team, you will architect the vision, multi-year roadmap, and bottom-line revenue impact across organic search and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) as a cohesive growth engine. We are navigating a generational paradigm shift from legacy SEO to AI-mediated discovery—including LLMs, AI Overviews, and conversational search environments. You will define Rula’s market position in this new era, translating category-defining strategy into high-ROI Go-To-Market execution, business cases, and scalable growth across both consumer (patient) and provider segments. Strategic Scope & Execution Unlocking our next phase of growth demands blending technical domain authority with a relentless experimentation culture. You and your team will steer full-funnel organic strategy—driving site architecture innovation, enterprise-grade structured data, and dominant share-of-voice in zero-click environments. Because organic growth at this level relies heavily on platform performance, you will act as a peer and strategic co-owner alongside Engineering, Product, and Design (EPD) leaders to identify technical investments that improve discoverability, site performance, and conversion, influencing shared roadmap priorities while respecting clear ownership boundaries.. You will also orchestrate alignment across PR, Lifecycle, Clinical, and Compliance teams—ensuring all growth mechanisms are technically robust, clinically authoritative, and fully compliant with HIPAA, WCAG, and healthcare regulatory standards. Leadership & Commercial Accountability As an enterprise growth leader, you will build, mentor, and scale a high-performing organic growth organization alongside an elite agency network, recruiting, developing, and retaining exceptional talent while fostering a culture of experimentation, accountability, and continuous learning. You hold direct P&L accountability for growth forecasting, capital allocation, and ROI—rigorously tracking metrics from AI answer-engine market share down to CAC, LTV, and net revenue contribution. Ultimately, you will distill complex performance data into compelling executive narratives that shape C-suite decision-making and influence company-wide investments in content, product, and brand.
