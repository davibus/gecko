About the Role: We are seeking a Senior Product Marketing Manager, Risk Advisory to join our Product Marketing team, reporting to the Senior Director of Product Marketing. This role is the connective tissue between Product and our go to market teams (sales, customer success, and marketing), translating our product roadmap and release cadence into positioning, messaging, and enablement that reaches the right internal and external audience at the right time. You'll need to be comfortable operating in an environment where features ship often and clear communication matters. Success here means proactively keeping GTM teams aligned with that pace, while showing a high degree of autonomy and ownership. Understanding the risk advisory landscape and how advisory firms operate is a key part of this role. Candidates with hands-on risk advisory experience (internal audit, SOC, compliance, or similar) are encouraged to apply, even without a traditional product marketing background.
