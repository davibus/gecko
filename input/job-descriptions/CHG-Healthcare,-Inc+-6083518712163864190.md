## Job description

Healthcare’s helping hand. CHG shook things up in 1979 by inventing the locum tenens staffing model. We connect doctors with patients who need their care. As the largest physician staffing firm in America, our providers treat millions of patients each year. Our industry is growing and demand is high. This means you’ll have plenty of opportunities to grow and develop in your career. Keeping healthcare healthy can be as fun as it is rewarding At CHG Healthcare, we connect physicians and advanced practice providers with the opportunities that fit their lives. Our marketing team plays a direct role in making that happen, and we're looking for a Sr. Growth Marketing Manager to help us do it better. A Division of CHG Healthcare is looking for a Growth Marketing Manager to join our Marketing and Customer Products team. This team drives provider awareness, engagement, and conversion across all channels, connecting the right physicians and advanced practice providers to the right opportunities at the right time. As a Growth Marketing Manager, you will own the development and execution of integrated marketing campaigns end-to-end, identify growth opportunities across the full funnel, and coordinate across channel teams to drive measurable business results. This role will report to the Sr. Marketing Director.
