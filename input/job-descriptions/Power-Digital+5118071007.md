A day in the life: You'll own a portfolio of 4-6 CPG accounts, primarily beauty, personal care, wellness, or food and beverage brands. This role centers on a comprehensive diagnosis of core business constraints impacting client growth, leveraging a deep understanding of the competitive landscape to identify and formulate strategic solutions. This includes pinpointing challenges across all points of sale - DTC/ecommerce acquisition, retail execution, Amazon market share erosion, or upstream strategic barriers like brand positioning - and developing a unified, data-backed strategy to overcome them. CPG accounts tend to have opinions about their channels that predate the data. Part of this job is having the product, competitive, and retail fluency to challenge those assumptions with credibility. Responsibilities: Employ AI technologies to enhance and optimize business processes Utilize and leverage Power Digital's Nova ecosystem as it relates to your department Run quarterly Power Circuit reviews — diagnose growth constraints across Business, Audience, Product, Messaging, Channels, and Measurement, with particular attention to how the client's retail and DTC performance interact Own the senior client relationship QBRs should drive decisions, not report history Consult on channel mix across DTC, Amazon, and retail — understand how they cannibalize or reinforce each other and make a recommendation Coordinate paid, SEO, content, influencer, and creative teams around a single account strategy Manage retention and expansion; lead renewals; grow MRR in your portfolio Build multi-threaded relationships with multiple layers of client contacts so no single point of contact departure puts an account at risk Ensure daily use of our proprietary technology (Iris and nova), Pulse (client performance and sentiment tool) cadence at 100%, and Power Circuit (our proprietary diagnostic system) compliance across managed ADs Manage and develop 1–2 direct reports — run regular 1:1s, build development plans, and own their performance reviews Coach analysts and specialists;
