## Job description (tracker description/listing field)

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Cosmetics/Ecommerce – Digital Marketing Specialist based in the United States.

The Digital Marketing Specialist will drive brand awareness, customer engagement, and e-commerce growth for an innovative skincare brand focused on microbiome science and skin health. This role combines digital marketing strategy, creative content development, performance advertising, and marketplace management. You will plan and execute campaigns across channels such as Amazon, Google Ads, search engines, and social media to strengthen brand visibility and increase sales. Working collaboratively with the wider team, you will shape brand messaging, develop influencer marketing initiatives, and create compelling content for a global audience. You will also use analytics to measure campaign performance, identify opportunities, and optimize marketing activities. This is an opportunity to contribute to a growing beauty brand as it expands its retail presence and reaches new customers.

As a Digital Marketing Specialist, you will manage and execute digital marketing initiatives across multiple channels, combining creative planning with data-driven optimization to support brand growth and e-commerce performance.

Develop and execute digital marketing strategies aligned with brand objectives and growth priorities.

Plan, launch, and manage advertising campaigns, promotional activities, and digital marketing initiatives.

- 

Plan and create social media content in collaboration with the team.

- 

Coordinate influencer marketing content and initiatives to strengthen brand awareness and audience engagement.

-

-

Operate and manage social media accounts and online advertising channels.

-

- 

Contribute to global marketing content and ensure consistent brand communication across digital touchpoints.
