About the Role

Our Sr. Account Managers, Paid Search are responsible for driving KPI improvements and developing cutting-edge strategies tailored to client goals. Your core responsibilities in this role include strategy development, tactical execution, and management of client relationships.

  • Taking on clients in month 1, you will build & optimize Google and Bing search campaigns for a roster of B2B, lead generation, and/or ecommerce clients across various industries
    • Client assignments will be a mix of leading paid search efforts on small-spending clients and acting as a supporting paid search team member on medium to enterprise clients
  • Define, monitor and analyze trends in clients’ KPIs
  • Develop both short & long term paid search strategy based on client-specific goals
  • Actively test and analyze keywords, advertising copy, landing pages, campaign types and positioning to drive results in line with client's goals
  • Communicate performance results and strategy recommendations to clients clearly & secure buy-in from decision-makers
    • Create slides with good design & strong storytelling for clients
  • Create quarterly business reviews & other client-facing presentations that outline performance, insights from testing, and key strategic opportunities
  • Delegate work to junior team members & provide clear direction & feedback when needed
  • Stay current on SEM industry trends, contribute to internal agency conversations around SEM (including but not limited to monthly department meetings, trainings, case studies, etc.)

About You

The essentials:

  • 2+ years of direct paid search client management including paid search strategy development, execution & client relationship management
  • Passion for crunching numbers, analyzing data, and navigating Excel/Google sheet spreadsheets
  • Possess high attention to detail and be able to execute with a high degree of accuracy
  • Performance Max experience is a must
  • Hands-on product feed management and shopping experience a plus
  • You are a communication guru both on calls and through written communication
  • ‘Driven’ and ‘Innovative’ are attributes commonly associated with you
  • You are proactive & willing to raise your hand to support your team members
  • Highly collaborative & works well with a team
  • 4-year college degree or equivalent work experience

About Monks
