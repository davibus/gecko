# AI resume review notes

This is a one-time review sample. The resume uses only the v2 master; the additions below are requests for information, not asserted accomplishments. Production resumes retain their two-page default.

## 1 Product Content and Catalog Automation — R1 Industries

Used ChatGPT within Google Sheets to rewrite Shopify product descriptions for SEO and remove duplicate content. Combined the content workflow with GraphQL inventory retrieval, Excel data preparation, and bulk Shopify uploads to replace manual catalog entry.

**What to add to the master:** Project dates; number of products and suppliers; your role in building the workflow; how descriptions were reviewed; time per product before and after; any measured search or conversion results.

## 2 AI Assisted Website Development — Utah MMC

Used Cursor and Claude to create SEO-friendly websites across an eight-company group. Configured Google Ads, GA4, and Google Search Console, and developed a ServiceTitan CRM program, bringing website delivery, marketing measurement, and customer-management systems into the same project scope.

**What to add to the master:** Dates and live site links; what you designed or coded; what Cursor/Claude did; website stack; number of sites launched; deployment timeline; measurable leads, traffic, or conversion changes.

## 3 AI Assisted Headless Commerce Development — Vortex

Developed a headless React and Next.js e-commerce website with assistance from Cursor, Codex, Claude, and Gemini. Connected Shopify to manufacturer databases and automated order and shipping updates through GraphQL integrations. Designed the operational system for roughly 10 minutes of weekly oversight once running.

**What to add to the master:** Dates; catalog size and number of manufacturer integrations; whether the ten-minute figure is a measured average or design target; previous weekly maintenance time; order volume; error reduction; links or screenshots.

## 4 Construction Site Prospecting System — Utah MMC

Developed a construction-site prospecting system with Cursor and Claude, using government satellite imagery to identify work sites, equipment in use, and potential service or equipment-rental opportunities. Applied the observations to determine which services and rental equipment the company could offer.

**What to add to the master:** Imagery provider and access method; geography and number of sites reviewed; exactly what was automated; whether image interpretation was manual or model-based; any model/API used; qualified leads, meetings, rentals, or revenue. The master does not establish computer-vision model development.

## 5 AI Assisted Promotion and Performance Analysis — 1-800 Contacts

Analyzed AI-assisted promotion performance using Namogoo alongside paid-media bidding, audience, attribution, incremental CPA, and customer lifetime-value analyses. Evaluated these areas as part of a broader marketing-analysis program that included Performance Max and Google Shopping performance, affiliate commissions, and predictive projections.

**What to add to the master:** Your exact role and dates; the Namogoo capability evaluated; test design and comparison period; campaign/sample size; recommendations implemented; measured incremental revenue, CPA, conversion, or margin impact.

## Before fitting this into two pages

After choosing a target job, retain the two or three AI projects most relevant to it, tighten repeated accomplishments, and keep the strongest quantified results. First review this longer sample; no permanent section or page-count changes have been made.
